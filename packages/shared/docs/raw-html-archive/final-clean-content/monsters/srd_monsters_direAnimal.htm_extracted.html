Dire Animal

Dire animals are larger, tougher, meaner versions of ordinary animals. Each kind tends to have a feral, prehistoric, or even demonic appearance.

Common dire animals include:

  • Dire Ape
  • Dire Badger
  • Dire Bat
  • Dire Bear
  • Dire Boar
  • Dire Lion
  • Dire Rat
  • Dire Shark
  • Dire Tiger
  • Dire Weasel
  • Dire Wolf
  • Dire Wolverine
