# D&D 3.x Quick Reference

## ⚡ **Combat Actions**

### **Action Types**
| **Action** | **Description** | **Movement Allowed** |
|------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Standard** | Attack, cast most spells | Yes (+ move action) |
| **Move** | Move up to speed, draw weapon | Yes |
| **Full-Round** | Full attack, charge, run | 5-ft step only |
| **Free** | Speak, drop item, simple actions | Yes |
| **Swift** | Quickened spell, some feats | Yes |
| **Immediate** | Interrupt, respond to trigger | Yes |

### **Attack Modifiers**
| **Situation** | **Attack Roll** | **Damage** | **Notes** |
|---------------|-----------------|------------|-----------|
| **Flanking** | +2 | — | Opposite sides of enemy |
| **Charge** | +2 | — | -2 AC until next turn |
| **Fighting Defensively** | -4 | — | +2 AC |
| **Power Attack** | -1 to -5 | +1 to +5 (2H: +2 to +10) | Choose before rolling |
| **Two-Weapon Fighting** | -6/-10 | — | Light off-hand: -4/-8 |
| **Sneak Attack** | — | +Xd6 | Flanking or flat-footed |

## 🛡️ **Armor Class Modifiers**

### **AC Components**
```
AC = 10 + Armor + Shield + Dex + Size + Natural + Deflection + Misc
```

### **Situational AC**
| **Situation** | **AC Modifier** | **Touch AC** | **Flat-Footed AC** |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------|---------------------|
| **Fighting Defensively** | +2 | +2 | +2 |
| **Total Defense** | +4 | +4 | +4 |
| **Charging** | -2 | -2 | -2 |
| **Prone (vs Melee)** | +4 | +4 | +4 |
| **Prone (vs Ranged)** | -4 | -4 | -4 |
| **Cover (Partial)** | +4 | +4 | +4 |
| **Cover (Total)** | — | — | Cannot be targeted |

## 🎯 **Cover & Concealment**

### **Cover**
| **Type** | **AC Bonus** | **Reflex Save** | **Description** |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Partial** | +4 | +2 | 25% to 50% covered |
| **Improved** | +8 | +4 | 75% covered |
| **Total** | — | — | 100% covered, no line of effect |

### **Concealment**
| **Type** | **Miss Chance** | **Description** |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Partial** | 20% | Dim light, light fog |
| **Total** | 50% | Darkness, heavy fog, invisible |

## 😵 **Common Conditions**

### **Movement Conditions**
| **Condition** | **Speed** | **Actions** | **AC** | **Attacks** |
|---------------|-----------|-------------|--------|-------------|
| **Prone** | Crawl 5 ft | Stand provokes AoO | +4 melee, -4 ranged | -4 |
| **Grappling** | 0 | Limited | +0 | -4 |
| **Entangled** | Half | — | -2 | -2 |
| **Paralyzed** | 0 | None | — | Helpless |

### **Sensory Conditions**
| **Condition** | **Attack** | **AC** | **Special** |
|---------------|------------|--------|-------------|
| **Blinded** | -2, 50% miss | -2 | Half speed, lose Dex to AC |
| **Deafened** | — | — | 20% spell failure (verbal) |
| **Invisible** | +2 | +2 | 50% miss vs you, total concealment |

### **Fear Conditions**
| **Condition** | **Effect** | **Duration** |
|---------------|------------|--------------|
| **Shaken** | -2 attacks, saves, checks | Varies |
| **Frightened** | As shaken + must flee | Varies |
| **Panicked** | As frightened + drop items, -2 saves | Varies |

## ✨ **Spellcasting**

### **Casting in Combat**
| **Action** | **Provokes AoO** | **Concentration** |
|------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Cast Spell** | Yes | DC 15 + damage |
| **Defensive Casting** | No | DC 15 + spell level |
| **Spell-Like Ability** | Yes | DC 10 + damage |

### **Concentration DCs**
| **Situation** | **DC** |
|---------------|--------|
| **Taking damage** | 10 + damage dealt + spell level |
| **Continuous damage** | 10 + 1/2 damage + spell level |
| **Affected by spell** | Spell save DC + spell level |
| **Grappling** | 10 + grappler CMB + spell level |
| **Weather** | 5 to 10 + spell level |

### **Weapon Properties**
