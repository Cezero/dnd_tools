# Health and Death

This section covers hit points, injuries, and death mechanics.

## Injury and Recovery

- **[Injury and Death](injury-death.md)** — Hit point loss, dying, death, recovery, and healing

## Quick Reference

### Hit Point Status

| **HP Range** | **Status** | **Effects** |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Full HP** | Healthy | No penalties |
| **1+ HP** | Wounded | No mechanical penalty |
| **0 HP** | Disabled | Can take 1 move OR standard action |
| **-1 to -9 HP** | Dying | Unconscious, losing 1 HP/round |
| **-10 HP or lower** | Dead | Character dies |

### Disabled (0 HP)

When at **0 hit points**, you are **disabled**:
- You can take **1 move action OR 1 standard action** per turn (not both)
- Taking a **standard action** or any **strenuous activity** drops you to **-1 HP** (dying)
- **Healing** brings you back to positive HP normally

### Dying (-1 to -9 HP)

When at **negative hit points** (but above -10), you are **dying**:
- You are **unconscious** and **helpless**
- You **lose 1 hit point per round** until you die or stabilize
- **DC 10 Constitution check** each round to become stable
- If you reach **-10 HP**, you die

### Stabilizing

A **stable** character:
- No longer loses hit points each round
- Remains unconscious at negative HP
- **Heals naturally** at 1 HP per day
- Wakes up when reaching **1 hit point**

#### Ways to Stabilize
- **Natural**: DC 10 Constitution check when dying
- **First Aid**: DC 15 Heal check by another character
- **Magical Healing**: Any amount brings to positive HP

### Massive Damage

When you take **50+ points of damage** from a single attack:
- Make a **DC 15 Fortitude save**
- **Failure**: Die immediately regardless of remaining HP
- **Success**: Take damage normally

### Nonlethal Damage

**Nonlethal damage** represents:
- Bruising, fatigue, temporary pain
- Tracked separately from lethal damage
- Doesn't cause death (only unconsciousness)

#### Effects
- When **nonlethal damage equals current HP**: fall unconscious
- When **nonlethal damage exceeds current HP**: unconscious and dying
- **Heals** at 1 point per hour

### Temporary Hit Points

**Temporary hit points**:
- Are **lost first** when taking damage
- **Don't stack** with each other (keep highest)
- **Can exceed** normal maximum HP
- **Don't count** for healing or death calculations
