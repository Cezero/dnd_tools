# Spells and Magic in Combat

This section covers spellcasting and magic item use during combat.

## Spellcasting

- **[Casting Spells in Combat](casting-in-combat.md)** — Complete spellcasting rules, components, concentration

## Quick Reference

### Spellcasting Basics

| **Casting Time** | **Action Type** | **When Effect Occurs** |
|------------------|-----------------|------------------------|
| **1 standard action** | Standard | Immediately |
| **1 round** | Full-round | Start of next turn |
| **1 minute** | 10 full-round actions | After 1 minute |

### Spell Components

| **Component** | **Requirement** | **Restriction** |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Verbal (V)** | Speak in firm voice | Can't cast if gagged/silenced |
| **Somatic (S)** | Free hand gestures | Can't cast if bound/grappled |
| **Material (M)** | Spell components | Need component pouch |
| **Focus (F)** | Specific items | Need focus in hand |
| **Divine Focus (DF)** | Holy symbol | Must present symbol |

### Attacks of Opportunity

**Spellcasting provokes AoO** unless:
- Spell requires only a **free action** to cast
- Caster uses **Casting on the Defensive** (Concentration DC 15 + spell level)

### Concentration Checks

| **Distraction** | **DC** |
|-----------------|--------|
| **Damaged while casting** | 10 + damage + spell level |
| **Continuous damage** | 10 + ½ damage + spell level |
| **Affected by spell** | DC of affecting spell + spell level |
| **Grappling/pinned** | 20 + spell level |
| **Vigorous motion** | 10 + spell level |
| **Violent motion** | 15 + spell level |
| **Extremely violent motion** | 20 + spell level |
| **Wind** | 5 to 20 + spell level |
| **Casting defensively** | 15 + spell level |
| **Entangled** | 15 + spell level |

### Touch Spells

#### Touch Attack AC
**Touch AC = 10 + Dex modifier + size modifier + deflection bonus**

**Does NOT include**:
- Armor bonus
- Shield bonus  
- Natural armor bonus

#### Holding the Charge
- Can hold touch spell charge **indefinitely**
- Touch **anyone or anything** discharges spell
- **Casting another spell** discharges held touch spell
- Can make **unarmed attacks** while holding charge

### Magic Items

| **Item Type** | **Activation** | **Action Type** | **AoO?** |
|---------------|----------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Spell completion** (scrolls) | Read | Standard | Yes |
| **Spell trigger** (wands) | Command word | Standard | No |
| **Command word** | Speak | Standard | No |
| **Use-activated** | Use | Varies | No |
| **Potions** | Drink | Standard | Yes |

### Spell-Like Abilities

**Spell-like abilities**:
- **Casting time**: 1 standard action (unless noted)
- **Provoke AoO**: Yes (unless noted)
- **Require concentration**: Yes
- **Can be disrupted**: Yes
- **Can cast defensively**: Yes (DC 15 + spell level)
