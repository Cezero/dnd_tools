## Quick Reference

### Movement Speed by Race & Armor

| **Race** | **No/Light Armor** | **Medium/Heavy Armor** |
|----------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Human, Elf, Half-elf, Half-orc | 30 ft (6 squares) | 20 ft (4 squares) |
| Dwarf | 20 ft (4 squares) | 20 ft (4 squares) |
| Halfling, Gnome | 20 ft (4 squares) | 15 ft (3 squares) |

### Movement in Combat

| **Activity** | **Distance** | **Notes** |
|--------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Move action** | Speed | Can combine with standard action |
| **Double move** | 2× Speed | Uses both move and standard action |
| **Run** | 4× Speed | Full-round action (3× in heavy armor) |
| **5-foot step** | 5 feet | No action, no AoO |

### Diagonal Movement Cost

| **Diagonal** | **Cost** |
|--------------|----------|
| 1st, 3rd, 5th... | 1 square |
| 2nd, 4th, 6th... | 2 squares |
