# How Combat Works

Combat is cyclical; everybody acts in turn in a regular cycle of rounds. Combat follows this sequence:

## Combat Sequence

### 1. Determine Awareness
Each combatant starts out **flat-footed**. Once a combatant acts, he or she is no longer flat-footed.

### 2. Handle Surprise Round (if applicable)
Determine which characters are aware of their opponents at the start of the battle. If some but not all of the combatants are aware of their opponents, a **surprise round** happens before regular rounds of combat begin. 

**Surprise Round Rules:**
- The combatants who are aware of the opponents can act in the surprise round, so they roll for initiative
- In initiative order (highest to lowest), combatants who started the battle aware of their opponents each take **one action** (either a standard action or a move action) during the surprise round
- Combatants who were unaware do **not** get to act in the surprise round
- If **no one** or **everyone** starts the battle aware, there is **no surprise round**

### 3. Roll Initiative
Combatants who have not yet rolled initiative do so. All combatants are now ready to begin their first regular round of combat.

### 4. Regular Combat Rounds
- Combatants act in **initiative order** (highest to lowest)
- When everyone has had a turn, the combatant with the highest initiative acts again
- Steps 4 and 5 repeat until combat ends

## Key Concepts

- **Flat-footed**: Cannot use Dexterity bonus to AC, cannot make attacks of opportunity
- **Initiative order**: Determines when each combatant acts each round
- **Combat round**: Represents 6 seconds of game time
- **Actions**: Each combatant can take various types of actions on their turn
