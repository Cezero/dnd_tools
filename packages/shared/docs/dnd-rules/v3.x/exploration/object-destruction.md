# Object Destruction

When attempting to break an object, you have **two choices**: smash it with a weapon or break it with sheer strength.

## Smashing an Object

**Smashing a weapon or shield** with a slashing or bludgeoning weapon is accomplished by the **sunder special attack**. Smashing an object is **a lot like sundering** a weapon or shield, except that your attack roll is opposed by the **object's AC**. 

Generally, you can smash an object only with a **bludgeoning or slashing weapon**.

### Object Armor Class
Objects are **easier to hit than creatures** because they usually don't move, but many are tough enough to shrug off some damage from each blow. 

An object's **Armor Class** is equal to: **10 + size modifier + Dexterity modifier**

#### Inanimate Object Modifiers
An **inanimate object** has:
- **Dexterity of 0** (-5 penalty to AC)
- **Additional -2 penalty** to its AC
- **Total AC penalty**: -7 for being inanimate

#### Aimed Attacks
If you take a **full-round action to line up a shot**, you get:
- **Automatic hit** with a melee weapon
- **+5 bonus** on attack rolls with a ranged weapon

## Object Properties

### Hardness
Each object has **hardness** — a number that represents how well it resists damage. Whenever an object takes damage, **subtract its hardness from the damage**. Only damage **in excess of its hardness** is deducted from the object's hit points.

### Hit Points
An object's **hit point total** depends on what it is made of and how big it is. When an object's hit points **reach 0**, it's **ruined**.

**Very large objects** have **separate hit point totals** for different sections.

## Damage Types and Objects

### Energy Attacks
- **Acid and sonic**: Deal damage normally after a successful hit
- **Electricity and fire**: Deal **half damage** (divide by 2 before applying hardness)
- **Cold**: Deal **one-quarter damage** (divide by 4 before applying hardness)

### Ranged Weapon Damage
Objects take **half damage from ranged weapons** (unless the weapon is a siege engine or something similar). **Divide the damage by 2** before applying the object's hardness.

### Ineffective Weapons
**Certain weapons just can't effectively deal damage** to certain objects.

**Examples:**
- **Piercing weapons** vs. **cloth or rope** (minimal effect)
- **Slashing weapons** vs. **stone or metal** (reduced effectiveness)
- **Weapon material** vs. **harder object material** (normal weapons vs. adamantine)

## Object Immunities and Vulnerabilities

### Immunities
Objects are **immune to**:
- **Nonlethal damage**
- **Critical hits**

**Even animated objects** (which are otherwise considered creatures) have these immunities because they are **constructs**.

### Vulnerability to Certain Attacks
**Certain attacks are especially successful** against some objects. In such cases, attacks:
- Deal **double their normal damage**
- May **ignore the object's hardness**

**Examples:**
- **Fire** vs. **wood, paper, cloth**
- **Acid** vs. **metal**
- **Cold** vs. **brittle materials**

## Magic Items

### Magic Armor, Shields, and Weapons
Each **+1 of enhancement bonus** adds:
- **+2 to the hardness** of armor, a weapon, or a shield
- **+10 to the item's hit points**

### Saving Throws
#### Nonmagical Items
- **Nonmagical, unattended items**: **Never make saving throws** (considered to have failed)
- **Attended items** (grasped, touched, or worn): Make saving throws **as the character** (using the character's saving throw bonus)

#### Magic Items
- **Magic items always get saving throws**
- **Save bonuses**: Fortitude, Reflex, and Will save bonuses are equal to **2 + one-half its caster level**
- **Attended magic items**: Either make saving throws **as owner** or use **own saving throw bonus**, whichever is better

## Breaking Items with Strength

When a character tries to **break something with sudden force** rather than by dealing damage, use a **Strength check** (rather than an attack roll and damage roll) to see whether they succeed. The **DC depends more on construction** than on material.

### Size Modifiers for Breaking
**Larger and smaller creatures** get size bonuses and penalties on Strength checks to break open doors:

| **Size** | **Modifier** |
|----------|--------------|
| **Fine** | -16 |
| **Diminutive** | -12 |
| **Tiny** | -8 |
| **Small** | -4 |
| **Medium** | +0 |
| **Large** | +4 |
| **Huge** | +8 |
| **Gargantuan** | +12 |
| **Colossal** | +16 |

### Condition Modifiers
- **Item lost half or more HP**: DC to break **drops by 2**
- **Crowbar**: Improves chance of breaking open a door
- **Portable ram**: Improves chance of breaking open a door

## Special Cases

### Damaged Objects
A **damaged object remains fully functional** until the item's hit points are reduced to **0**, at which point it is **destroyed**.

**Damaged (but not destroyed) objects** can be repaired with the **Craft skill**.

### Animated Objects
**Animated objects count as creatures** for purposes of determining their Armor Class (do not treat them as inanimate objects).
