# Carrying Capacity

**Encumbrance rules** determine how much a character's armor and equipment slow them down. Encumbrance comes in two parts:
- **Encumbrance by armor**
- **Encumbrance by total weight**

## Encumbrance by Armor

A character's **armor** defines their:
- **Maximum Dexterity bonus to AC**
- **Armor check penalty**
- **Speed**
- **Running speed**

Unless your character is **weak** or **carrying a lot of gear**, that's all you need to know. The extra gear your character carries won't slow them down any more than the armor already does.

If your character is **weak or carrying a lot of gear**, then you'll need to calculate **encumbrance by weight**. This is most important when trying to carry some **heavy object**.

## Encumbrance by Weight

To determine whether your character's gear is heavy enough to slow them down more than armor already does:

1. **Total the weight** of all the character's items, including armor, weapons, and gear
2. **Compare this total** to the character's Strength on the Carrying Capacity table
3. **Determine load category**: light, medium, or heavy load

### Load Effects

Like armor, a character's load affects:
- **Maximum Dexterity bonus to AC**
- **Check penalty** (works like armor check penalty)
- **Movement speed**
- **Running speed**

### Important Rules
- A **medium or heavy load** counts as **medium or heavy armor** for abilities or skills restricted by armor
- **Carrying a light load** does not encumber a character
- If wearing armor, use the **worse figure** (from armor or from load) for each category
- **Do not stack penalties**

## Lifting and Dragging

### Maximum Lift
A character can **lift as much as their maximum load over their head**.

### Double Maximum (Overloaded)
A character can lift **double their maximum load** off the ground, but can only **stagger around** with it.

**While overloaded:**
- **Loses any Dexterity bonus to AC**
- Can move only **5 feet per round** (as a full-round action)

### Push or Drag
A character can generally **push or drag** along the ground as much as **five times their maximum load**.

**Modifying Conditions:**
- **Favorable conditions**: Double these numbers
- **Bad circumstances**: Reduce to one-half or less

## Tremendous Strength

For **Strength scores not shown** on the table:
1. Find the Strength score between **20 and 29** that has the same number in the "ones" digit
2. **Multiply** the numbers in that row **by 4 for every ten points** the creature's Strength is above the score for that row
