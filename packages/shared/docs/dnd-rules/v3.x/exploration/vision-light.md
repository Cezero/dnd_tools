# Vision and Light

**Dwarves and half-orcs** have darkvision, but everyone else needs light to see by. This section covers light sources, vision types, and illumination effects.

## Vision Types

### Normal Vision
Most **humans** and similar races have normal vision and require light sources to see in darkness.

### Low-Light Vision
**Characters with low-light vision** (elves, gnomes, and half-elves) can see objects **twice as far away** as the given radius. **Double the effective radius** of bright light and of shadowy illumination for such characters.

### Darkvision
**Characters with darkvision** (dwarves and half-orcs) can see **lit areas normally** as well as **dark areas within 60 feet**. A creature can't hide within **60 feet** of a character with darkvision unless it is **invisible or has cover**.

## Illumination Effects

### Bright Light
In an area of **bright light**, all characters can **see clearly**. A creature **can't hide** in an area of bright light unless it is **invisible or has cover**.

### Shadowy Illumination
In an area of **shadowy illumination**, a character can **see dimly**. Creatures within this area have **concealment** relative to that character (**20% miss chance**). A creature in an area of shadowy illumination can make a **Hide check** to conceal itself.

### Darkness
In areas of **darkness**, creatures without darkvision are **effectively blinded**. In addition to the obvious effects, a blinded creature has:
- **50% miss chance** in combat (all opponents have total concealment)
- **Loses any Dexterity bonus to AC**
- **Takes a -2 penalty to AC**
- **Moves at half speed**
- **Takes a -4 penalty** on Search checks and most Strength and Dexterity-based skill checks

> **See also**: [Physical Hazards - Darkness](../environments/hazards/physical-hazards.md) for complete blindness rules

## Light Source Considerations

### Duration Management
- **Torches and candles**: Limited duration, need replacement
- **Oil lamps**: Require fuel refills
- **Magical light**: May be dispelled or have limited duration
- **Everburning torch**: Permanent but expensive

### Tactical Considerations
- **Stealth**: Light sources reveal your location to enemies
- **Combat**: Areas of different illumination affect Hide checks and attacks
- **Range**: Different light sources have different effective ranges
- **Weather**: Wind and rain can extinguish flames (see [Weather](../environments/weather/))

### Light Source Interactions
- **Multiple sources**: Light areas can overlap and combine
- **Magical darkness**: Some spells create supernatural darkness that defeats normal light
- **Dispelling**: Magical light sources can be dispelled
- **Suppression**: Some effects can temporarily suppress light sources

### Special Situations

#### Underground
- **Complete darkness**: Most underground areas have no natural light
- **Phosphorescence**: Some underground areas have natural, dim light sources
- **Magical illumination**: Permanent light sources in constructed areas

#### Outdoor Night
- **Moonlight**: Provides shadowy illumination in open areas
- **Starlight**: Minimal illumination, effectively darkness
- **Overcast**: Clouds can reduce natural nighttime illumination

#### Weather Effects
- **Fog**: Reduces effective light radius (see [Weather - Precipitation](../environments/weather/precipitation.md))
- **Rain**: May extinguish unprotected flames
- **Wind**: Can blow out candles and torches
