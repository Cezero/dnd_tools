### Monster Advancement

| **Method** | **HD Increase** | **Size Change** | **Ability Increases** |
|------------|-----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Age** | Varies | Sometimes | All abilities |
| **Class Levels** | +1 per level | No | Per class |

| **Templates** | Varies | Rarely | Varies |
