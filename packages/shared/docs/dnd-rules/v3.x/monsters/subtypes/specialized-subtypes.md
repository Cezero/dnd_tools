# Specialized Subtypes

Specialized subtypes represent creatures with unique characteristics that don't fit into other categories.

## Shapechanger Subtype

A **shapechanger** has the **supernatural ability** to assume **one or more alternate forms**.

### Important Note
Many magical effects allow some kind of shape shifting, and **not every creature that can change shape has the shapechanger subtype**.

### Traits

A shapechanger possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry):

#### Weapon Proficiency
- **Proficient with its natural weapons**
- **Proficient with simple weapons**
- **Proficient with any weapons** mentioned in the creature's description

#### Armor Proficiency
- **Proficient with any armor** mentioned in the creature's description, **as well as all lighter forms**
- If **no form of armor is mentioned**, the shapechanger is **not proficient with armor**
- A shapechanger is **proficient with shields** if it is proficient with **any type of armor**

## Augmented Subtype

A creature receives this subtype whenever **something happens to change its original type**.

### How It's Acquired
- Some creatures (those with an **inherited template**) are **born with this subtype**
- Others **acquire it** when they take on an **acquired template**

### Properties
- The **augmented subtype is always paired** with the creature's **original type**
- A creature with the augmented subtype usually has the **traits of its current type**, but the **features of its original type**

## Extraplanar Subtype

A subtype applied to **any creature when it is on a plane other than its native plane**.

### Dynamic Nature
- A creature that **travels the planes** can **gain or lose this subtype** as it goes from plane to plane
- Monster entries assume that encounters with creatures take place on the **Material Plane**
- Every creature whose **native plane is not the Material Plane** has the **extraplanar subtype** (but would not have when on its home plane)
- Creatures not labeled as extraplanar are **natives of the Material Plane**, and they **gain the extraplanar subtype if they leave the Material Plane**

### Transitive Planes Exception
- **No creature has the extraplanar subtype** when it is on a **transitive plane**, such as:
  - The **Astral Plane**
  - The **Ethereal Plane** 
  - The **Plane of Shadow**

### Home Plane Information
- Every extraplanar creature in this book has a **home plane mentioned in its description**

## Native Subtype

A subtype applied only to **outsiders**. These creatures have **mortal ancestors** or a **strong connection to the Material Plane**.

### Traits
- Can be **raised, reincarnated, or resurrected** just as other living creatures can be
- Creatures with this subtype are **native to the Material Plane** (hence the subtype's name)
- Unlike **true outsiders**, native outsiders **need to eat and sleep**

## Goblinoid Subtype

**Goblinoids** are **stealthy humanoids** who live by **hunting and raiding** and who **all speak Goblin**.

### Characteristics
- **Stealthy** nature
- **Hunting and raiding** lifestyle
- **Common language**: All goblinoids speak Goblin

## Reptilian Subtype

These creatures are **scaly** and usually **coldblooded**.

### Important Note
The **reptilian subtype is only used** to describe a **set of humanoid races**, **not all animals and monsters that are truly reptiles**.

### Characteristics
- **Scaly** skin
- Usually **coldblooded** physiology
- Applied specifically to **humanoid races** rather than all reptilian creatures
