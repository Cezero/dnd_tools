# Monster Subtypes

**Subtypes** provide additional characteristics and abilities to creatures beyond their basic type. A creature can have multiple subtypes.

## Subtype Documentation

### Elemental Subtypes
- **[Air Subtype](elemental-subtypes.md#air-subtype)** — Elemental Plane of Air connection, perfect fly speeds
- **[Earth Subtype](elemental-subtypes.md#earth-subtype)** — Elemental Plane of Earth connection, burrow speeds
- **[Fire Subtype](elemental-subtypes.md#fire-subtype)** — Fire immunity, cold vulnerability
- **[Cold Subtype](elemental-subtypes.md#cold-subtype)** — Cold immunity, fire vulnerability
- **[Water Subtype](elemental-subtypes.md#water-subtype)** — Elemental Plane of Water connection, swim speeds

### Alignment Subtypes
- **[Chaotic Subtype](alignment-subtypes.md#chaotic-subtype)** — Chaotic-aligned outsiders
- **[Evil Subtype](alignment-subtypes.md#evil-subtype)** — Evil-aligned outsiders (fiends)
- **[Good Subtype](alignment-subtypes.md#good-subtype)** — Good-aligned outsiders
- **[Lawful Subtype](alignment-subtypes.md#lawful-subtype)** — Lawful-aligned outsiders

### Celestial Subtypes
- **[Angel Subtype](celestial-subtypes.md#angel-subtype)** — Good celestial outsiders with protective auras
- **[Archon Subtype](celestial-subtypes.md#archon-subtype)** — Lawful good celestials with aura of menace

### Physical Subtypes
- **[Incorporeal Subtype](physical-subtypes.md#incorporeal-subtype)** — No physical body, special interaction rules
- **[Swarm Subtype](physical-subtypes.md#swarm-subtype)** — Collections of tiny creatures acting as one
- **[Aquatic Subtype](physical-subtypes.md#aquatic-subtype)** — Always have swim speeds, breathe underwater

### Specialized Subtypes
- **[Shapechanger Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#shapechanger-subtype)** — Supernatural shape-changing ability
- **[Augmented Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#augmented-subtype)** — Type changed from original
- **[Extraplanar Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#extraplanar-subtype)** — Creature not on native plane
- **[Native Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#native-subtype)** — Outsiders with mortal connections
- **[Goblinoid Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#goblinoid-subtype)** — Stealthy humanoid raiders
- **[Reptilian Subtype](specialized-subtypes.md#reptilian-subtype)** — Scaly, cold-blooded humanoids

## Quick Reference

### Subtype Effects Summary

| **Subtype** | **Key Benefits** | **Key Vulnerabilities** |
|-------------|------------------|------------------------|
| **Air** | Perfect fly speed | — |
| **Earth** | Burrow through rock | — |
| **Fire** | Fire immunity | Cold vulnerability (+50%) |
| **Cold** | Cold immunity | Fire vulnerability (+50%) |
| **Water** | Swim speed, breathe underwater | — |
| **Incorporeal** | 50% miss chance, pass through objects | Force effects always hit |
| **Swarm** | Immunity to single-target spells | +50% area damage |

### Alignment Subtypes

| **Subtype** | **Alignment Effect** | **DR Overcome** |
|-------------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Chaotic** | Treated as chaotic | Chaotic weapons |
| **Evil** | Treated as evil | Evil weapons |
| **Good** | Treated as good | Good weapons |
| **Lawful** | Treated as lawful | Lawful weapons |

### Special Qualities by Subtype

#### Celestial Powers
- **Angels**: Protective aura (+4 AC/saves), tongues, immunities
- **Archons**: Aura of menace, greater teleport, magic circle vs evil

#### Physical Traits
- **Incorporeal**: Deflection bonus = Cha bonus, no natural armor
- **Swarm**: No critical hits, special attack patterns
- **Aquatic**: Automatic swim success, underwater breathing
