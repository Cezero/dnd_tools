# Alignment Subtypes

Alignment subtypes are usually applied only to **outsiders native to the aligned Outer Planes**. These subtypes affect how creatures interact with alignment-based effects and damage reduction.

## Chaotic Subtype

A subtype usually applied only to **outsiders native to the chaotic-aligned Outer Planes**.

### Traits
- **Most creatures** that have this subtype also have **chaotic alignments**
- However, **if their alignments change** they **still retain the subtype**
- **Any effect that depends on alignment** affects a creature with this subtype **as if the creature has a chaotic alignment**, no matter what its alignment actually is
- The creature also **suffers effects according to its actual alignment**
- A creature with the chaotic subtype **overcomes damage reduction** as if its natural weapons and any weapons it wields were **chaotic-aligned**

## Evil Subtype

A subtype usually applied only to **outsiders native to the evil-aligned Outer Planes**. **Evil outsiders are also called fiends**.

### Traits

## Damage Reduction Interaction

All alignment subtypes allow creatures to overcome certain types of damage reduction:

- **Chaotic subtype**: Weapons count as chaotic-aligned
- **Evil subtype**: Weapons count as evil-aligned  
- **Good subtype**: Weapons count as good-aligned
- **Lawful subtype**: Weapons count as lawful-aligned

This applies to both **natural weapons** and **any weapons the creature wields**.
