# Celestial Subtypes

Celestial subtypes represent **good outsiders** native to the good-aligned Outer Planes, each with distinctive abilities and traits.

## Angel Subtype

**Angels** are a race of **celestials**, or **good outsiders**, native to the **good-aligned Outer Planes**.

### Traits

An angel possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry):

#### Senses
- **Darkvision** out to **60 feet** and **low-light vision**

#### Resistances and Immunities
- **Immunity** to **acid, cold, and petrification**
- **Resistance** to **electricity 10** and **fire 10**
- **+4 racial bonus** on **saves against poison**

#### Special Abilities

##### Protective Aura (Su)
Against **attacks made or effects created by evil creatures**, this ability provides:
- **+4 deflection bonus to AC** 
- **+4 resistance bonus on saving throws**
- Affects **anyone within 20 feet** of the angel

Otherwise, it functions as:
- **Magic circle against evil** effect with a **radius of 20 feet**
- **Lesser globe of invulnerability** with a **radius of 20 feet**
- **Caster level equals angel's HD**

*Note: The defensive benefits from the circle are not included in an angel's statistics block.*

##### Tongues (Su)
- All angels can **speak with any creature that has a language**
- Functions as though using a **tongues spell** (caster level equal to angel's Hit Dice)
- This ability is **always active**

## Archon Subtype

**Archons** are a race of **celestials**, or **good outsiders**, native to **lawful good-aligned Outer Planes**.

### Traits

An archon possesses the following traits (unless otherwise noted in a creature's entry):

#### Senses
- **Darkvision** out to **60 feet** and **low-light vision**

#### Special Abilities

##### Aura of Menace (Su)
A **righteous aura** surrounds archons that **fight or get angry**.

**Effect:**
- Any **hostile creature within a 20-foot radius** of an archon must succeed on a **Will save** to resist its effects
- The **save DC varies** with the type of archon, is **Charisma-based**, and includes a **+2 racial bonus**
- Those who **fail** take a **-2 penalty on attacks, AC, and saves for 24 hours** or until they successfully hit the archon that generated the aura
- A creature that has **resisted or broken the effect** cannot be affected again by the **same archon's aura for 24 hours**

#### Resistances and Immunities
- **Immunity** to **electricity and petrification**
- **+4 racial bonus** on **saves against poison**

##### Magic Circle against Evil (Su)
- A **magic circle against evil** effect **always surrounds an archon**
- **Caster level equals the archon's Hit Dice**

*Note: The defensive benefits from the circle are not included in an archon's statistics block.*

##### Teleport (Su)
- Archons can use **greater teleport at will**, as the spell (**caster level 14th**)
- **Exception**: The creature can transport only **itself and up to 50 pounds of objects**

##### Tongues (Su)
- All archons can **speak with any creature that has a language**
- Functions as though using a **tongues spell** (**caster level 14th**)
- This ability is **always active**
