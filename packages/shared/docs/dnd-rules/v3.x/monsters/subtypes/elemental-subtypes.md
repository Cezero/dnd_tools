# Elemental Subtypes

Elemental subtypes represent creatures with strong connections to the elemental planes.

## Air Subtype

This subtype usually is used for **elementals and outsiders** with a connection to the **Elemental Plane Air**.

### Traits
- **Air creatures always have fly speeds** and usually have **perfect maneuverability**

## Earth Subtype

This subtype usually is used for **elementals and outsiders** with a connection to the **Elemental Plane of Earth**.

### Traits
- **Earth creatures usually have burrow speeds**
- **Most earth creatures can burrow through solid rock**

## Fire Subtype

A creature with the **fire subtype** has special resistances and vulnerabilities related to fire and cold.

### Traits
- **Immunity to fire**
- **Vulnerability to cold** — takes **half again as much (+50%) damage** as normal from cold, regardless of whether a saving throw is allowed, or if the save is a success or failure

## Cold Subtype

A creature with the **cold subtype** has special resistances and vulnerabilities related to cold and fire.

### Traits
- **Immunity to cold**
- **Vulnerability to fire** — takes **half again as much (+50%) damage** as normal from fire, regardless of whether a saving throw is allowed, or if the save is a success or failure

## Water Subtype

This subtype usually is used for **elementals and outsiders** with a connection to the **Elemental Plane of Water**.

### Traits
- **Creatures with the water subtype always have swim speeds**
- Can **move in water without making Swim checks**
- A **water creature can breathe underwater** and usually can **breathe air as well**
