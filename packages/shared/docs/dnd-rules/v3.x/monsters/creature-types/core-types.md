# Core Creature Types

This document covers the fundamental creature types that form the backbone of D&D's monster classification system.

## Aberration Type

An aberration has a **bizarre anatomy**, **strange abilities**, an **alien mindset**, or any combination of the three.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 8-sided Hit Dice
- **Base Attack Bonus**: ¾ total Hit Dice (as cleric)
- **Good Saves**: Will saves
- **Skill Points**: (2 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die, with quadruple skill points for the first Hit Die

### Traits
- **Darkvision**: Out to 60 feet
- **Weapon Proficiency**: 
  - **Natural weapons**: Proficient with all natural weapons
  - **Humanoid forms**: Proficient with all simple weapons and described weapons
- **Armor Proficiency**: Proficient with described armor type and all lighter types
- **Biological Needs**: Eat, sleep, and breathe

### Examples
- Beholders, mind flayers, owlbears, rust monsters

---

## Animal Type

An animal is a **living, nonhuman creature**, usually a vertebrate with **no magical abilities** and **no innate capacity** for language or culture.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 8-sided Hit Dice  
- **Base Attack Bonus**: ¾ total Hit Dice (as cleric)
- **Good Saves**: Fortitude and Reflex (dire animals also have good Will saves)
- **Skill Points**: (2 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die, with quadruple skill points for the first Hit Die

### Traits
- **Intelligence**: Score of 1 or 2 (no creature with Intelligence 3+ can be an animal)
- **Low-light Vision**: Enhanced vision in dim light
- **Alignment**: Always neutral
- **Treasure**: None
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Natural weapons only
  - **Noncombative herbivores**: Natural weapons as secondary attacks (-5 penalty, ½ Str modifier to damage)
- **Armor Proficiency**: None unless trained for war
- **Biological Needs**: Eat, sleep, and breathe

### Examples
- Bears, wolves, eagles, horses, sharks

---

## Construct Type

A construct is an **animated object** or **artificially constructed creature**.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 10-sided Hit Dice
- **Base Attack Bonus**: ¾ total Hit Dice (as cleric)  
- **Good Saves**: None
- **Skill Points**: (2 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die with quadruple for first Hit Die, **if** the construct has Intelligence (most constructs are mindless)

### Size-Based Bonus Hit Points

| **Size** | **Bonus HP** |
|----------|--------------|
| Fine | — |
| Diminutive | — |
| Tiny | — |
| Small | 10 |
| Medium | 20 |
| Large | 30 |
| Huge | 40 |
| Gargantuan | 60 |
| Colossal | 80 |

### Traits
- **No Constitution Score**: Immune to Constitution-based effects
- **Vision**: Low-light vision and darkvision out to 60 feet
- **Mind Immunity**: Immune to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, phantasms, patterns, morale effects)
- **Condition Immunity**: Immune to poison, sleep, paralysis, stunning, disease, death effects, necromancy effects
- **Damage Immunity**: Not subject to critical hits, nonlethal damage, ability damage/drain, fatigue, exhaustion, energy drain
- **Fortitude Immunity**: Immune to any effect requiring Fortitude save (unless it works on objects or is harmless)
- **Healing**: Cannot heal naturally; often repaired by specific effects or Craft Construct feat
- **Death**: Not at risk from massive damage; destroyed at 0 hit points; cannot be raised or resurrected
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Natural weapons only (unless humanoid form with described weapons)
- **Armor Proficiency**: None
- **Biological Needs**: Do not eat, sleep, or breathe

### Examples
- Golems, animated objects, shield guardians

---

## Dragon Type

A dragon is a **reptilelike creature**, usually winged, with **magical or unusual abilities**.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 12-sided Hit Dice
- **Base Attack Bonus**: Equal to total Hit Dice (as fighter)
- **Good Saves**: Fortitude, Reflex, and Will
- **Skill Points**: (6 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die, with quadruple skill points for the first Hit Die

### Traits
- **Vision**: Darkvision out to 60 feet and low-light vision
- **Immunity**: Sleep and paralysis effects
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Proficient with all natural weapons
- **Armor Proficiency**: Proficient with no armor
- **Biological Needs**: Eat, sleep, and breathe

### Examples
- True dragons (chromatic and metallic), dragonnes, wyverns

---

## Humanoid Type

A humanoid usually has **two arms, two legs, and one head**, or a **human-like torso, arms, and a head**. Humanoids have **few or no supernatural or spell-like abilities**, but most can speak and usually have well-developed societies.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 8-sided Hit Dice
- **Base Attack Bonus**: ¾ total Hit Dice (as cleric)
- **Good Saves**: Varies by humanoid type
- **Skill Points**: (2 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die, with quadruple skill points for the first Hit Die

### Traits
- **Vision**: Usually standard human vision (some have darkvision or low-light vision)
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Proficient with all simple weapons and martial weapons described
- **Armor Proficiency**: Proficient with described armor and shields
- **Biological Needs**: Eat, sleep, and breathe

### Examples
- Humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, goblins

---

## Undead Type

Undead are **once-living creatures** animated by **spiritual or supernatural forces**.

### Features
- **Hit Dice**: 12-sided Hit Dice
- **Base Attack Bonus**: ½ total Hit Dice (as wizard)
- **Good Saves**: Will only
- **Skill Points**: (4 + Int modifier, minimum 1) per Hit Die, with quadruple skill points for the first Hit Die

### Traits
- **No Constitution Score**: Immune to Constitution-based effects
- **Vision**: Darkvision out to 60 feet
- **Immunity**: Mind-affecting effects, poison, sleep, paralysis, stunning, disease, death effects
- **Condition Immunity**: Not subject to critical hits, nonlethal damage, ability damage/drain, fatigue, exhaustion, energy drain
- **Fortitude Immunity**: Immune to effects requiring Fortitude saves (unless harmless or affects objects)
- **Healing**: Cannot heal naturally; usually healed by negative energy
- **Death**: Not at risk from massive damage; destroyed at 0 hit points
- **Turn Resistance**: Many undead have turn resistance
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Proficient with natural weapons and described weapons
- **Armor Proficiency**: Proficient with described armor types
- **Biological Needs**: Do not eat, sleep, or breathe

### Examples
- Skeletons, zombies, ghosts, liches, vampires
