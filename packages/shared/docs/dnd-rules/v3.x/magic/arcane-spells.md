# Arcane Spells

**Wizards, sorcerers, and bards** cast arcane spells. Compared to divine spells, arcane spells are **more likely to produce dramatic results**.

## Preparing Wizard Spells

A wizard's **level** limits the number of spells she can prepare and cast. Her high **Intelligence score** might allow her to prepare a few extra spells.

### Basic Rules
- She can prepare the **same spell more than once**, but each preparation counts as one spell toward her daily limit
- To prepare a spell, the wizard must have an **Intelligence score of at least 10 + the spell's level**

## Rest Requirements

To prepare her daily spells, a wizard must first **sleep for 8 hours**. The wizard does not have to slumber for every minute, but she must refrain from:
- **Movement**
- **Combat**
- **Spellcasting**
- **Skill use**
- **Conversation**
- **Any other fairly demanding physical or mental task**

### Rest Interruptions
- If her rest is **interrupted**, each interruption adds **1 hour** to the total rest time
- She must have at least **1 hour of uninterrupted rest** immediately prior to preparing spells
- If the character **does not need to sleep** for some reason, she still must have **8 hours of restful calm** before preparing any spells

## Recent Casting Limit

If a wizard has cast spells recently, the drain on her resources reduces her capacity to prepare new spells. When she prepares spells for the coming day, **all spells cast within the last 8 hours** count against her daily limit.

## Preparation Environment

To prepare any spell, a wizard must have enough **peace, quiet, and comfort** to allow for proper concentration.

### Environmental Requirements
- Surroundings need not be luxurious, but must be **free from overt distractions**
- **Exposure to inclement weather** prevents the necessary concentration
- Any **injury or failed saving throw** while studying prevents concentration
- Must have **access to spellbooks** to study from
- Must have **sufficient light** to read them by

### Major Exception
A wizard can prepare a **read magic** spell even **without a spellbook**.

## Spell Preparation Time

After resting, a wizard must **study her spellbook** to prepare any spells that day.

### Time Requirements
- **Full preparation**: 1 hour (all spells)
- **Partial preparation**: Proportionally smaller time, but **always at least 15 minutes** (minimum for proper mental state)

## Spell Selection and Preparation

Until she prepares spells from her spellbook, the only spells a wizard has available are ones that she **already had prepared from the previous day** and has not yet used.

### Preparation Rules
- During the study period, she **chooses which spells to prepare**
- If she already has spells prepared from the previous day that she has not cast, she can **abandon some or all** to make room for new spells
- She can **leave some spell slots open** when preparing spells for the day

### Multiple Preparation Sessions
- Later during the day, she can **repeat the preparation process** as often as she likes (time and circumstances permitting)
- During extra sessions, she can **fill unused spell slots**
- She **cannot abandon a previously prepared spell** to replace it with another one
- She **cannot fill a slot that is empty** because she has cast a spell in the meantime
- That sort of preparation requires a **mind fresh from rest**
- Extra preparation takes **at least 15 minutes**, and longer if preparing more than one-quarter of her spells

## Spell Slots

The character class tables show **how many spells of each level** a character can cast per day. These openings are called **spell slots**.

### Slot Rules
- A spellcaster can **fill a higher-level slot with a lower-level spell**
- A spellcaster who **lacks sufficient ability score** still gets the slots but must fill them with **lower-level spells**

## Prepared Spell Retention

Once a wizard prepares a spell, it remains in her mind as a **nearly cast spell** until she:
- **Uses the prescribed components** to complete and trigger it
- **Abandons it**

Certain other events can wipe a prepared spell from a character's mind:
- **Effects of magic items**
- **Special attacks from monsters**

### Death and Prepared Spells
If a spellcaster **dies**, all prepared spells stored in their mind are **wiped away**. Potent magic (such as **raise dead**, **resurrection**, or **true resurrection**) can recover the lost energy when it recovers the character.

## Arcane Magical Writings

To record an arcane spell in written form, a character uses **complex notation** that describes the magical forces involved in the spell.

### Writing System
- The writer uses the **same system** no matter what her native language or culture
- Each character uses the system **in her own way**
- Another person's magical writing remains **incomprehensible** to even the most powerful wizard until she takes time to study and decipher it

### Deciphering Magical Writing
To decipher an arcane magical writing (such as a single spell in another's spellbook or on a scroll):
- Make a **Spellcraft check (DC 20 + spell's level)**
- If the check **fails**, the character cannot attempt to read that particular spell again **until the next day**
- A **read magic** spell automatically deciphers magical writing without a skill check
- If the **person who created the writing** is on hand to help, success is also automatic

### After Deciphering
- Once a character deciphers a particular magical writing, she **does not need to decipher it again**
- Deciphering allows the reader to **identify the spell** and gives some idea of its effects
- If the magical writing was a **scroll** and the reader can cast arcane spells, she can attempt to **use the scroll**

## Wizard Spells and Borrowed Spellbooks

A wizard can use a **borrowed spellbook** to prepare a spell she already knows and has recorded in her own spellbook, but preparation success is **not assured**.

### Process
1. **Decipher the writing** in the book (see Arcane Magical Writings above)
2. Make a **Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell's level)** to prepare the spell
3. If the check **succeeds**, the wizard can prepare the spell
4. She must **repeat the check** to prepare the spell again, no matter how many times she has prepared it before
5. If the check **fails**, she cannot try to prepare the spell from the same source again **until the next day**

## Adding Spells to a Wizard's Spellbook

Wizards can add new spells to their spellbooks through several methods. If a wizard has **specialized in a school of magic**, she can learn spells only from schools whose spells she can cast.

### Spells Gained at a New Level
Wizards perform spell research between adventures. Each time a character attains a new wizard level, she gains **two spells of her choice** to add to her spellbook.
- The two free spells must be of **spell levels she can cast**
- If she has specialized in a school of magic, **one of the two free spells** must be from her **specialty school**

### Spells Copied from Another's Spellbook or Scroll
A wizard can add a spell to her book whenever she encounters one on a magic scroll or in another wizard's spellbook.

#### Copying Process
1. **Decipher the magical writing** (see Arcane Magical Writings above)
2. **Spend a day studying** the spell
3. At the end of the day, make a **Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell's level)**
   - **Specialist bonus**: +2 bonus if the new spell is from her specialty school
   - **Prohibited schools**: Cannot learn spells from prohibited schools
4. If the check **succeeds**, the wizard understands the spell and can copy it into her spellbook
5. **Results**: 
   - Spellbook that was copied from remains **unharmed**
   - Spell successfully copied from a **magic scroll disappears** from the parchment

#### Failure and Fees
- If the check **fails**, the wizard cannot understand or copy the spell
- She cannot attempt to learn or copy that spell again **until she gains another rank in Spellcraft**
- A spell being copied from a scroll **does not vanish** if the check fails
- Most wizards charge a fee for copying spells: usually **spell level × 50 gp**

### Independent Research
A wizard can **research a spell independently**, duplicating an existing spell or creating an entirely new one.

## Writing a New Spell into a Spellbook

Once a wizard understands a new spell, she can record it into her spellbook.

### Requirements
- **Time**: 24 hours, regardless of the spell's level
- **Space**: One page per spell level (even 0-level spells take one page)
- **Spellbook capacity**: 100 pages total
- **Materials cost**: 100 gp per page

**Note**: A wizard does **not** have to pay these costs in time or gold for the spells she gains **free at each new level**.

## Replacing and Copying Spellbooks

### Reconstructing a Lost Spellbook
A wizard can use the spell learning procedure to reconstruct a lost spellbook:
- If she has a particular spell **prepared**, she can write it directly into a new book at **100 gp per page**
- The process **wipes the prepared spell** from her mind, just as casting it would
- If she does **not have the spell prepared**, she can prepare it from a borrowed spellbook and then write it

### Duplicating an Existing Spellbook
Uses the same procedure as replacing, but the task is **much easier**:
- **Time requirement**: Halved
- **Cost per page**: Halved

### Selling a Spellbook
- **Captured spellbooks**: Can be sold for **½ the cost** of purchasing and inscribing the spells (½ of 100 gp per page)
- **Completely filled spellbook** (100 pages of spells): Worth **5,000 gp**

## Sorcerers and Bards

**Sorcerers and bards** cast arcane spells, but they **do not have spellbooks** and **do not prepare their spells**.

### Requirements
- A sorcerer's or bard's **class level** limits the number of spells he can cast
- High **Charisma score** might allow a few extra spells
- Must have a **Charisma score of at least 10 + spell's level** to cast the spell

### Daily Readying of Spells
Each day, sorcerers and bards must **focus their minds** on casting their spells:
1. **8 hours of rest** (just like a wizard)
2. **15 minutes concentrating** (bards must sing, recite, or play an instrument)
3. During this period, the character **readies his mind** to cast his daily allotment of spells
4. **Without such a period**, the character does not regain the spell slots used the day before

### Recent Casting Limit
As with wizards, any spells cast within the **last 8 hours** count against the sorcerer's or bard's daily limit.

### Adding Spells to Repertoire
A sorcerer or bard **gains spells each time he attains a new level** in his class and **never gains spells any other way**.
- When gaining a new level, consult the class tables to learn how many spells from the appropriate spell list he now knows
- With permission, sorcerers and bards can select spells they gain from **new and unusual spells** they have gained some understanding of
