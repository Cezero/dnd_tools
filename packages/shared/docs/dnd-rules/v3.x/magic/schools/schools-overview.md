# Schools of Magic

Almost every spell belongs to one of **eight schools of magic**. A school of magic is a **group of related spells** that work in similar ways.

## The Eight Schools

### Abjuration
**Protective spells** that create barriers and defenses.

#### Primary Functions
- **Create physical or magical barriers**
- **Negate magical or physical abilities**
- **Harm trespassers**
- **Banish subjects to another plane**

#### Special Rules
- **Field interference**: If one abjuration spell is active within **10 feet** of another for **24 hours or more**, magical fields interfere
- **Search penalty**: DC to find such spells with Search skill **drops by 4**
- **Barrier limitations**: Cannot be used to **push away** creatures the barrier keeps at bay
- **Pressure feedback**: Forcing barrier against protected creature creates **discernible pressure**
- **Spell termination**: Continuing to apply pressure **ends the spell**

#### Examples
- *Dispel magic*, *protection from evil*, *shield*, *antimagic field*

---

### Conjuration
Spells that **bring manifestations** of objects, creatures, or energy.

#### Five Subschools
Each conjuration spell belongs to one of **five subschools**:

##### Calling
- **Transports creatures** from another plane to your plane
- **One-time return ability** to plane of origin
- **Creatures actually die** when killed (don't reform)
- **Instantaneous duration** — cannot be dispelled

##### Creation
- **Manipulates matter** to create objects or creatures
- **Duration dependent**: 
  - **Non-instantaneous**: Magic holds creation together, vanishes when spell ends
  - **Instantaneous**: Assembled through magic, lasts indefinitely

##### Healing
- **Divine conjurations** that heal creatures or bring them back to life

##### Summoning
- **Instantly brings** creatures or objects to designated place
- **Temporary presence**: Summoned creatures return when spell ends
- **Death mechanics**: Summoned creatures "die" at 0 hp but reform in 24 hours
- **Spell limitations**: Cannot use innate summoning or XP-costing abilities

##### Teleportation
- **Transports creatures or objects** great distances
- **Can cross planar boundaries** with powerful versions
- **One-way travel** (unless otherwise noted) and not dispellable
- **Astral travel**: Instantaneous through Astral Plane
- **Blocking effects**: Anything blocking astral travel blocks teleportation

#### General Rules
- **Cannot appear inside** another creature or object
- **Must arrive** in open location on supporting surface
- **Must appear within range** but doesn't have to remain there
- **Usually obey commands** but not always

#### Examples
- *Summon monster*, *teleport*, *cure light wounds*, *create water*

---

### Divination
Spells that **enable learning secrets**, predicting the future, finding hidden things, and foiling deceptive spells.

#### Special Mechanics
- **Cone-shaped areas**: Many divination spells use cones that move with caster
- **Direction dependent**: Extend in direction you look
- **Area definition**: Cone defines area you can sweep each round
- **Multiple rounds**: Studying same area can gain additional information

#### Scrying Subschool
**Remote sensing** spells that allow observation at a distance:
- **Magical sensors** created to observe distant locations
- **Detection difficulty**: Can be detected by those being watched
- **Spell resistance**: Some scrying effects allow spell resistance
- **Blocking methods**: Various ways to prevent scrying

#### Examples
- *Detect magic*, *scrying*, *true seeing*, *legend lore*

---

### Enchantment
**Mind-affecting spells** that influence thoughts and emotions.

#### Two Subschools

##### Charm
- **Changes target's perception** of the caster
- **Friendship effects**: Target regards caster as trusted friend
- **Retains identity**: Target keeps own personality and goals
- **Suggestion susceptible**: More likely to accept caster's ideas

##### Compulsion
- **Forces target to act** in specific ways
- **Overrides free will**: Direct control over actions
- **Mental domination**: Can force unwilling behavior
- **Stronger effects**: More direct than charm effects

#### Mind-Affecting Rules
- **Intelligence requirement**: Usually requires Intelligence score
- **Immunity**: Mindless creatures typically immune
- **Duration effects**: May have lingering mental changes
- **Dispelling**: Can usually be dispelled normally

#### Examples
- *Charm person*, *suggestion*, *dominate person*, *hold person*

---

### Evocation
Spells that **manipulate energy** or create something from nothing.

#### Energy Manipulation
- **Raw magical energy** shaped into desired effects
- **Elemental forces**: Fire, cold, electricity, acid, sonic
- **Force effects**: Pure magical force
- **Light and darkness**: Illumination effects

#### Damage Dealing
- **Direct damage**: Most damaging spells are evocation
- **Energy types**: Various forms of harmful energy
- **Area effects**: Many evocations affect large areas
- **Precise targeting**: Some allow careful target selection

#### Examples
- *Fireball*, *lightning bolt*, *magic missile*, *wall of fire*

---

### Illusion
Spells that **deceive the senses** or minds of others.

#### Illusion Categories
- **Visual illusions**: Affect sight
- **Auditory illusions**: Affect hearing  
- **Mental illusions**: Directly affect the mind
- **Multi-sensory**: Affect multiple senses

#### Reality Interaction
- **Disbelief saves**: Will saves to see through illusions
- **Physical interaction**: Some illusions have physical substance
- **Duration effects**: How long illusions persist
- **Detection methods**: Ways to identify illusions

#### Examples
- *Invisibility*, *mirror image*, *phantasmal force*, *major image*

---

### Necromancy
Spells that **manipulate life force** and the boundary between life and death.

#### Life and Death
- **Negative energy**: Harms living, heals undead
- **Positive energy**: Heals living, harms undead
- **Life force manipulation**: Direct effects on life energy
- **Death effects**: Instant death or major harm

#### Undead Creation
- **Animation spells**: Create undead servants
- **Control spells**: Command existing undead
- **Enhancement spells**: Improve undead abilities
- **Communication**: Speak with dead or undead

#### Examples
- *Animate dead*, *inflict wounds*, *speak with dead*, *energy drain*

---

### Transmutation
Spells that **transform or change** the properties of creatures, objects, or conditions.

#### Transformation Types
- **Physical changes**: Alter appearance or form
- **Property changes**: Modify material properties
- **Enhancement**: Improve existing capabilities
- **Polymorph**: Change into different creature types

#### Change Categories
- **Temporary changes**: Revert when spell ends
- **Permanent changes**: Last until dispelled or countered
- **Progressive changes**: Gradual transformation over time
- **Conditional changes**: Triggered under specific circumstances

#### Examples
- *Polymorph*, *haste*, *stone to flesh*, *enlarge person*

---

## Universal Spells

A small number of spells belong to **no school** and are considered **universal**:

### Universal Spell List
- **Arcane mark** — Personal magical signature
- **Limited wish** — Minor reality alteration
- **Permanency** — Makes other spells permanent
- **Prestidigitation** — Minor magical effects
- **Wish** — Major reality alteration

### Universal Properties
- **No school restrictions**: Available regardless of school specialization
- **Broad applications**: Serve multiple magical purposes
- **Meta-magical nature**: Often affect other magic itself
