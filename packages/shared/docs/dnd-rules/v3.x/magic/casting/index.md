## Quick Reference

### Basic Casting Requirements
- **Choose spell** from prepared spells or known spells
- **Speak** (verbal components)
- **Gesture** (somatic components)  
- **Materials/Focus** (material components)
- **Concentrate** (maintain focus)

### Concentration Check DCs

| **Situation** | **DC** |
|---------------|--------|
| **Injured while casting** | 10 + damage + spell level |
| **Continuous damage** | 10 + ½ damage + spell level |
| **Affected by spell** | Spell's save DC + spell level |
| **Grappling/pinned** | 20 + spell level |
| **Vigorous motion** | 10 + spell level |
| **Violent motion** | 15 + spell level |
| **Bad weather** | 5-10 + spell level |
| **Casting defensively** | 15 + spell level |
| **Entangled** | 15 |

### Caster Level Effects
- **Range**: Often "X ft. + Y ft./level"
- **Duration**: Often "X rounds/minutes/hours per level"  
- **Damage**: Often "XdY per Z levels (max)"
- **Spell Resistance**: 1d20 + caster level vs. SR
- **Dispel Checks**: 1d20 + caster level vs. DC (11 + target's caster level)
