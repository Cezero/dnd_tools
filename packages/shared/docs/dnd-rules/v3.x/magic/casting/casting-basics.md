# Casting Spells - Basics

Whether a spell is arcane or divine, and whether a character prepares spells in advance or chooses them on the spot, **casting a spell works the same way**.

## Choosing A Spell

First you must **choose which spell to cast**:

### Prepared Casters
If you're a **cleric, druid, experienced paladin, experienced ranger, or wizard**, you select from among spells **prepared earlier in the day** and not yet cast.

> **See**: [Preparing Wizard Spells](../arcane-spells.md) and [Preparing Divine Spells](../divine-spells.md)

### Spontaneous Casters  
If you're a **bard or sorcerer**, you can select **any spell you know**, provided you are capable of casting spells of that level or higher.

## Basic Casting Requirements

To cast a spell, you must be able to:
- **Speak** (if the spell has a verbal component)
- **Gesture** (if it has a somatic component)  
- **Manipulate the material components or focus** (if any)
- **Concentrate** to cast a spell

## Spell Versions

If a spell has **multiple versions**, you choose which version to use when you cast it. You **don't have to prepare** (or learn, in the case of a bard or sorcerer) a specific version of the spell.

## After Casting

### Prepared Spells
Once you've cast a **prepared spell**, you can't cast it again until you **prepare it again**. If you've prepared **multiple copies** of a single spell, you can cast each copy once.

### Spontaneous Spells
If you're a **bard or sorcerer**, casting a spell counts against your **daily limit** for spells of that spell level, but you can cast the same spell again if you haven't reached your limit.

## Spell Failure

If you ever try to cast a spell in conditions where the **characteristics of the spell cannot be made to conform**, the casting fails and the spell is wasted.

Spells also fail if:
- Your **concentration is broken**
- You're **wearing armor** while casting a spell with somatic components (might fail)

## The Spell's Result

Once you know which creatures (or objects or areas) are affected, and whether those creatures have made successful **saving throws** (if any were allowed), you can apply whatever results a spell entails.
