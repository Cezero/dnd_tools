# Concentration Checks

To cast a spell, you must **concentrate**. If something interrupts your concentration while you're casting, you must make a **Concentration check** or lose the spell. The more distracting the interruption and the higher the level of the spell you are trying to cast, the higher the DC is.

**If you fail the check**, you lose the spell just as if you had cast it to no effect.

## Injury

If while trying to cast a spell you **take damage**, you must make a Concentration check:
- **DC**: 10 + points of damage taken + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Failure**: You lose the spell without effect

The interrupting event strikes during spellcasting if it comes:
- **Between when you start and when you complete** a spell (for a spell with a casting time of 1 full round or more)
- **In response to your casting** the spell (such as an attack of opportunity provoked by the spell or a contingent attack, such as a readied action)

### Continuous Damage
If you are taking **continuous damage**, half the damage is considered to take place while you are casting a spell:
- **DC**: 10 + ½ the damage that the continuous source last dealt + the level of the spell you're casting
- If the last damage dealt was the **last damage** that the effect could deal, then the damage is over and it does not distract you

**Note**: Repeated damage does **not** count as continuous damage.

## Spell Effects

If you are **affected by a spell** while attempting to cast a spell of your own, you must make a Concentration check or lose the spell you are casting.

### Damage-Dealing Spells
If the spell affecting you **deals damage**:
- **DC**: 10 + points of damage + the level of the spell you're casting

### Non-Damage Spells
If the spell **interferes with you or distracts you** in some other way:
- **DC**: The spell's saving throw DC + the level of the spell you're casting
- For a spell with **no saving throw**: Use the DC that the spell's saving throw would have if a save were allowed

## Grappling or Pinned

The only spells you can cast while **grappling or pinned** are those:
- **Without somatic components**
- Whose **material components** (if any) you have in hand

Even so, you must make a Concentration check:
- **DC**: 20 + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Failure**: Lose the spell

## Motion-Based Distractions

### Vigorous Motion
If you are in vigorous motion, you must make a Concentration check:
- **Examples**: Riding on a moving mount, taking a bouncy ride in a wagon, on a small boat in rough water, below-decks in a storm-tossed ship, or simply being jostled in a similar fashion
- **DC**: 10 + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Failure**: Lose the spell

### Violent Motion
If you are in violent motion, you must make a Concentration check:
- **Examples**: On a galloping horse, taking a very rough ride in a wagon, on a small boat in rapids or in a storm, on deck in a storm-tossed ship, or being tossed roughly about in a similar fashion
- **DC**: 15 + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Failure**: Lose the spell

## Weather Conditions

You must make a Concentration check if you try to cast a spell in **violent weather**:

### High Wind with Rain/Sleet
- **DC**: 5 + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Conditions**: High wind carrying blinding rain or sleet

### Wind-Driven Debris
- **DC**: 10 + the level of the spell you're casting  
- **Conditions**: Wind-driven hail, dust, or debris

**Note**: If the weather is caused by a spell, use the rules in the Spell subsection above.

## Defensive Casting

If you want to cast a spell **without provoking any attacks of opportunity**, you must make a Concentration check:
- **DC**: 15 + the level of the spell you're casting
- **Failure**: You lose the spell

## Entangled

If you want to cast a spell while **entangled** (in a net, by a tanglefoot bag, or while affected by a spell with similar effects), you must make a Concentration check:
- **DC**: 15
- **Failure**: You lose the spell
