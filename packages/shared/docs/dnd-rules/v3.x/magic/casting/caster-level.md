# Caster Level

**Caster level** is a measure of a spellcaster's power and determines many aspects of spell effects.

## What Caster Level Affects

Caster level influences:
- **Range** of many spells
- **Duration** of many spells  
- **Damage** dealt by many spells
- **Area of effect** for some spells
- **Spell resistance** checks
- **Dispel checks** (both making them and the DC to dispel your spells)

## Determining Caster Level

### Class Levels
Your **caster level** equals your **class level** in the spellcasting class:
- **Wizard 5th level**: Caster level 5th
- **Cleric 3rd level**: Caster level 3rd
- **Multiclass example**: Wizard 3/Cleric 4 = 3rd level arcane caster, 4th level divine caster

### Partial Casters
Some classes cast at **reduced caster level**:
- **Paladin**: Caster level = class level - 3 (minimum 1st)
- **Ranger**: Caster level = class level - 3 (minimum 1st)
- **Bard**: Full caster level (class level)
- **Eldritch Knight**: Some spellcasting advancement
- **Mystic Theurge**: Advances both arcane and divine casting

## Variable Caster Level

You can cast a spell at a **lower caster level** than normal, but:
- The caster level you choose must be **high enough for you to cast the spell** in question
- **All level-dependent features** must be based on the same caster level

## Adjustments to Caster Level

In the event that a **class feature, domain granted power, or other special ability** provides an adjustment to your caster level, that adjustment applies to:
- **Effects based on caster level** (such as range, duration, and damage dealt)
- **Caster level check** to overcome your target's spell resistance
- **Caster level used in dispel checks** (both the dispel check and the DC of the check)

## Caster Level Checks

To make a **caster level check**:
1. **Roll**: 1d20 + your caster level (in the relevant class)
2. **Success**: If the result equals or exceeds the DC (or spell resistance)

### Common Uses
- **Overcoming spell resistance**: 1d20 + caster level vs. spell resistance
- **Dispel checks**: 1d20 + caster level vs. DC (11 + target's caster level)
- **Counterspell with dispel magic**: 1d20 + caster level vs. DC (11 + other caster's level)
- **Penetrating magical protections**: Various DCs depending on effect

## Special Situations

### Magic Items
- **Spell trigger items** (wands, staves): Use the **minimum caster level** needed to cast the spell
- **Spell completion items** (scrolls): Use the **caster level of the creator** or minimum required
- **Command word items**: Usually have a **set caster level** listed in the item description

### Spell-Like Abilities
- **Racial spell-like abilities**: Usually caster level equals **Hit Dice**
- **Class spell-like abilities**: Usually caster level equals **class level**
- **Monster spell-like abilities**: Caster level listed in the stat block

### Multiclassing Effects
- **Different spell types**: Arcane and divine caster levels are **separate**
- **Stacking**: Generally, caster levels from different classes do **not** stack
- **Prestige classes**: May advance existing caster levels or grant new ones

## Examples

### Range Calculation
**Fireball** has a range of **400 ft. + 40 ft./level**:
- **5th level caster**: 400 + (40 × 5) = 600 ft. range
- **10th level caster**: 400 + (40 × 10) = 800 ft. range

### Duration Calculation  
**Mage armor** has a duration of **1 hour/level**:
- **3rd level caster**: 3 hours duration
- **8th level caster**: 8 hours duration

### Damage Calculation
**Magic missile** deals **1d4+1 per missile, 1 missile per two caster levels** (max 5):
- **1st level caster**: 1 missile, 1d4+1 damage
- **5th level caster**: 3 missiles, 3d4+3 damage  
- **9th level caster**: 5 missiles, 5d4+5 damage
