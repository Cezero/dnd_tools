# Counterspells

It is possible to cast **any spell as a counterspell**. By doing so, you are using the spell's energy to **disrupt the casting of the same spell** by another character. 

**Counterspelling works** even if one spell is divine and the other arcane.

## How Counterspells Work

### Requirements
- You must **ready an action** to counterspell
- You must be able to **identify the spell** being cast
- You must have the **same spell prepared** (or be able to cast it spontaneously)

### Process
1. **Ready Action**: "I ready to counterspell if someone begins casting a spell"
2. **Identify Spell**: When an opponent begins casting, make a **Spellcraft check (DC 15 + spell level)** to identify the spell
3. **Cast Counterspell**: If you have the same spell available, you can cast it as a counterspell
4. **Resolution**: The original spell is negated

### Alternative: Dispel Magic
You can use **dispel magic** as a counterspell against any spell. This requires:
- **Caster level check**: 1d20 + your caster level vs. DC (11 + the other caster's level)
- **Success**: The spell is countered
- **Failure**: The spell takes effect normally

## Identifying Spells

To identify a spell being cast:
- **Spellcraft check**: DC 15 + spell level
- **Success**: You identify the spell and can attempt to counter it (if you have it available)
- **Failure**: You cannot attempt to counter this casting

### Automatic Identification
- If you have the **same spell prepared** and the caster is using **the same spell**, you automatically recognize it
- A **read magic** spell allows automatic identification of any spell being cast

## Counterspell Types

### Exact Counterspell
- Using the **exact same spell** to counter
- **Always successful** if you complete your casting
- Uses your prepared spell or spell slot

### Dispel Magic Counterspell  
- Using **dispel magic** to counter any spell
- Requires a **caster level check**
- More versatile but less reliable

### Opposed Schools
Some spells can be countered by spells from **opposing schools**:
- **Fireball** can be countered by **cold** spells of 3rd level or higher
- **Lightning bolt** can be countered by **earth** spells of 3rd level or higher
- Check individual spell descriptions for specific counterspell options

## Timing and Actions

### Ready Action Required
- You must **ready an action** to counterspell
- This uses your **action for the round**
- You cannot act normally and counterspell in the same round

### Timing Window
- You can only counterspell **during the casting** of the spell
- Once the spell is **complete**, it cannot be countered (but might be dispelled)
- **Instantaneous spells** are the most common targets

### Initiative Considerations
- **Higher initiative**: Can ready action and respond to later casters
- **Lower initiative**: Must ready on your turn and wait for next round

## Limitations

### Range
- Must be **within range** of the spell being cast
- Usually requires **line of sight** to the caster
- **Counterspell range** is typically the same as the spell's range

### Components
- Must be able to **complete the verbal, somatic, and material components** of your counterspell
- **Silence** prevents counterspelling spells with verbal components
- **Bound hands** prevent counterspelling spells with somatic components

### Spell Availability
- Must have the **exact spell available** (prepared or known)
- **Metamagic versions** count as different spells for counterspelling
- **Spell-like abilities** can sometimes be countered by the equivalent spell

## Metamagic and Counterspells

### Metamagic Effects
- A **metamagic spell** can be countered by the **base spell**
- The **base spell** cannot necessarily counter a **metamagic version**
- Example: **Enlarged fireball** can be countered by **fireball**, but **fireball** cannot counter **enlarged fireball**

### Spontaneous Metamagic
- **Sorcerers** applying metamagic spontaneously take longer to cast
- This provides **more time** for opponents to ready counterspells
- **Prepared metamagic** (wizards) casts at normal speed
