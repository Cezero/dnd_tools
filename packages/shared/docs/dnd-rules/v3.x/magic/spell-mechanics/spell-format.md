# Spell Description Format

The description of each spell is presented in a **standard format**. Each category of information is explained and defined below.

## Standard Spell Format

### Name
The **first line** of every spell description gives the **name by which the spell is generally known**.

#### Naming Conventions
- **Common names** used by most spellcasters
- **Alternate names** may exist in different cultures
- **Creator names** sometimes included in formal titles
- **Descriptive names** indicating primary effect

### School (Subschool)
Beneath the spell name is a line giving the **school of magic** (and the **subschool**, if appropriate) that the spell belongs to.

#### Eight Schools of Magic
Almost every spell belongs to one of **eight schools of magic**. A school of magic is a **group of related spells** that work in similar ways.

| **School** | **Primary Focus** |
|------------|------------------|
| **Abjuration** | Protection and barriers |
| **Conjuration** | Bringing things into being |
| **Divination** | Information gathering |
| **Enchantment** | Mind affecting |
| **Evocation** | Energy and force |
| **Illusion** | Deception and misdirection |
| **Necromancy** | Death and undeath |
| **Transmutation** | Changing properties |

#### Universal Spells
A **small number of spells** are **universal**, belonging to no school:
- *Arcane mark*
- *Limited wish*
- *Permanency*
- *Prestidigitation*
- *Wish*

### Level and Class
Indicates which **spellcasting classes** can cast the spell and at what **level**.

#### Class Designations
- **Brd** — Bard
- **Clr** — Cleric
- **Drd** — Druid
- **Pal** — Paladin
- **Rgr** — Ranger
- **Sor/Wiz** — Sorcerer/Wizard

#### Level Ranges
- **Level 0** — Cantrips and orisons
- **Levels 1-9** — Standard spell progression
- **Level progression** varies by class

### Components
Lists the **requirements** to cast the spell.

#### Component Types
- **V (Verbal)** — Spoken incantations
- **S (Somatic)** — Gestures and hand movements
- **M (Material)** — Physical components consumed
- **F (Focus)** — Reusable magical focus items
- **DF (Divine Focus)** — Holy/unholy symbols
- **XP** — Experience point costs

### Casting Time
How long it takes to **complete the spell**.

#### Common Casting Times
- **Standard action** — Most spells
- **Full-round action** — Complex spells
- **1 minute** — Ritual-like casting
- **Longer periods** — Major magical workings

### Range
How far the spell can **reach from the caster**.

#### Range Categories
- **Personal** — Affects only the caster
- **Touch** — Must touch the target
- **Close** — 25 ft + 5 ft/2 levels
- **Medium** — 100 ft + 10 ft/level
- **Long** — 400 ft + 40 ft/level
- **Unlimited** — No distance limit

### Target/Area/Effect
What the spell **affects** or **creates**.

#### Target Types
- **Single creature** — One specific target
- **Multiple creatures** — Several individual targets
- **Area effects** — Affects a defined area
- **Creation effects** — Brings something into being

### Duration
How long the **spell's effects last**.

#### Duration Types
- **Instantaneous** — Effect happens immediately and permanently
- **Concentration** — Lasts while caster concentrates
- **Rounds/minutes/hours** — Specific time periods
- **Permanent** — Lasts until dispelled or destroyed
- **Dismissible** — Caster can end early

### Saving Throw
Whether targets can **resist the spell's effects**.

#### Save Types
- **Fortitude** — Physical resistance
- **Reflex** — Evasion and agility
- **Will** — Mental resistance
- **None** — No save allowed

#### Save Results
- **Negates** — Successful save prevents all effects
- **Half** — Reduces damage by half
- **Partial** — Reduces some effects
- **Special** — Unique save mechanics

### Spell Resistance
Whether **spell resistance** can **protect against the spell**.

#### Spell Resistance Effects
- **Yes** — Spell resistance applies normally
- **No** — Spell resistance has no effect
- **Special** — Unique spell resistance interactions

## Spell Description Components

### Descriptive Text
The main body explains:
- **What the spell does** in detail
- **How the spell works** mechanically
- **Special conditions** or limitations
- **Interaction with other effects**

### Variable Effects
Many spells have effects that change based on:
- **Caster level** — Higher level = stronger effects
- **Target properties** — Size, type, HD
- **Environmental factors** — Terrain, planar effects
- **Material component quality** — Better materials = better results

### Spell Interaction
How spells work with:
- **Other spells** of the same type
- **Magic items** and equipment
- **Special abilities** and class features
- **Environmental conditions**
