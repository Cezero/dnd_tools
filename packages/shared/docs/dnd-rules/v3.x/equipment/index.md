## Quick Reference

### Weapon Categories

| **Category** | **Proficiency** | **Examples** |
|--------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Simple Melee** | Simple | Dagger, club, quarterstaff |
| **Simple Ranged** | Simple | Light crossbow, dart, sling |
| **Martial Melee** | Martial | Longsword, battleaxe, halberd |
| **Martial Ranged** | Martial | Longbow, heavy crossbow |
| **Exotic** | Exotic feat required | Bastard sword, whip, spiked chain |

### Armor Types

| **Armor Type** | **Max Dex** | **Check Penalty** | **Spell Failure** | **Speed 30ft** | **Speed 20ft** |
|----------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------|----------------|
| **Light Armor** | +6 to +8 | 0 to -2 | 5% to 15% | 30 ft | 20 ft |
| **Medium Armor** | +2 to +5 | -2 to -5 | 15% to 25% | 20 ft | 15 ft |
| **Heavy Armor** | +0 to +3 | -5 to -8 | 25% to 40% | 20 ft | 15 ft |

### Weapon Properties

| **Property** | **Effect** |
|--------------|------------|
| **Reach** | Attack targets 10 ft away, can't attack adjacent |
| **Trip** | Can be used to trip opponents |
| **Disarm** | +2 bonus on disarm attempts |
| **Double** | Two-weapon fighting with one weapon |
| **Thrown** | Can be used as ranged weapon |
| **Finesse** | Use Dex instead of Str for attack rolls |

### Special Materials

| **Material** | **Benefit** | **Cost Modifier** |
|--------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Adamantine** | DR bypass, hardness 20 | +3,000 gp |
| **Mithral** | -50% armor check penalty, -10% spell failure | +1,000 gp/lb |
| **Cold Iron** | Bypass some DR | +2,000 gp |
| **Silver** | Bypass some DR | +90 gp |
| **Darkwood** | Half weight | +10 gp/lb |

### Currency Exchange

| **Coin** | **Value** | **Weight** |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Copper (cp)** | 1 cp | 50 coins = 1 lb |
| **Silver (sp)** | 10 cp | 50 coins = 1 lb |
| **Gold (gp)** | 100 cp | 50 coins = 1 lb |
| **Platinum (pp)** | 1,000 cp | 50 coins = 1 lb |

### Weapon Size Categories

| **Character Size** | **Weapon Size** | **Damage Modifier** |
|-------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **Small** | Small weapons | Normal damage |
| **Small** | Medium weapons | -2 attack, two-handed |
| **Medium** | Small weapons | -2 damage (min 1) |
| **Medium** | Medium weapons | Normal damage |
| **Medium** | Large weapons | -2 attack, two-handed |

### Nonproficiency Penalties

| **Weapon Type** | **Attack Penalty** |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Simple weapons** | -4 |
| **Martial weapons** | -4 |
| **Exotic weapons** | -4 |
| **Armor** | Check penalty applies to attack rolls |
