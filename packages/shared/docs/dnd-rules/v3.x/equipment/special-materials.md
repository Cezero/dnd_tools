# Special Materials

In addition to magic items created with spells, some substances have **innate special properties**.

## Multiple Materials Rule

- If you make a suit of armor or weapon out of **more than one special material**, you get the benefit of only the **most prevalent material**
- However, you can build a **double weapon** with each head made of a **different special material**

## Special Weapons Materials Overview

Each of the special materials described below has a **definite game effect**. Some creatures have **damage reduction** based on their creature type or core concept. Some are resistant to all but a special type of damage, such as that dealt by **evil-aligned weapons** or **bludgeoning weapons**. Others are **vulnerable to weapons of a particular material**. 

Characters may choose to carry **several different types of weapons**, depending upon the campaign and types of creatures they most commonly encounter.

## Adamantine

### Cost Modifiers

| **Type of Adamantine Item** | **Item Cost Modifier** |
|----------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Ammunition** | +60 gp |
| **Light armor** | +5,000 gp |
| **Medium armor** | +10,000 gp |
| **Heavy armor** | +15,000 gp |
| **Weapon** | +3,000 gp |
| **Shield** | +2,000 gp |

### Properties

This **ultrahard metal** adds to the quality of a weapon or suit of armor.

#### Weapon Properties
- Weapons fashioned from adamantine have a **natural ability to bypass hardness** when sundering weapons or attacking objects
- **Ignoring hardness less than 20**

#### Armor Properties
- Armor made from adamantine grants its wearer **damage reduction**:
  - **1/-** if it's light armor
  - **2/-** if it's medium armor
  - **3/-** if it's heavy armor

#### Masterwork Quality
- Adamantine is so costly that weapons and armor made from it are **always of masterwork quality**
- The **masterwork cost is included** in the prices given above
- Thus, adamantine weapons and ammunition have a **+1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls**
- The **armor check penalty** of adamantine armor is **lessened by 1** compared to ordinary armor of its type

#### Restrictions
- **Items without metal parts cannot be made** from adamantine
- An **arrow could be made** of adamantine, but a **quarterstaff could not**
- Only **weapons, armor, and shields normally made of metal** can be fashioned from adamantine

#### Durability
- Weapons, armor and shields normally made of steel that are made of adamantine have **one-third more hit points** than normal
- **Adamantine has 40 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 20**

## Darkwood

### Properties

This **rare magic wood** is as hard as normal wood but **very light**.

#### Wooden Items
- Any **wooden or mostly wooden item** (such as a bow, an arrow, or a spear) made from darkwood:
  - Is considered a **masterwork item**
  - **Weighs only half as much** as a normal wooden item of that type

#### Restrictions
- Items **not normally made of wood** or **only partially of wood** (such as a battleaxe or a mace) either:
  - **Cannot be made from darkwood**, or
  - **Do not gain any special benefit** from being made of darkwood

#### Shield Benefits
- The **armor check penalty** of a darkwood shield is **lessened by 2** compared to an ordinary shield of its type

#### Pricing
- To determine the price of a darkwood item:
  - Use the **original weight**
  - Add **10 gp per pound** to the price of a masterwork version of that item

#### Durability
- **Darkwood has 10 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 5**

## Dragonhide

### Crafting

**Armorsmiths** can work with the hides of dragons to produce **armor or shields of masterwork quality**.

#### Hide Yield by Size Difference

| **Size Difference** | **Armor Type Available** | **Additional Shield** |
|-------------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| **1 size smaller** | 1 suit of masterwork **hide armor** | Small or large masterwork shield¹ |
| **2 sizes smaller** | 1 suit of masterwork **banded mail** | Small or large masterwork shield¹ |
| **3 sizes smaller** | 1 suit of masterwork **half-plate** | Small or large masterwork shield¹ |
| **4 sizes smaller** | 1 masterwork **breastplate** or **full plate** | Small or large masterwork shield¹ |

¹ *Provided that the dragon is Large or larger*

### Special Properties

#### Druid Compatibility
- Because dragonhide armor **isn't made of metal**, **druids can wear it without penalty**

#### Cost and Crafting Time
- Dragonhide armor costs **double what masterwork armor** of that type ordinarily costs
- It takes **no longer to make** than ordinary armor of that type

#### Durability
- **Dragonhide has 10 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 10**

## Cold Iron

### Properties

This **iron, mined deep underground**, known for its **effectiveness against fey creatures**, is forged at a **lower temperature** to preserve its delicate properties.

### Cost
- **Weapons made of cold iron cost twice as much** to make as their normal counterparts
- Any **magical enhancements cost an additional 2,000 gp**

### Restrictions
- **Items without metal parts cannot be made** from cold iron
- An **arrow could be made** of cold iron, but a **quarterstaff could not**

### Double Weapons
- A **double weapon that has only half** of it made of cold iron **increases its cost by 50%**

### Durability
- **Cold iron has 30 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 10**

## Mithral

### Cost Modifiers

| **Type of Mithral Item** | **Item Cost Modifier** |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| **Light armor** | +1,000 gp |
| **Medium armor** | +4,000 gp |
| **Heavy armor** | +9,000 gp |
| **Shield** | +1,000 gp |
| **Other items** | +500 gp/lb. |

### Properties

**Mithral** is a **very rare silvery, glistening metal** that is **lighter than iron but just as hard**. When worked like steel, it becomes a **wonderful material** from which to create armor and is occasionally used for other items as well.

#### Armor Category Changes
- **Most mithral armors are one category lighter** than normal for purposes of movement and other limitations:
  - **Heavy armors are treated as medium**
  - **Medium armors are treated as light**
  - **Light armors are still treated as light**

#### Armor Benefits
- **Spell failure chances** for armors and shields made from mithral are **decreased by 10%**
- **Maximum Dexterity bonus is increased by 2**
- **Armor check penalties are lessened by 3** (to a minimum of 0)

#### Weight Reduction
- An item made from mithral **weighs half as much** as the same item made from other metals
- In the case of weapons, this lighter weight **does not change a weapon's size category** or the ease with which it can be wielded (whether it is light, one-handed, or two-handed)
- **Items not primarily of metal** are **not meaningfully affected** by being partially made of mithral
  - Example: A **longsword can be a mithral weapon**, while a **scythe cannot be**

#### Masterwork Quality
- **Weapons or armors fashioned from mithral are always masterwork items** as well
- The **masterwork cost is included** in the prices given above

#### Durability
- **Mithral has 30 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 15**

## Alchemical Silver

### Cost Modifiers

| **Type of Alchemical Silver Item** | **Item Cost Modifier** |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Ammunition** | +2 gp |
| **Light weapon** | +20 gp |
| **One-handed weapon, or one head of a double weapon** | +90 gp |
| **Two-handed weapon, or both heads of a double weapon** | +180 gp |

### Properties

A **complex process involving metallurgy and alchemy** can bond silver to a weapon made of steel so that it **bypasses the damage reduction** of creatures such as **lycanthropes**.

#### Combat Effects
- On a successful attack with a silvered weapon, the wielder takes a **-1 penalty on the damage roll** (with the usual minimum of 1 point of damage)

#### Restrictions
- The alchemical silvering process **can't be applied to nonmetal items**
- It **doesn't work on rare metals** such as **adamantine, cold iron, and mithral**

#### Durability
- **Alchemical silver has 10 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 8**
