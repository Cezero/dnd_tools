# Dungeon Walls and Floors

Dungeon terrain forms the fundamental structure of underground environments, affecting movement, combat, and exploration.

## Walls Overview

Dungeon walls can be **masonry** (stones piled and mortared), **hewn from solid rock** (rough, chiseled appearance), or **smooth natural stone** from caves. Dungeon walls are **difficult to break down or through**, but they're **generally easy to climb**.

## Standard Wall Types

### Masonry Walls
**Most common** kind of dungeon wall, usually **at least 1 foot thick**.

#### Characteristics
- **Construction**: Fitted stones, usually mortared
- **Age effects**: Often sport cracks and crevices
- **Inhabitants**: Dangerous slimes or small monsters in cracks
- **Sound blocking**: Stop all but the loudest noises
- **Climb difficulty**: DC 20 Climb check

#### Tactical Considerations
- **Cover**: Provides standard cover in combat
- **Hiding places**: Cracks can conceal small creatures
- **Structural integrity**: Generally stable but may have weak points

### Superior Masonry Walls
**Better built** than standard masonry walls with improved construction.

#### Features
- **Construction**: Smoother, tighter-fitting stones, less cracking
- **Covering**: Often covered with plaster or stucco
- **Decoration**: May bear paintings, carved reliefs, or other ornamentation
- **Durability**: No more difficult to destroy than regular masonry
- **Climb difficulty**: More difficult to climb (DC 25)

#### Uses
- **Important areas**: Throne rooms, temples, noble quarters
- **Defensive positions**: Better appearance and harder to scale
- **Cultural significance**: Often decorated with historical scenes

### Hewn Stone Walls
Result when **chambers or passages are tunneled** out from solid rock.

#### Characteristics
- **Surface**: Rough texture with tool marks visible
- **Ledges**: Minuscule ledges where fungus grows
- **Fissures**: Cracks housing vermin, bats, subterranean snakes
- **Thickness**: At least 3 feet when separating chambers
- **Climb difficulty**: DC 25 Climb check

#### Natural Elements
- **Fungal growth**: Common in damp areas
- **Small creature habitats**: Various crevice-dwelling species
- **Water seepage**: May have damp or wet patches

### Unworked Stone Walls
**Natural cave walls** with uneven, rarely flat surfaces.

#### Properties
- **Texture**: Smooth to touch but filled with tiny holes
- **Features**: Hidden alcoves and ledges at various heights
- **Moisture**: Usually wet or damp from water action
- **Thickness**: At least 5 feet when separating areas
- **Climb difficulty**: DC 15 Climb check (easiest to climb)

#### Formation
- **Water carved**: Most frequently created by water action
- **Natural processes**: Geological formation over time
- **Irregular shapes**: No predictable pattern or structure

## Special Wall Types

### Reinforced Walls
**Masonry walls strengthened** with iron bars.

#### Construction
- **Iron placement**: Bars on one or both sides, or within the wall
- **Enhanced durability**: Hit points doubled
- **Increased resistance**: Break DC increased by +10
- **Same hardness**: Hardness remains unchanged

### Iron Walls
**Metal barriers** placed around important areas.

#### Usage
- **High security**: Vaults, treasure rooms, prisons
- **Thin but strong**: Only 3 inches thick but very durable
- **Expensive construction**: Usually limited to critical areas

### Paper Walls
**Screens** designed to block line of sight only.

#### Purpose
- **Visual barrier**: Block sight but not movement or sound
- **Temporary division**: Easy to install and remove
- **Minimal resistance**: Extremely easy to break through

### Wooden Walls
**Recent additions** to older dungeons.

#### Common Uses
- **Animal pens**: Containing livestock or monsters
- **Storage divisions**: Creating smaller storage areas
- **Room subdivision**: Making multiple rooms from larger spaces
- **Temporary construction**: Often not part of original design

### Magically Treated Walls
**Enhanced walls** with magical reinforcement.

#### Magical Effects
- **Enhanced durability**: +20 to break DC, ×2 hardness and hit points
- **Spell resistance**: Saving throw vs spells (2 + ½ caster level)
- **Cost**: 1,500 gp per 10×10 foot section
- **Creation requirements**: Craft Wondrous Item feat

### Walls with Arrow Slits
**Defensive walls** allowing ranged attacks while providing protection.

#### Construction Materials
- **Any durable material**: Masonry, hewn stone, or wood most common
- **Strategic placement**: Positioned to cover important areas

#### Combat Benefits for Defenders
- **Improved cover**: +8 bonus to Armor Class
- **Enhanced saves**: +4 bonus on Reflex saves
- **Evasion benefits**: Improved evasion class feature benefits

## Floor Types

### Flagstone
**Most common** dungeon floor type.

#### Construction
- **Fitted stones**: Like masonry walls but horizontal
- **Condition**: Usually cracked and only somewhat level
- **Growth**: Slime and mold in cracks
- **Water**: Rivulets between stones or stagnant puddles

#### Movement Effects
- **Standard movement**: No movement penalties normally
- **Slippery areas**: Water and slime may create hazards
- **Uneven sections**: May require balance checks

### Uneven Flagstone
**Flagstone in poor condition** with significant irregularities.

#### Characteristics
- **Surface quality**: More cracked and uneven than standard
- **Movement penalty**: May slow movement in some areas
- **Trip hazards**: Loose or protruding stones
- **Maintenance**: Shows signs of neglect

### Hewn Stone Floors
**Carved from solid rock** during tunnel construction.

#### Properties
- **Rough texture**: Tool marks and uneven surface
- **Drainage**: May have carved channels
- **Durability**: Very resistant to damage
- **Traction**: Usually good footing despite roughness

### Rubble Floors

#### Light Rubble
- **Composition**: Small stones and debris
- **Movement**: Normal speed but unstable footing
- **Sound**: May make noise when stepped on

#### Dense Rubble
- **Composition**: Larger stones and extensive debris
- **Movement**: Reduced movement speed
- **Balance**: Requires more careful footing
- **Concealment**: Can hide small objects or creatures

### Smooth Stone Floors
**Polished or naturally smooth** stone surfaces.

#### Characteristics
- **Surface**: Very even and smooth
- **Traction**: May be slippery when wet
- **Appearance**: Often indicates important areas
- **Maintenance**: Requires care to maintain smoothness

### Natural Stone Floors
**Unmodified cave floors** with natural formations.

#### Features
- **Irregular surface**: Natural contours and formations
- **Water features**: Pools, streams, wet areas
- **Varied levels**: Natural steps and slopes
- **Organic matter**: May have soil, guano, or organic debris

## Special Floor Features

### Slippery Floors
- **Causes**: Water, ice, oil, or slime
- **Effect**: Balance checks required for normal movement
- **Combat impact**: Penalties to movement and attacks

### Grated Floors
- **Construction**: Metal or stone grating over open space
- **Purpose**: Drainage, ventilation, or defensive
- **Vision**: Can see through to areas below
- **Small objects**: May fall through gaps

### Ledges
- **Formation**: Natural or carved elevated areas
- **Access**: Often require climbing to reach
- **Tactical value**: Provide elevation advantage
- **Width**: Varies from narrow to room-sized

### Transparent Floors
- **Material**: Magical force, crystal, or reinforced glass
- **Visibility**: Can see areas below
- **Psychology**: May cause fear of heights
- **Strength**: Varies by construction method

### Sliding Floors
- **Mechanism**: Mechanical or magical movement
- **Purpose**: Trap activation, secret passages, or transport
- **Control**: May be automatic or controlled
- **Speed**: Varies from slow drift to rapid movement

### Trap Floors
- **Hidden mechanisms**: Concealed pressure plates or triggers
- **Various effects**: Falling, spikes, gas, alarms
- **Detection**: Usually require careful searching
- **Bypass**: May have safe paths or disarm methods
