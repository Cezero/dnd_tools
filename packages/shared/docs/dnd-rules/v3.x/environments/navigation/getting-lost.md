# Getting Lost in the Wilderness

Navigation in the wilderness can be challenging, especially in poor conditions or difficult terrain.

## Conditions That Cause Getting Lost

### Safe Travel
**Following an obvious road, trail, or feature** such as a stream or shoreline **prevents any possibility** of becoming lost. However, travelers **striking off cross-country** may become disoriented.

### Poor Visibility
Any time characters **cannot see at least 60 feet** in the prevailing conditions of visibility, they may become lost. This includes:
- **Fog, snow, or downpour** — lose ability to see landmarks
- **Nighttime travel** — depends on light sources, moonlight, and vision types
- **Darkvision or low-light vision** may help in darkness

### Difficult Terrain
Any character in **forest, moor, hill, or mountain terrain** may become lost if moving away from obvious paths. **Forests are especially dangerous** because they:
- **Obscure far-off landmarks**
- **Make it hard to see** the sun or stars

## Getting Lost Table

| **Circumstance** | **Survival DC** |
|------------------|-----------------|
| **Moor or hill, with map** | 6 |
| **Mountain, with map** | 8 |
| **Moor or hill, no map** | 10 |
| **Poor visibility** | 12 |
| **Mountain, no map** | 12 |
| **Forest** | 15 |

## Navigation Mechanics

### Making the Check
- **Character leading the way** must succeed on a Survival check or become lost
- **Use the highest DC** that applies from the table above
- **+2 bonus** for 5+ ranks in Knowledge (geography) or Knowledge (local) for the area
- **Check once per hour** of local or overland movement
- **Only the leader** makes the check for a party moving together

### When Conditions Apply
If conditions exist that make getting lost a possibility, the navigation check is required. **Combine multiple risk factors** by using the highest applicable DC.

## Effects of Being Lost

### Random Movement
If a party becomes lost, it is **no longer certain** of moving in the intended direction:
- **Randomly determine direction** for each hour of movement
- **Movement continues to be random** until:
  - Blunder into an unmistakable landmark
  - Recognize they are lost and regain bearings

### Recognizing You're Lost
- **Once per hour** of random travel, each character may attempt a **Survival check**
- **DC 20, -1 per hour** of random travel
- **Some circumstances** may make it obvious that characters are lost

## Regaining Your Bearings

### Setting a New Course
A lost party must determine the correct direction to reach their objective:
- **Survival check** required (**DC 15, +2 per hour** of random travel)
- **Failure** means choosing a random direction as "correct"
- **Still subject to getting lost again** on the new course

### Conflicting Directions
When multiple characters attempt to determine direction:
- **Make Survival checks in secret** for each character
- **Tell successful characters** the correct direction
- **Tell failed characters** a random direction they think is right

### Ways to Become Un-Lost

| **Method** | **Requirements** |
|------------|------------------|
| **Reach destination** | Successfully follow correct course |
| **Find landmark** | Random movement into unmistakable feature |
| **Conditions improve** | Fog lifts, sun comes up (+4 Survival bonus) |
| **Magic** | Spells like *find the path* or divination |

## Navigation Tips

### Preparation
- **Obtain detailed maps** of the travel area
- **Study local geography** before departing
- **Note major landmarks** and their relative positions
- **Plan routes** along obvious features when possible

### Party Organization
- **Designate an experienced navigator** as leader
- **Have backup navigators** ready
- **Share navigation knowledge** among party members
- **Establish procedures** for when lost

### Environmental Awareness
- **Monitor weather conditions** for visibility changes
- **Track time of day** and celestial positions
- **Note wind direction** and other natural indicators
- **Maintain situational awareness** at all times
