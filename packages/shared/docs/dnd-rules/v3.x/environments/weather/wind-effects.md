# Wind Effects

The wind can create a stinging spray of sand or dust, fan a large fire, heel over a small boat, and blow gases or vapors away. If powerful enough, it can even knock characters down, interfere with ranged attacks, or impose penalties on some skill checks.

## Wind Categories

### Light Wind (0-10 mph)
A **gentle breeze**, having **little or no game effect**.

### Moderate Wind (11-20 mph)
A **steady wind** with a **50% chance** of extinguishing small, unprotected flames, such as **candles**.

### Strong Wind (21-30 mph)
**Gusts** that automatically **extinguish unprotected flames** (candles, torches, and the like). Such gusts impose:
- **-2 penalty** on ranged attack rolls
- **-2 penalty** on Listen checks

### Severe Wind (31-50 mph)
In addition to automatically **extinguishing any unprotected flames**, winds of this magnitude:
- **Protected flames**: Cause protected flames (such as lanterns) to **dance wildly** and have a **50% chance** of extinguishing these lights
- **Ranged attacks**: **-4 penalty** on ranged weapon attacks
- **Listen checks**: **-4 penalty** on Listen checks
- **Spell effect**: This is the velocity of wind produced by a **gust of wind** spell

### Windstorm (51-74 mph)
Powerful enough to **bring down branches if not whole trees**, windstorms:
- **Unprotected flames**: Automatically **extinguish**
- **Protected flames**: **75% chance** of blowing out protected flames (lanterns, etc.)
- **Ranged attacks**: Ranged weapon attacks are **impossible**
- **Siege weapons**: Even siege weapons have a **-4 penalty** on attack rolls
- **Listen checks**: **-8 penalty** due to the howling of the wind

### Hurricane-Force Wind (75-174 mph)
- **Flames**: **All flames are extinguished**
- **Ranged attacks**: Ranged attacks are **impossible** (except with siege weapons, which have a **-8 penalty**)
- **Listen checks**: Listen checks are **impossible** — all characters can hear is the roaring of the wind
- **Destruction**: Hurricane-force winds often **fell trees**

### Tornado (175-300 mph)
- **Flames**: **All flames are extinguished**
- **Ranged attacks**: **All ranged attacks are impossible** (even with siege weapons)
- **Listen checks**: **Impossible** — only the roaring of wind can be heard
- **Special suction effect**: See [Powerful Storms](powerful-storms.md) for tornado-specific rules

1. **Siege weapons**: The siege weapon category includes ballista and catapult attacks as well as boulders tossed by giants

## Wind Effect Definitions

### Checked
**Creatures are unable to move forward** against the force of the wind. **Flying creatures** are blown back **1d6×5 feet**.

### Knocked Down
**Creatures are knocked prone** by the force of the wind. **Flying creatures** are instead blown back **1d6×10 feet**.

### Blown Away
- **Ground creatures**: Knocked prone and rolled **1d4×10 feet**, taking **1d4 points of nonlethal damage per 10 feet**
- **Flying creatures**: Blown back **2d6×10 feet** and take **2d6 points of nonlethal damage** due to battering and buffeting

## Special Wind Situations

### Magical Wind
- **Spells**: Some spells create wind effects (gust of wind, control weather)
- **Magic items**: May generate constant or temporary wind effects
- **Planar effects**: Elemental Plane of Air may have constant strong winds

### Directional Effects
- **Headwind**: Moving against wind direction may impose additional movement penalties
- **Tailwind**: Moving with wind direction may provide movement bonuses
- **Crosswind**: May affect ranged attacks even more severely

### Environmental Combinations
- **Wind + Rain**: Creates additional visibility and movement penalties
- **Wind + Snow**: Can create blizzard conditions and snowdrifts
- **Wind + Sand/Dust**: Creates sandstorm/duststorm effects
- **Wind + Fire**: Can rapidly spread fires or create firestorms

### Sheltered Areas
- **Buildings**: Provide complete protection from wind effects
- **Natural windbreaks**: Trees, rocks, hills can reduce wind effects
- **Magical protection**: Spells like wind wall can block wind
- **Positioning**: Taking cover reduces exposure to wind effects
