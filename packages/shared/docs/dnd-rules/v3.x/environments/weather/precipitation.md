# Precipitation

Bad weather frequently **slows or halts travel** and makes it virtually impossible to navigate from one spot to another. Torrential downpours and blizzards **obscure vision as effectively as a dense fog**.

Most precipitation is **rain**, but in cold conditions it can manifest as **snow, sleet, or hail**. Precipitation of any kind followed by a **cold snap** in which the temperature dips from above freezing to **30° F or below** may produce **ice**.

## Precipitation Types

When **Precipitation** occurs, roll **d%** to determine the type:
- **01-30**: **Fog**
- **31-90**: **Rain/Snow** (snow only when temperature is 30° F or below)
- **91-00**: **Sleet/Hail** (only when temperature is 30° F or below)

**Most precipitation lasts for 2d4 hours**. By contrast, **hail lasts for only 1d20 minutes** but usually accompanies **1d4 hours of rain**.

## Rain

**Rain** has the following effects:
- **Visibility**: Reduces visibility ranges by **half**
- **Spot/Search**: **-4 penalty** on Spot and Search checks
- **Flames**: Same effect as **severe wind** (50% chance to extinguish protected flames)
- **Ranged attacks**: **-4 penalty** on ranged weapon attacks
- **Listen checks**: **-4 penalty** due to rainfall noise

## Snow

**Falling snow** has the same effects on visibility, ranged weapon attacks, and skill checks as **rain**, plus:
- **Movement**: Costs **2 squares of movement** to enter a snow-covered square
- **Accumulation**: A day of snowfall leaves **1d6 inches** of snow on the ground
- **Flames**: Same effect as **moderate wind** (50% chance to extinguish unprotected flames)

### Heavy Snow

**Heavy snow** has the same effects as normal snowfall, but also:
- **Visibility**: Restricts visibility as **fog** does (see [Wind Effects](wind-effects.md))
- **Movement**: Costs **4 squares of movement** to enter a square covered with heavy snow
- **Accumulation**: A day of heavy snow leaves **1d4 feet** of snow on the ground
- **Snowdrifts**: Heavy snow accompanied by strong or severe winds may result in snowdrifts **1d4×5 feet deep**, especially around objects big enough to deflect wind (cabins, large tents)
- **Lightning**: **10% chance** that heavy snowfall is accompanied by lightning (see [Storms](storms.md))

## Sleet

**Essentially frozen rain**, sleet has:
- **While falling**: Same effect as **rain** (except **75% chance** to extinguish protected flames)
- **Once on ground**: Same effect as **snow**

## Hail

**Hail** has unique effects:
- **Visibility**: Does **not** reduce visibility
- **Listen checks**: **-4 penalty** due to the sound of falling hail
- **Damage**: Sometimes (**5% chance**) hail becomes large enough to deal **1 point of lethal damage** (per storm) to anything in the open
- **Movement**: Once on the ground, has the same effect on movement as **snow**

## Special Precipitation

### Downpour

**Downpour** is treated as **rain** but:
- **Concealment**: **Conceals as fog**
- **Floods**: Can create floods (see [Environmental Hazards](../hazards/))
- **Duration**: Lasts for **2d4 hours**

### Ice Formation

Precipitation followed by a **cold snap** (temperature dropping to 30° F or below) may produce **ice**:
- **Movement**: Costs **2 squares of movement** to enter an ice-covered square
- **Balance/Tumble**: DC increases by **+5**
- **Duration**: Ice persists until temperature rises above freezing for extended period

## Fog

Whether in the form of a **low-lying cloud** or a **mist rising from the ground**, fog:
- **Visibility**: Obscures all sight, **including darkvision**, beyond **5 feet**
- **Concealment**: Creatures **5 feet away** have concealment (**20% miss chance** on attacks)
- **Complete obscurement**: Creatures **more than 5 feet away** have total concealment

### Fog Sources
- **Natural fog**: Forms in low-lying areas, near water
- **Weather fog**: Result of precipitation table
- **Magical fog**: Created by spells like **fog cloud**
- **Environmental fog**: Swamps, marshes, coastal areas

## Precipitation Effects Summary

| **Type** | **Visibility** | **Movement** | **Attacks** | **Listen** | **Flames** |
|----------|----------------|--------------|-------------|------------|------------|
| **Rain** | -50% range | Normal | -4 penalty | -4 penalty | Severe wind effect |
| **Snow** | -50% range | 2× cost | -4 penalty | -4 penalty | Moderate wind effect |
| **Heavy Snow** | Fog effect | 4× cost | -4 penalty | -4 penalty | Moderate wind effect |
| **Sleet** | -50% range | 2× cost (ground) | -4 penalty | -4 penalty | 75% protected flames |
| **Hail** | Normal | 2× cost (ground) | Normal | -4 penalty | Normal |
| **Fog** | 5 ft. max | Normal | 20% miss at 5 ft. | Normal | Normal |
