# Weather Basics

Sometimes weather can play an important role in an adventure. This section covers basic weather terms and random weather generation.

## Random Weather Table

| **d%** | **Weather** | **Cold Climate** | **Temperate Climate¹** | **Desert** |
|--------|-------------|------------------|------------------------|------------|
| **01-70** | Normal weather | Cold, calm | Normal for season² | Hot, calm |
| **71-80** | Abnormal weather | Heat wave (01-30) or cold snap (31-100) | Heat wave (01-50) or cold snap (51-100) | Hot, windy |
| **81-90** | Inclement weather | Precipitation (snow) | Precipitation (normal for season) | Hot, windy |
| **91-99** | Storm | Snowstorm | Thunderstorm, snowstorm³ | Duststorm |
| **100** | Powerful storm | Blizzard | Windstorm, blizzard⁴, hurricane, tornado | Downpour |

**Notes:**
1. **Temperate** includes forest, hills, marsh, mountains, plains, and warm aquatic
2. **Winter** is cold, **summer** is warm, **spring and autumn** are temperate  
3. **Marsh regions** are slightly warmer in winter

## Basic Weather Terms

### Wind Conditions

#### Calm
**Wind speeds** are light (**0 to 10 mph**).

#### Windy  
**Wind speeds** are moderate to strong (**10 to 30 mph**). See [Wind Effects](wind-effects.md) for details.

### Temperature Conditions

#### Cold
Between **0° and 40° Fahrenheit** during the day, **10 to 20 degrees colder** at night.

#### Moderate
Between **40° and 60° Fahrenheit** during the day, **10 to 20 degrees colder** at night.

#### Warm
Between **60° and 85° Fahrenheit** during the day, **10 to 20 degrees colder** at night.

#### Hot
Between **85° and 110° Fahrenheit** during the day, **10 to 20 degrees colder** at night.

### Temperature Variations

#### Cold Snap
**Lowers temperature by -10° F**.

#### Heat Wave
**Raises temperature by +10° F**.

## Weather Duration

Most weather conditions have specific durations:
- **Normal weather**: Continues until next weather check
- **Abnormal weather**: Typically lasts **1d4 days**
- **Inclement weather**: Usually lasts **2d4 hours**
- **Storms**: Duration varies by type (see [Storms](storms.md))
- **Powerful storms**: Duration varies greatly (see [Powerful Storms](powerful-storms.md))

## Local Weather Modifications

The **Random Weather Table** is appropriate for general use and can be used as a basis for local weather tables. Consider these factors when creating regional weather:

### Geographic Modifiers
- **Coastal areas**: More moderate temperatures, higher precipitation
- **Mountain regions**: Colder temperatures, more extreme weather
- **Desert areas**: Extreme temperature swings, low precipitation
- **Forest areas**: More moderate conditions, filtered wind

### Seasonal Adjustments
- **Spring**: Increased precipitation, moderate temperatures
- **Summer**: Higher temperatures, thunderstorms
- **Autumn**: Temperature drops, increased wind
- **Winter**: Cold temperatures, snow in cold climates

### Magical Weather
In fantasy settings, weather may be:
- **Magically controlled** by powerful spellcasters
- **Influenced by planar proximity** (Elemental Plane effects)
- **Created by magical items** or artifacts
- **Result of divine intervention** or curse effects
