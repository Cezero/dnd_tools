## Quick Reference

### Random Weather (d%)

| **Roll** | **Cold Climate** | **Temperate Climate** | **Desert** |
|----------|------------------|----------------------|------------|
| **01-70** | Cold, calm | Normal for season | Hot, calm |
| **71-80** | Heat wave/cold snap | Heat wave/cold snap | Hot, windy |
| **81-90** | Precipitation (snow) | Precipitation | Hot, windy |
| **91-99** | Snowstorm | Thunderstorm/snowstorm | Duststorm |
| **100** | Blizzard | Windstorm/blizzard/hurricane/tornado | Downpour |

## Weather Planning

### Adventure Considerations
- **Travel time**: Weather can significantly delay journeys
- **Visibility**: Affects stealth, tracking, and combat
- **Equipment**: Proper gear is essential for survival
- **Shelter**: Planning rest stops around weather

### Dramatic Opportunities
- **Chase scenes**: Wind and rain add tension
- **Combat encounters**: Weather creates tactical challenges
- **Survival scenarios**: Extreme weather tests character limits
- **Story moments**: Weather can enhance mood and atmosphere
