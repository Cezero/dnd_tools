# Storms

The combined effects of **precipitation (or dust) and wind** that accompany all storms create dangerous conditions for travelers and adventurers.

## General Storm Effects

All storms share these characteristics:
- **Visibility**: Reduce visibility ranges by **three-quarters**
- **Skill penalties**: **-8 penalty** on Spot, Search, and Listen checks
- **Ranged attacks**: Make ranged weapon attacks **impossible** (except siege weapons with **-4 penalty**)
- **Flames**: Automatically **extinguish candles, torches, and similar unprotected flames**
- **Protected flames**: **50% chance** to extinguish protected flames (lanterns, etc.)
- **Wind effects**: See [Wind Effects](wind-effects.md) for consequences to creatures without shelter
- **Duration**: Storms last for **2d4-1 hours**

## Storm Types

### Duststorm (CR 3)

**Desert storms** that differ from other storms by having **no precipitation**. Instead, they blow **fine grains of sand**.

#### Standard Duststorm
- **Wind**: Accompanied by **severe winds** (31-50 mph)
- **Sand obscurement**: Obscures vision like other storms
- **Flame effects**: **Smother unprotected flames** and can **choke protected flames** (**50% chance**)
- **Sand deposit**: Leave behind **1d6 inches of sand**

#### Greater Duststorm
- **Chance**: **10% chance** for a greater duststorm
- **Wind**: Accompanied by **windstorm-magnitude winds** (51-74 mph)
- **Damage**: Deal **1d3 points of nonlethal damage each round** to anyone caught in the open without shelter
- **Choking hazard**: Poses a choking hazard (see Drowning rules)
  - **Protection**: Character with scarf or similar protection across mouth and nose doesn't begin to choke until after **10 × Constitution score** rounds
- **Sand deposit**: Leave **2d3-1 feet of fine sand** in their wake

### Snowstorm

**Cold-weather storms** combining wind and heavy snowfall.

#### Effects
- **All standard storm effects** (visibility, skill penalties, ranged attacks, flames)
- **Snow accumulation**: Leave **1d6 inches of snow** on the ground afterward
- **Movement**: Snow-covered areas cost **extra movement** to traverse
- **Cold danger**: Combined with wind chill, can create **hypothermia conditions**

#### Blizzard Potential
- Some snowstorms can escalate to **blizzard** status (see [Powerful Storms](powerful-storms.md))
- **Temperature**: Often accompanied by **extreme cold**
- **Duration**: Standard duration (**2d4-1 hours**) unless it becomes a blizzard

### Thunderstorm

**Electrical storms** featuring rain, wind, and lightning.

#### Standard Effects
- **All standard storm effects**
- **Precipitation**: Usually **rain**, but sometimes also **hail**
- **Wind**: **Severe winds** (31-50 mph)

#### Lightning Hazard
- **Frequency**: Assume **one bolt per minute** for a **1-hour period** at the center of the storm
- **Damage**: Each bolt causes **1d10 eight-sided dice** of electricity damage
- **Targets**: Greatest hazard to characters without proper shelter, especially those in **metal armor**
- **Range**: Lightning can strike within the storm's area of effect

#### Special Conditions
- **Metal armor**: Characters wearing metal armor are **more likely** to be struck by lightning
- **High ground**: Characters on elevated terrain face **increased lightning risk**
- **Water proximity**: Standing in or near water **increases** lightning danger

#### Tornado Formation
- **Chance**: **One in ten thunderstorms** is accompanied by a **tornado** (see [Powerful Storms](powerful-storms.md))
- **Warning signs**: Rapid pressure changes, distinctive cloud formations
- **Formation time**: Tornadoes typically form during the **peak intensity** of the thunderstorm

## Storm Survival

### Seeking Shelter
- **Sturdy buildings**: Provide **complete protection** from storm effects
- **Natural shelter**: Caves, dense forest canopy offer **partial protection**
- **Makeshift shelter**: Reduce some effects but may not protect against all dangers
- **Open terrain**: Offers **no protection** and subjects characters to full storm effects

### Navigation During Storms
- **Visibility**: Severely reduced makes navigation **extremely difficult**
- **Landmarks**: Familiar landmarks become **impossible to see**
- **Getting lost**: High chance of becoming lost in storm conditions
- **Compass/magical navigation**: May be the only reliable navigation methods

### Equipment Considerations
- **Waterproofing**: Essential for protecting gear during storms
- **Wind resistance**: Lightweight items may be blown away
- **Flame protection**: Lanterns and torches need extra protection
- **Emergency supplies**: Extra food and water in case storm delays travel

## Storm Intensity Scaling

### Mild Storms
- **Reduced effects**: May have only **-4 penalties** instead of **-8**
- **Shorter duration**: **1d4 hours** instead of **2d4-1**
- **Less wind**: **Strong winds** (21-30 mph) instead of severe

### Severe Storms
- **Enhanced effects**: May impose **additional penalties**
- **Longer duration**: **1d4 days** for extended storm systems
- **Multiple types**: Storms may combine effects (thunderstorm with heavy snow)
