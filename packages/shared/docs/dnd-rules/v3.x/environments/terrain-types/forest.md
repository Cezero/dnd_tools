# Forest Terrain

Forest terrain provides diverse environments ranging from open woodlands to dense jungles, each with unique challenges and opportunities.

## Forest Categories

Forest terrain can be divided into **three categories**: sparse, medium, and dense. An immense forest could have all three categories within its borders, with more **sparse terrain at the outer edge** and **dense forest at its heart**.

### Forest Terrain Features

| **Terrain Feature** | **Sparse** | **Medium** | **Dense** |
|-------------------|------------|------------|-----------|
| **Typical trees** | 50% | 70% | 80% |
| **Massive trees** | — | 10% | 20% |
| **Light undergrowth** | 50% | 70% | 50% |
| **Heavy undergrowth** | — | 20% | 50% |

## Forest Elements

### Trees

The **most important terrain element** in a forest is the trees. Trees provide tactical advantages and obstacles:

#### Typical Trees
- **+2 bonus to Armor Class** and **+1 bonus on Reflex saves** for creatures in the same square
- **Bonuses don't stack** with cover bonuses from other sources
- **Doesn't affect fighting space** — creature uses tree to advantage
- **Trunk statistics**: AC 4, hardness 5, 150 hp
- **Climbing**: DC 15 Climb check

#### Massive Trees
Found in **medium and dense forests**:
- **Take up an entire square** and provide cover to anyone behind them
- **Statistics**: AC 3, hardness 5, 600 hp
- **Climbing**: DC 15 Climb check
- **Full cover** for creatures behind them

### Undergrowth

Vines, roots, and short bushes cover much of the forest ground:

#### Light Undergrowth
- **Movement cost**: 2 squares of movement to enter
- **Provides concealment** (20% miss chance)
- **Skill penalties**: +2 DC to Tumble and Move Silently checks
- **Reason**: Leaves and branches get in the way

#### Heavy Undergrowth
- **Movement cost**: 4 squares of movement to enter
- **Enhanced concealment**: 30% miss chance (instead of usual 20%)
- **Skill effects**:
  - **+5 DC to Tumble and Move Silently checks**
  - **+5 circumstance bonus on Hide checks**
- **Movement restrictions**: **Running and charging impossible**
- **Distribution**: Squares often clustered together

#### Undergrowth Notes
- **Undergrowth and trees aren't mutually exclusive** — common for a square to have both
- **Easy to hide** in heavy undergrowth areas

### Forest Canopy

**Elevated platforms and treehouses** are common in forests:

#### Platform Access
- **Climb tree branches**: DC 15 Climb check
- **Rope ladders**: DC 0 Climb check  
- **Pulley elevators**: Strength check each round (full-round action) to rise

#### Combat Advantages
- **Cover** when fighting creatures on the ground
- **Concealment** in medium or dense forests when fighting ground creatures
- **Rope bridges** typically connect platforms

### Other Forest Elements

#### Fallen Logs
- **Height**: Generally 3 feet tall
- **Cover**: Functions as low walls
- **Movement cost**: 5 feet to cross

#### Forest Streams  
- **Width**: 5 to 10 feet wide
- **Depth**: No more than 5 feet deep
- **Crossing**: Usually fordable

#### Pathways
- **Movement**: Allow normal movement
- **Cover/Concealment**: Provide neither
- **Distribution**: 
  - **Common** in sparse and medium forests
  - **Less common** in dense forests
  - **Game trails** found even in unexplored forests

## Stealth and Detection

### Visibility Ranges

| **Forest Type** | **Maximum Spot Distance** |
|-----------------|---------------------------|
| **Sparse** | 3d6 × 10 feet |
| **Medium** | 2d8 × 10 feet |
| **Dense** | 2d6 × 10 feet |

### Hiding Opportunities
- **Undergrowth squares** provide concealment for Hide skill use
- **Logs and massive trees** provide cover for hiding
- **Usually easy** to use Hide skill in forests

### Sound and Listening
- **Background noise** makes Listen checks more difficult
- **Increased DC**: +2 per 10 feet (instead of normal +1)
- **Move Silently** also more difficult in undergrowth

## Forest Categories Detailed

### Sparse Forest
- **Typical trees**: 50% of squares
- **Light undergrowth**: 50% of squares  
- **No massive trees** or heavy undergrowth
- **Best visibility**: 3d6 × 10 feet detection range
- **Easiest movement** through forest terrain

### Medium Forest
- **Typical trees**: 70% of squares
- **Massive trees**: 10% of squares
- **Light undergrowth**: 70% of squares
- **Heavy undergrowth**: 20% of squares
- **Moderate visibility**: 2d8 × 10 feet detection range
- **Balanced** between accessibility and cover

### Dense Forest  
- **Typical trees**: 80% of squares
- **Massive trees**: 20% of squares
- **Light undergrowth**: 50% of squares
- **Heavy undergrowth**: 50% of squares
- **Limited visibility**: 2d6 × 10 feet detection range
- **Difficult movement** but excellent concealment

## Tactical Considerations

### Advantages
- **Abundant cover and concealment** for ambushes
- **Multiple elevation levels** via canopy platforms
- **Natural barriers** from undergrowth and trees
- **Hide skill bonuses** from terrain features

### Challenges
- **Restricted movement** in heavy undergrowth
- **Limited visibility** for ranged attacks
- **Getting lost** more likely (DC 15 Survival)
- **Difficult spellcasting** with somatic components in tight spaces

### Navigation Tips
- **Follow streams** or obvious paths when possible
- **Use canopy** for better overview of area
- **Mark trails** when exploring new areas
- **Travel during daylight** when possible
