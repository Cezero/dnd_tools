# Marsh Terrain

Marsh terrain includes wetlands ranging from relatively dry moors to waterlogged swamps, creating unique movement and combat challenges.

## Marsh Categories

**Two categories** of marsh exist:
- **Moors** — Relatively dry marshlands
- **Swamps** — Watery marshlands

Both are often **bordered by lakes**, which effectively serve as a third category of terrain found in marshes.

### Marsh Terrain Features

| **Terrain Feature** | **Moor** | **Swamp** |
|-------------------|----------|-----------|
| **Shallow bog** | 20% | 40% |
| **Deep bog** | 5% | 20% |
| **Light undergrowth** | 30% | 20% |
| **Heavy undergrowth** | 10% | 20% |

## Bog Features

### Shallow Bog
**Depth**: About 1 foot of deep mud or standing water

#### Movement Effects
- **Movement cost**: 2 squares of movement to enter
- **Tumble penalty**: +2 DC increase
- **Move Silently penalty**: +2 DC increase

#### Tactical Properties
- **Provides difficult terrain** for all creatures
- **Slows pursuit** and retreat
- **Makes stealth harder** due to splashing

### Deep Bog
**Depth**: Roughly 4 feet of standing water

#### Movement Effects
- **Medium+ creatures**: 4 squares of movement to enter, or swim
- **Small or smaller creatures**: Must swim to move through
- **Tumbling**: Impossible in deep bog
- **Move Silently penalty**: +2 DC increase

#### Cover Benefits
- **Medium+ creatures**: Standard cover from water
- **Small or smaller creatures**: Improved cover (+8 AC, +4 Reflex saves)
- **Crouching**: Medium+ creatures can crouch (move action) for improved cover
- **Attack penalty**: -10 penalty on attacks against non-underwater creatures

#### Distribution
- **Deep bog squares** usually clustered together
- **Surrounded by irregular ring** of shallow bog squares

## Other Marsh Features

### Undergrowth
The bushes, rushes, and tall grasses in marshes **function as forest undergrowth**:
- **Light undergrowth**: 2 squares movement, concealment, +2 DC penalties
- **Heavy undergrowth**: 4 squares movement, 30% miss chance, +5 DC penalties
- **Note**: Bog squares **do not also have undergrowth**

### Quicksand
**Deceptively solid appearance** that may trap careless characters.

#### Detection
- **Normal pace**: DC 8 Survival check to spot danger before stepping in
- **Charging/running**: No chance to detect before blundering in
- **Typical size**: 20 feet in diameter
- **Momentum**: Charging/running carries character 1d2 × 5 feet into quicksand

#### Effects of Quicksand
- **Tread water**: DC 10 Swim check each round to stay in place
- **Move**: DC 15 Swim check to move 5 feet in desired direction
- **Sinking**: Failing by 5+ means sinking below surface and drowning
- **Surface return**: DC 15 Swim check (+1 per consecutive round underwater)

#### Rescue from Quicksand
**Equipment needed**: Branch, spear haft, rope, or similar reaching tool

**Rescue process**:
1. **Rescuer**: DC 15 Strength check to pull victim
2. **Victim**: DC 10 Strength check to hold onto tool
3. **Failure consequences**:
   - **Victim fails**: DC 15 Swim check immediately to stay above surface
   - **Success**: Victim pulled 5 feet closer to safety

### Hedgerows
**Common in moors** — tangles of stones, soil, and thorny bushes.

#### Narrow Hedgerows
- **Function**: As low walls
- **Movement cost**: 15 feet of movement to cross
- **Height**: Under 5 feet

#### Wide Hedgerows  
- **Height**: More than 5 feet tall
- **Size**: Take up entire squares
- **Cover**: Provide total cover (like walls)
- **Movement**: 4 squares of movement to move through
- **Climbing**: DC 10 Climb check reduces to 2 squares movement

### Other Elements
- **Trees**: Some marshes (particularly swamps) have trees like forests
- **Tree distribution**: Usually clustered in small stands
- **Paths**: Lead across many marshes, winding to avoid bog areas
- **Path benefits**: Allow normal movement, don't provide concealment

## Stealth and Detection

### Visibility Ranges

| **Marsh Type** | **Maximum Spot Distance** |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| **Moor** | 6d6 × 10 feet |
| **Swamp** | 2d8 × 10 feet |

### Concealment Opportunities
- **Undergrowth** provides plentiful concealment
- **Deep bogs** provide cover for smaller creatures
- **Easy to hide** due to varied terrain features

### Sound Mechanics
- **No Listen penalties** from marsh terrain itself
- **Move Silently more difficult** in undergrowth and bogs
- **Splashing sounds** in bog areas can give away position

## Marsh Categories Detailed

### Moor
- **Characteristics**: Relatively dry marshland
- **Shallow bogs**: 20% of squares
- **Deep bogs**: 5% of squares  
- **Undergrowth**: 30% light, 10% heavy
- **Visibility**: Excellent (6d6 × 10 feet)
- **Movement**: Generally easier than swamps

### Swamp
- **Characteristics**: Watery marshland
- **Shallow bogs**: 40% of squares
- **Deep bogs**: 20% of squares
- **Undergrowth**: 20% light, 20% heavy
- **Visibility**: Limited (2d8 × 10 feet)
- **Movement**: Significantly more difficult

## Tactical Considerations

### Advantages
- **Natural difficult terrain** slows enemies
- **Cover from bogs** for smaller creatures
- **Concealment** from undergrowth abundant
- **Channeling effect** from bog placement

### Challenges
- **Slow movement** through bog areas
- **Quicksand hazards** can trap characters
- **Swimming requirements** in deep areas
- **Getting lost** possible (see Navigation)

### Combat Tips
- **Use bog cover** for ranged attacks
- **Force enemies** through difficult terrain
- **Be aware of quicksand** locations
- **Exploit size advantages** in deep bogs

### Environmental Hazards
- **Disease** from stagnant water
- **Insect swarms** in warm weather
- **Poisonous plants** and creatures
- **Bog gases** in some areas
