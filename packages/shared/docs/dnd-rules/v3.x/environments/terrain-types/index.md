## Movement Costs

### Forest Movement
- **Light undergrowth**: 2 squares of movement
- **Heavy undergrowth**: 4 squares of movement
- **Fallen logs**: 5 feet extra movement to cross
- **Normal paths**: Standard movement

### Marsh Movement
- **Shallow bog**: 2 squares of movement
- **Deep bog**: 4 squares of movement (Medium+) or swim
- **Narrow hedgerows**: 15 feet movement to cross
- **Wide hedgerows**: 4 squares movement (2 with DC 10 Climb)

## Concealment and Cover

### Forest Concealment
- **Light undergrowth**: 20% miss chance
- **Heavy undergrowth**: 30% miss chance
- **Trees**: +2 AC, +1 Reflex saves
- **Massive trees**: Full cover

### Marsh Concealment  
- **Undergrowth**: Same as forest undergrowth
- **Deep bog water**: Cover for Medium+, improved cover for Small-
- **Hedgerows**: Low walls (narrow) or total cover (wide)

## Detection Ranges

| **Terrain** | **Maximum Spot Distance** |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Sparse forest | 3d6 × 10 feet |
| Medium forest | 2d8 × 10 feet |
| Dense forest | 2d6 × 10 feet |
| Moor | 6d6 × 10 feet |
| Swamp | 2d8 × 10 feet |

## Skill Modifiers

### Forest Penalties
- **Tumble**: +2 DC (light undergrowth), +5 DC (heavy)
- **Move Silently**: +2 DC (light), +5 DC (heavy)
- **Listen**: +2 DC per 10 feet instead of +1

### Forest Bonuses
- **Hide**: +5 circumstance bonus in heavy undergrowth
- **Climb**: Trees require DC 15 checks

### Marsh Penalties
- **Tumble**: +2 DC (shallow bog), impossible (deep bog)
- **Move Silently**: +2 DC in bog areas
- **Swim**: Required in deep bogs for Small creatures

## Tactical Considerations

### Forest Tactics
- **Ambush opportunities** from concealment
- **Canopy advantage** for elevated combat
- **Limited sight lines** favor close combat
- **Fire hazards** in dry conditions

### Marsh Tactics  
- **Channeling enemies** through non-bog areas
- **Using bogs for cover** (especially small creatures)
- **Exploiting movement restrictions** of heavy enemies
- **Quicksand as natural trap**
