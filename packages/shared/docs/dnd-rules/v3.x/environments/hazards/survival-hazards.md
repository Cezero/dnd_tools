# Survival Environmental Hazards

This section covers environmental hazards related to basic survival: starvation, thirst, suffocation, and water dangers.

## Starvation and Thirst

Characters might find themselves without food or water and with no means to obtain them.

### Basic Needs
- **Normal climates**: Medium characters need at least **a gallon of fluids** and about **a pound of decent food** per day
- **Small characters**: Need **half as much** as Medium characters
- **Very hot climates**: Characters need **two or three times as much water** to avoid dehydration

### Water Deprivation
A character can go **without water** for **1 day plus a number of hours equal to his Constitution score**.

After this time, the character must make a **Constitution check each hour**:
- **DC**: 10, +1 for each previous check
- **Failure**: Take **1d6 points of nonlethal damage**

### Food Deprivation
A character can go **without food for 3 days**, in growing discomfort.

After this time, the character must make a **Constitution check each day**:
- **DC**: 10, +1 for each previous check
- **Failure**: Take **1d6 points of nonlethal damage**

### Starvation/Thirst Effects
Characters who have taken **nonlethal damage from lack of food or water** are **fatigued**. 

**Nonlethal damage from thirst or starvation cannot be recovered** until the character gets food or water, as needed—**not even magic that restores hit points** heals this damage.

## Suffocation

### Holding Breath
A character who has no air to breathe can **hold her breath for 2 rounds per point of Constitution**.

After this period, the character must make a **DC 10 Constitution check** to continue holding her breath. The save must be **repeated each round**, with the **DC increasing by +1** for each previous success.

### Suffocation Process
When the character **fails one of these Constitution checks**, she begins to suffocate:
1. **First round**: Falls **unconscious (0 hit points)**
2. **Following round**: Drops to **-1 hit points and is dying**
3. **Third round**: **Suffocates** (dies)

### Slow Suffocation (Sealed Chambers)
A **Medium character** can breathe easily for **6 hours** in a sealed chamber measuring **10 feet on a side**.

After that time, the character takes **1d6 points of nonlethal damage every 15 minutes**.

#### Air Consumption Modifiers
- **Each additional Medium character**: Proportionally reduces the time the air will last
- **Significant fire source** (torch): Proportionally reduces the time the air will last
- **Small characters**: Consume **half as much air** as Medium characters
- **Larger volume of air**: Lasts for a longer time

#### Unconsciousness from Slow Suffocation
When a character **falls unconscious** from this nonlethal damage:
1. **Immediately**: Drops to **-1 hit points and is dying**
2. **Next round**: **Suffocates** (dies)

## Water Dangers

### Calm Water
- **Wading**: Any character can wade in relatively **calm water that isn't over his head** (no check required)
- **Swimming in calm water**: Only requires **skill checks with DC 10**
- **Trained swimmers**: Can just **take 10**
- **Important**: Armor or heavy gear makes any attempt at swimming **much more difficult**

### Fast-Moving Water
**Fast-moving water** is much more dangerous:
- **Successful check**: **DC 15 Swim** or **DC 15 Strength check**
- **Success**: Deals **1d3 points of nonlethal damage per round** (1d6 lethal if flowing over rocks and cascades)
- **Failure**: Character must make **another check that round** to avoid going under

### Very Deep Water
**Very deep water** poses multiple dangers:
- **Navigation**: Generally **pitch black**, posing a navigational hazard
- **Water pressure**: Deals **1d6 points per minute** for every **100 feet below the surface**
- **Pressure save**: **Fortitude save (DC 15, +1 for each previous check)** means no damage that minute
- **Cold water**: Very cold water deals **1d6 points of nonlethal damage from hypothermia** per minute of exposure

### Drowning
Any character can **hold her breath** for a number of rounds equal to **twice her Constitution score**.

After this period, the character must make a **DC 10 Constitution check every round** to continue holding her breath. **Each round, the DC increases by 1**.

> **See also**: Swim skill description

#### Drowning Process
When the character **finally fails her Constitution check**, she begins to drown:
1. **First round**: Falls **unconscious (0 hp)**
2. **Following round**: Drops to **-1 hit points and is dying**
3. **Third round**: **Drowns** (dies)

#### Alternative Drowning Media
It is possible to **drown in substances other than water**, such as:
- **Sand**
- **Quicksand**
- **Fine dust**
- **Silos full of grain**
