# Environmental Hazards

Natural hazards in wilderness environments can pose significant threats to adventuring parties.

## Environmental Hazards

### Core Hazard Types
- **[Elemental Hazards](elemental-hazards.md)** — Acid, cold, fire, lava, and smoke effects
- **[Physical Hazards](physical-hazards.md)** — Darkness and falling damage
- **[Survival Hazards](survival-hazards.md)** — Starvation, thirst, suffocation, and drowning

### Specific Hazards
- **[Forest Fires](forest-fires.md)** — Wildfire mechanics and effects

## Quick Reference

### Forest Fire Dangers

| **Hazard** | **Effect** | **Save/Check** |
|------------|------------|----------------|
| **Heat damage** | 1d6/round breathing | Automatic |
| **Nonlethal heat** | 1d4 nonlethal | Fort DC 15+1/check |
| **Catching fire** | Standard fire rules | At leading edge |
| **Smoke inhalation** | Choking | Fort DC 15+1/check |

### Detection Distances

| **Hazard Type** | **Detection Range** |
|----------------|-------------------|
| **Forest fire** | 2d6 × 100 feet (Spot) |
| **Fire heat** | 100 feet (automatic) |
| **Smoke plume** | Visible at great distance |

### Movement Speeds
- **Forest fire advancement**: 120 feet/round (moderate winds)
- **Human running**: 120 feet/round (average human)
- **Fire duration**: 2d4 × 10 minutes per area

## Environmental Hazard Types

### Weather Hazards
- **Extreme heat** — Dehydration, heat stroke
- **Extreme cold** — Hypothermia, frostbite  
- **Storms** — Lightning, flooding, high winds
- **Fog** — Visibility reduction, getting lost

### Terrain Hazards
- **Quicksand** — Entrapment and drowning
- **Unstable ground** — Collapses and falls
- **Poisonous plants** — Contact and ingestion toxins
- **Rockslides** — Falling debris damage

### Fire-Related Hazards
- **Wildfires** — Spreading combustion
- **Flash fires** — Sudden ignition
- **Smoke clouds** — Vision and breathing impairment
- **Heated surfaces** — Contact burns

### Water Hazards  
- **Flash floods** — Sudden water flow
- **Thin ice** — Cold water immersion
- **Rapids** — Drowning and battering
- **Contaminated water** — Disease and poison

## Hazard Survival Tips

### Fire Safety
- **Create firebreaks** when camping
- **Monitor weather** for fire conditions
- **Have escape routes** planned
- **Wet down area** before abandoning campfires

### General Precautions
- **Study local hazards** before travel
- **Carry appropriate gear** for conditions
- **Travel in groups** for mutual assistance
- **Inform others** of travel plans

### Emergency Procedures
- **Assess immediate danger** quickly
- **Signal for help** if possible
- **Use available resources** creatively
- **Prioritize group safety** over individual items
