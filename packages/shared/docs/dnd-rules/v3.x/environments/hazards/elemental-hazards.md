# Elemental Environmental Hazards

This section covers environmental hazards related to the classical elements: acid, cold/ice, fire/lava, and air/smoke.

## Acid Effects

**Corrosive acids** deal **1d6 points of damage per round of exposure** except in the case of **total immersion** (such as into a vat of acid), which deals **10d6 points of damage per round**. 

An **attack with acid** (such as from a hurled vial or a monster's spittle) counts as **a round of exposure**.

### Acid Fumes
The **fumes from most acids** are inhaled poisons. Those who come close enough to a large body of acid to dunk a creature in it must make a **DC 13 Fortitude save** or take **1 point of Constitution damage**. 

All such characters must make a **second save 1 minute later** or take another **1d4 points of Constitution damage**.

### Special Considerations
**Creatures immune to acid's caustic properties** might still **drown in it** if they are totally immersed.

## Cold Dangers

**Cold and exposure** deal **nonlethal damage** to the victim. This nonlethal damage **cannot be recovered** until the character gets out of the cold and warms up again. 

Once a character is **rendered unconscious** through the accumulation of nonlethal damage, the cold and exposure begins to deal **lethal damage at the same rate**.

### Cold Weather (Below 40° F)
An **unprotected character** must make a **Fortitude save each hour**:
- **DC**: 15, +1 per previous check
- **Failure**: Take **1d6 points of nonlethal damage**
- **Survival skill**: May provide bonus to save and help others

### Severe Cold (Below 0° F)
An **unprotected character** must make a **Fortitude save once every 10 minutes**:
- **DC**: 15, +1 per previous check  
- **Failure**: Take **1d6 points of nonlethal damage**
- **Winter clothing**: Only need to check **once per hour**
- **Survival skill**: May provide bonus to save and help others

### Cold Effects
A character who takes **any nonlethal damage from cold or exposure** is beset by **frostbite or hypothermia** (treat as **fatigued**). These penalties end when the character **recovers the nonlethal damage**.

### Extreme Cold (Below -20° F)
- **Lethal damage**: **1d6 points per minute** (no save)
- **Additional save**: **Fortitude save (DC 15, +1 per previous check)** or take **1d4 points of nonlethal damage**
- **Metal contact**: Those wearing **metal armor** or coming into contact with very cold metal are affected as if by a **chill metal** spell

## Ice Effects

Characters walking on **ice** must:
- **Movement**: Spend **2 squares of movement** to enter a square covered by ice
- **Skill penalty**: DC for **Balance and Tumble checks** increases by **+5**
- **Cold damage risk**: Characters in prolonged contact with ice may run the risk of taking damage from severe cold

## Fire Effects

### Catching On Fire
Characters exposed to **burning oil, bonfires, and noninstantaneous magic fires** might find their clothes, hair, or equipment on fire. 

**Spells with instantaneous duration** don't normally set a character on fire, since the heat and flame from these come and go in a flash.

### Fire Saves
Characters at risk of catching fire are allowed a **DC 15 Reflex save** to avoid this fate.

### On Fire Effects
If a character's **clothes or hair catch fire**:
1. Takes **1d6 points of damage immediately**
2. **Each subsequent round**: Make another **Reflex saving throw**
   - **Failure**: Take another **1d6 points of damage** that round
   - **Success**: The fire has gone out

### Extinguishing Fire
- **Automatic**: Jumping into **enough water to douse** himself
- **Alternative**: Rolling on the ground or smothering with cloaks permits another save with **+4 bonus**

### Equipment Fire Damage
Those with **clothes or equipment catching fire** must make **DC 15 Reflex saves for each item**. **Flammable items that fail** take the same amount of damage as the character.

## Lava Effects

**Lava or magma** deals **2d6 points of damage per round of exposure**, except in the case of **total immersion** (such as falling into the crater of an active volcano), which deals **20d6 points of damage per round**.

### Continued Damage
**Damage from magma continues for 1d3 rounds** after exposure ceases, but this additional damage is only **half** of that dealt during actual contact:
- **Normal exposure**: 1d6 points per round
- **Total immersion**: 10d6 points per round

### Fire Immunity
An **immunity or resistance to fire** serves as an immunity to lava or magma. However, a **creature immune to fire** might still **drown** if completely immersed in lava.

## Smoke Effects

A character who breathes **heavy smoke** must make a **Fortitude save each round**:
- **DC**: 15, +1 per previous check
- **Failure**: Spend that round **choking and coughing**

### Choking Damage
A character who **chokes for 2 consecutive rounds** takes **1d6 points of nonlethal damage**.

### Vision Obscurement
**Smoke obscures vision**, giving **concealment (20% miss chance)** to characters within it.
