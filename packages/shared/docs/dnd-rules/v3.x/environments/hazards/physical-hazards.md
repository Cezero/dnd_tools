# Physical Environmental Hazards

This section covers physical environmental hazards including darkness and falling damage.

## Darkness

**Darkvision** allows many characters and monsters to see perfectly well without any light at all, but characters with **normal vision** (or **low-light vision**) can be rendered completely blind by putting out the lights.

### Sources of Darkness
- **Torches or lanterns** can be blown out by sudden gusts of subterranean wind
- **Magical light sources** can be dispelled or countered
- **Magical traps** might create fields of impenetrable darkness

### Blinded by Darkness Effects

In many cases, some characters or monsters might be able to see, while others are blinded. For purposes of the following points, a **blinded creature** is one who simply can't see through the surrounding darkness.

#### Combat Effects
- **No precision damage**: Blinded creatures lose the ability to deal extra damage due to precision (e.g., sneak attack)
- **Total concealment**: All opponents have total concealment from a blinded creature, so the blinded creature has a **50% miss chance** in combat
- **AC penalties**: Blinded creature loses **Dexterity adjustment to AC** and takes a **-2 penalty to AC**

#### Movement Effects
- **Hampered movement**: Pay **2 squares of movement per square** moved into (double normal cost)
- **No running or charging**: Blinded creatures can't run or charge

#### Attack Effects
- **Must pinpoint location**: Blinded creature must first pinpoint the location of an opponent to attack the right square
- **Random attacks**: If attacking without pinpointing foe, attacks a **random square within reach**
- **Ranged attacks**: For ranged attacks against unpinpointed foes, roll to determine which adjacent square the creature is facing

#### Skill Effects
- **Search penalty**: **-4 penalty on Search checks** and most Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks (including armor check penalty)
- **Vision-based skills**: Automatically fails any skill check relying on vision

#### Gaze Effects
- **Cannot use gaze attacks** and are **immune to gaze attacks**

### Locating Opponents

#### Listen Checks
A creature blinded by darkness can make a **Listen check as a free action each round** to locate foes:
- **DC**: Equal to opponents' Move Silently checks
- **Success**: Hear an unseen creature "over there somewhere"
- **Beat DC by 20**: Reveals the unseen creature's square (but still has total concealment)

#### Groping
A blinded creature can **grope about to find unseen creatures**:
- **Action**: Standard action to make touch attack into **two adjacent squares**
- **Hit chance**: **50% miss chance** on the touch attack
- **Success**: **Pinpoints** the unseen creature's current location (no damage dealt)
- **Movement**: If the unseen creature moves, location becomes unknown again

#### Being Struck
- If a blinded creature is **struck by an unseen foe**, the blinded character **pinpoints the location** of the creature that struck them
- **Exception**: If the unseen creature has **reach greater than 5 feet**, blinded character knows location but has not pinpointed them
- **Ranged attacks**: Blinded character knows **general direction** but not location

#### Special Senses
A creature with the **scent ability** automatically pinpoints unseen creatures **within 5 feet** of its location.

## Falling

### Basic Falling Damage
The basic rule is simple: **1d6 points of damage per 10 feet fallen**, to a **maximum of 20d6**.

### Deliberate Jumps
If a character **deliberately jumps** instead of merely slipping or falling:
- **Damage**: Same as falling
- **First 1d6**: Becomes **nonlethal damage**

### Jump/Tumble Checks
A **DC 15 Jump check** or **DC 15 Tumble check** allows the character to:
- **Avoid any damage** from the first 10 feet fallen
- **Convert damage** from the second 10 feet to nonlethal damage

#### Example
- **Character slips 30 feet**: Takes **3d6 damage**
- **Character deliberately jumps 30 feet**: Takes **1d6 nonlethal + 2d6 lethal**
- **Character jumps with successful skill check**: Takes **1d6 nonlethal + 1d6 lethal**

### Yielding Surfaces
Falls onto **yielding surfaces** (soft ground, mud) convert the **first 1d6 of damage to nonlethal damage**. This reduction is **cumulative** with reduced damage due to deliberate jumps and Jump skill.

### Falling into Water
Falls into water are handled differently:

#### Deep Water (At Least 10 Feet Deep)
- **First 20 feet**: **No damage**
- **Next 20 feet**: **Nonlethal damage** (1d3 per 10-foot increment)
- **Beyond 40 feet**: **Lethal damage** (1d6 per additional 10-foot increment)

#### Shallow Water
- Use **normal falling damage** rules
- Water may act as a **yielding surface** (convert first 1d6 to nonlethal)

#### Very Cold Water
**Very cold water** deals **1d6 points of nonlethal damage from hypothermia** per minute of exposure.

### Objects Falling on Characters
Just as characters take damage when they fall more than 10 feet, so too do they take damage when things fall on them. Objects that fall upon characters deal damage based on their **weight and the distance they have fallen**.

#### Object Damage Table
Objects weighing **200 pounds or less** that fall upon a character deal **1d6 points of damage** per 10 feet fallen, to a maximum of **20d6** points of damage.

For every **200 additional pounds** of the object's weight, increase the damage by **1d6** points (to a maximum of **20d6** points of damage).

#### Saving Throws
Characters who **successfully make a DC 15 Reflex save** take **half damage** from a falling object.
