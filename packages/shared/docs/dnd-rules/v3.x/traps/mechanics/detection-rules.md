# Trap Detection and Disabling

## Detection Rules

### Simple Mechanical Traps
- **DC 20 Search check** detects before triggered
- Simple traps include: snares, tripwire traps, large traps (such as pits)

### Complex Mechanical Traps
- Requires **trapfinding class feature**
- **DC 21+ Search check** detects before triggered
- Complex mechanisms include: pressure plates, door-linked mechanisms, weight changes, air disturbances, vibrations, and other unusual triggers

### Magic Traps
- Requires **trapfinding class feature** to detect
- **DC varies** by highest spell level used
- Cannot be detected by characters without trapfinding

## Search and Disable Device DCs

The builder sets the Search and Disable Device DCs for a mechanical trap. For a magic trap, the values depend on the highest-level spell used.

### Mechanical Trap DCs
- **Base DC**: 20 for both Search and Disable Device
- Raising or lowering these DCs affects **base cost** and possibly **CR**
- Builder can modify DCs during construction

### Magic Trap DCs
- **Search DC**: 25 + spell level of highest-level spell used
- **Disable Device DC**: 25 + spell level of highest-level spell used
- Only characters with **trapfinding** can attempt either check
- These DCs do **not** affect the trap's cost or CR

## Disabling Traps

### Mechanical Traps
- **Disable Device check** against trap's DC
- **Tools required**: Thieves' tools or makeshift tools (-2 penalty)
- **Time**: Usually 1 round per attempt
- **Failure**: May trigger the trap (DM's discretion)

### Magic Traps
- **Disable Device check** against DC (25 + spell level)
- Requires **trapfinding class feature**
- **Tools**: May require special tools for complex mechanisms
- **Alternative**: **Dispel magic** can disable magic traps

### Taking 20
- **Mechanical traps**: Allowed if no penalty for failure
- **Magic traps**: Usually not recommended due to trigger risk
- **Time**: 20 times normal (20 rounds for most traps)

## Special Detection Situations

### Invisible or Hidden Traps
- **Search DC** increases based on concealment
- **Detect Magic** reveals magic traps (but not mechanics)
- **True Seeing** pierces illusions hiding traps

### Environmental Factors
- **Lighting**: Poor light imposes penalties to Search
- **Noise**: Loud environments may mask trigger sounds
- **Distractions**: Combat or stress may impose penalties

### Multiple Attempts
- **Retry**: Allowed for Search checks
- **Failure margin**: Large failures may trigger trap
- **Group effort**: Multiple characters can search different areas
