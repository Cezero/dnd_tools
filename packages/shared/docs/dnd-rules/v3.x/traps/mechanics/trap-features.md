# Special Trap Features

Some traps include optional features that can make them considerably more deadly. The most common features are discussed below.

## Alchemical Items

Mechanical traps may incorporate **alchemical devices** or other special substances:
- **Tanglefoot bags**
- **Alchemist's fire**
- **Thunderstones**
- **Acid vials**
- **Other special substances**

**Effect on CR:** If the item mimics a spell effect, it increases the CR as shown on design tables.

## Gas Traps

With a gas trap, the danger is in the **inhaled poison** it delivers. 

**Common Features:**
- Usually have **never miss** feature
- Usually have **onset delay** feature
- **Area effect** (affects multiple targets)
- **Ventilation** can reduce effectiveness

**Gas Types:**
- **Poison gas** (ability damage/death)
- **Sleep gas** (unconsciousness)
- **Confusion gas** (mental effects)
- **Choking gas** (suffocation)

## Liquid Traps

Any trap that involves a **danger of drowning** is in this category.

**Common Features:**
- Usually have **never miss** feature
- Usually have **onset delay** feature
- **Escape methods** available (Swimming, climbing)

**Liquid Types:**
- **Water** (drowning)
- **Acid** (ongoing damage)
- **Oil** (fire hazard)
- **Quicksand** (entrapment)

## Multiple Target Feature

Traps with this feature can **affect more than one character**.

**Implementation:**
- **Area effects** (explosions, gas)
- **Multiple mechanisms** (arrow volley)
- **Chain reactions** (domino effects)
- **Persistent hazards** (flooding)

## Never Miss Feature

When the entire dungeon wall moves to crush you, quick reflexes won't help—the wall can't possibly miss. A trap with this feature has:
- **No attack bonus**
- **No saving throw to avoid**
- **Always has onset delay**

**Examples:**
- **Crushing walls**
- **Flooding chambers**
- **Gas-filled rooms**
- **Collapsing ceilings**

## Onset Delay

An **onset delay** is the time between when the trap is sprung and when it deals damage. A **never miss** trap always has an onset delay.

**Common Delays:**
- **1 round** (immediate but avoidable)
- **1d4 rounds** (short escape window)
- **1 minute** (exploration time)
- **10 minutes** (extended warning)

**Escape Opportunities:**
- **Movement** out of area
- **Disabling** the trap
- **Countermeasures** (magical protection)

## Poison Features

Traps that employ **poison** are deadlier than nonpoisonous counterparts and have correspondingly higher CRs.

**Suitable Poison Types:**
- **Injury poisons** (weapon coatings)
- **Contact poisons** (touch surfaces)
- **Inhaled poisons** (gas traps)

**Note:** Ingested poisons are **not suitable** for traps.

**Damage Types:**
- **Poison damage only**
- **Weapon + poison damage**
- **Special poison effects** (ability drain, paralysis)

## Pit Features

### Pit Spikes
Treat spikes at the bottom of a pit as **daggers**:
- **Attack bonus**: +10 each
- **Damage bonus**: +1 per 10 feet of pit depth (maximum +5)
- **Number of attacks**: 1d4 spikes per falling character
- **CR consideration**: Do not add to average damage calculation

### Pit Bottom Hazards
If something other than spikes waits at the bottom, treat it as a **separate trap**:
- **Location trigger**: Activates on significant impact
- **Independent mechanics**: Separate Search/Disable DCs
- **Stacking effects**: Both traps can affect victims

**Examples:**
- **Acid pool**
- **Monster lair**
- **Secondary trap**
- **Teleportation circle**

## Touch Attack Feature

This feature applies to any trap that needs only a successful **touch attack** (melee or ranged) to hit.

**Benefits:**
- **Higher hit chance** (ignores armor and natural armor)
- **Only Dex and size** modify AC
- **Effective against heavily armored targets**

**Examples:**
- **Ray effects**
- **Touch spells**
- **Contact poisons**
- **Electrical discharge**

## Custom Features

Builders can create unique trap features:

### Environmental Integration
- **Weather effects** (lightning, wind)
- **Terrain hazards** (landslides, sinkholes)
- **Natural creatures** (summoned animals)

### Magical Enhancement
- **Spell combinations** (multiple effects)
- **Metamagic effects** (empowered, enlarged)
- **Persistent magic** (lasting effects)

### Mechanical Complexity
- **Sequential triggers** (multi-stage traps)
- **Conditional activation** (intelligent responses)
- **Adaptive mechanisms** (learning traps)
