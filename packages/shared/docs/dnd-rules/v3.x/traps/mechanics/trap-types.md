# Trap Types

A trap can be either **mechanical** or **magic** in nature.

## Mechanical Traps

**Mechanical traps** include:
- Pits
- Arrow traps  
- Falling blocks
- Water-filled rooms
- Whirling blades
- Anything else that depends on a mechanism to operate

A mechanical trap can be constructed by a PC through successful use of the **Craft (trapmaking)** skill (see [Designing a Trap](../designing.md) and the skill description).

### Mechanical Trap Construction Requirements

- **Craft (trapmaking)** skill
- Raw materials (cost varies by complexity)
- Time (based on market price and DC)

## Magic Traps

Magic traps are further divided into **spell traps** and **magic device traps**.

### Magic Device Traps

**Magic device traps** initiate spell effects when activated, just as wands, rods, rings, and other magic items do. Creating a magic device trap requires the **Craft Wondrous Item** feat (see [Designing a Trap](../designing.md) and the feat description).

**Construction Requirements:**
- **Craft Wondrous Item** feat
- Ability to cast required spells (or hire NPC)
- Raw materials and time

### Spell Traps

**Spell traps** are simply spells that themselves function as traps. Creating a spell trap requires the services of a character who can cast the needed spell or spells, who is usually either the character creating the trap or an NPC spellcaster hired for the purpose.

**Construction Requirements:**
- Spellcaster who can cast the needed spell(s)
- Standard spellcasting components
- Time to cast and set the trap

## Trap Effects and Saves

### Mechanical Traps
- Use **normal attack rolls** with specific attack bonus
- **Saving throws** vary by trap design
- **Damage** depends on trap mechanism

### Spell Traps
- Produce the **spell's effect**
- **Save DC**: 10 + spell level + caster's relevant ability modifier
- Follow normal spell rules

### Magic Device Traps
- Produce effects of **spells included in construction**
- **Save DC**: 10 + spell level × 1.5
- Some spells make **attack rolls** instead
