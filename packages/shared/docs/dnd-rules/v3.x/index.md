## 🎲 **Quick Reference Tables**

### **Action Economy**
| **Action Type** | **Examples** | **Movement** |
|-----------------|--------------|--------------|
| **Standard** | Attack, cast spell | Yes (with move) |
| **Move** | Move speed, draw weapon | Yes |
| **Full-Round** | Full attack, charge | Limited (5-ft step) |
| **Free** | Speak, drop item | Yes |
| **Swift** | Quickened spell | Yes |
| **Immediate** | Interrupt action | Yes |

### **Combat Modifiers**
| **Situation** | **Attack** | **AC** | **Notes** |
|---------------|------------|--------|-----------|
| **Flanking** | +2 | — | Must threaten opposite sides |
| **Charging** | +2 | -2 | Must move straight line |
| **Fighting Defensively** | -4 | +2 | Standard action |
| **Total Defense** | — | +4 | Standard action, no attacks |
| **Cover (Partial)** | -2 | +4 | 25% to 50% covered |
| **Concealment (Partial)** | — | — | 20% miss chance |

### **Condition Quick Reference**
| **Condition** | **Attack** | **AC** | **Movement** | **Other Effects** |
|---------------|------------|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Blinded** | -2 | -2 | Half speed | 50% miss chance |
| **Deafened** | — | — | — | 20% spell failure |
| **Prone** | -4 | +4 vs melee, -4 vs ranged | — | Stand up provokes AoO |
| **Grappling** | -4 | +0 | — | Limited actions |
