## Quick Reference

### Ability Types

| **Type** | **Dispellable** | **Spell Resistance** | **Antimagic Field** | **Provokes AoO** |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Extraordinary (Ex)** | No | No | No | No |
| **Spell-Like (Sp)** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Supernatural (Su)** | No | No | Yes | No |
| **Natural** | No | No | No | No |

### Common Extraordinary Abilities

#### Sensory
- **Blindsight** — See without sight, ignores concealment
- **Darkvision** — See in darkness (black & white only)
- **Scent** — Detect creatures by smell within 30 ft
- **Tremorsense** — Detect ground vibrations

#### Movement
- **Burrow** — Tunnel through earth (+8 to related checks)
- **Climb** — Scale surfaces (+8 Climb bonus, retain Dex to AC)
- **Fly** — Aerial movement (maneuverability varies)
- **Swim** — Aquatic movement (+8 Swim bonus)

#### Defensive
- **Damage Reduction X/Y** — Reduce damage by X unless from Y
- **Fast Healing X** — Regain X hit points per round
- **Evasion** — Reflex save for no damage instead of half
- **Spell Resistance X** — Resist spells (caster level vs X)

#### Combat
- **Improved Grab** — Free grapple attempt on hit
- **Constrict** — Automatic damage while grappling
- **Rake** — Extra claw attacks while grappling
- **Rend** — Bonus damage when both claws hit
- **Pounce** — Full attack after charge

### Common Supernatural Abilities

#### Area Effects
- **Breath Weapon** — Cone/line area attack (usually Reflex save)
- **Aura** — Constant area effect around creature
- **Fear Aura** — Cause fear in nearby enemies

#### Targeted Effects
- **Gaze Attack** — Eye contact causes effect (various saves)
- **Energy Drain** — Inflict negative levels on touch
- **Death Attack** — Instant death on failed save

### Common Spell-Like Abilities
- **At Will** — Usable unlimited times per day
- **3/day** — Three times per day
- **1/day** — Once per day
- **Caster Level** — Determines effect strength and dispel DC

### Save DC Formulas

| **Ability Type** | **Save DC Formula** |
|------------------|-------------------|
| **Spell-Like** | 10 + Spell Level + Cha modifier |
| **Supernatural** | 10 + ½ HD + Ability modifier (usually Cha) |
| **Extraordinary** | 10 + ½ HD + relevant ability modifier |

### Ability Frequency Guidelines

| **Frequency** | **Usage Pattern** | **Power Level** |
|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Constant** | Always active | Moderate |
| **At Will** | No daily limit | Moderate |
| **3/day** | Three uses daily | Significant |
| **1/day** | Once per day | High |
| **1/week** | Once per week | Very High |

### Common Immunities

| **Immunity** | **Effect** | **Common In** |
|--------------|------------|---------------|
| **Mind Effects** | All mental influence | Constructs, undead |
| **Poison** | All poison effects | Constructs, undead |
| **Disease** | All diseases | Constructs, outsiders |
| **Energy Type** | Specific energy immunity | Elementals, dragons |
| **Critical Hits** | No critical hits/sneak attack | Constructs, oozes |
