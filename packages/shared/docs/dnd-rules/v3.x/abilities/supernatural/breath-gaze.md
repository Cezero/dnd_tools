# Breath Weapons and Gaze Attacks

This document covers supernatural abilities that affect areas or require direct visual contact.

## Breath Weapon

A creature attacking with a **breath weapon** is actually expelling something from its mouth (rather than conjuring it by means of a spell or some other magical effect). Most creatures with breath weapons are limited to a **number of uses per day** or by a **minimum length of time** that must pass between uses.

### Breath Weapon Mechanics
- **Standard action** to use (typically)
- **No attack roll** is necessary — the breath simply fills its stated area
- **Usually deals damage** and is often based on some type of energy
- **Creatures are smart** enough to save their breath weapon until they really need it

### Saving Throws
- Breath weapons usually allow a **Reflex save for half damage**
- **DC = 10 + ½ breathing creature's racial HD + breathing creature's Con modifier**
- Some breath weapons allow a **Fortitude save** or **Will save** instead of Reflex
- **Exact DC** is given in the creature's descriptive text

### Breath Weapon Properties
- **Supernatural abilities** except where noted
- **Creature is immune** to its own breath weapon unless otherwise noted
- **Creatures unable to breathe** can still use breath weapons (the term is something of a misnomer)

### Common Breath Weapon Types

| **Type** | **Shape** | **Damage Type** | **Save** |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| **Fire** | Cone or line | Fire | Reflex half |
| **Cold** | Cone or line | Cold | Reflex half |
| **Acid** | Line | Acid | Reflex half |
| **Lightning** | Line | Electricity | Reflex half |
| **Poison Gas** | Cone | Poison/ability damage | Fortitude negates |
| **Fear** | Cone | Fear effect | Will negates |
| **Sleep** | Cone | Sleep effect | Will negates |
| **Paralysis** | Cone | Paralysis | Fortitude negates |

### Breath Weapon Areas
- **Cone** — Spreads out from creature's mouth in expanding cone
- **Line** — Straight line from creature's mouth to maximum range
- **Cloud** — Area around creature or targeted location

## Gaze Attacks

A **gaze attack** takes effect when foes look at the attacking creature's eyes. The attack can have almost any sort of effect: petrification, death, charm, and so on.

### Gaze Attack Mechanics
- **Supernatural ability** that affects vision
- **No action required** by the gazing creature
- **Affects all creatures** within range that can see the creature's eyes
- **Range** typically 30 feet unless otherwise specified

### Gaze Attack Process
1. **At start of gazing creature's turn**, all creatures within range that can see its eyes are affected
2. **Saving throw** allowed (type varies by gaze attack)
3. **Effect occurs** based on save result
4. **Usually once per round** per creature

### Avoiding Gaze Attacks

#### Averting Eyes
- **Looking away** from the creature's face
- **50% chance** to avoid the gaze attack each round
- **Creature has concealment** against averted attacker (20% miss chance)
- **Voluntary choice** each round

#### Closing Eyes
- **Automatic immunity** to gaze attacks
- **Creature has total concealment** against blinded attacker (50% miss chance)
- **Blinded condition** applies to attacker

#### Blindfold/Darkness
- **Complete immunity** if creature cannot see
- **Same penalties** as closing eyes
- **Must be truly unable to see** (not just reduced vision)

### Gaze Attack Defenses

#### Indirect Vision
- **Mirrors** allow attacking without direct eye contact
- **Still subject** to gaze attack
- **Creature has concealment** (20% miss chance) in mirror

#### Blind-Fight
- **Reduces miss chance** from concealment
- **Does not prevent** gaze attacks
- **Only affects combat penalties**

### Common Gaze Effects

| **Gaze Type** | **Effect** | **Save** | **Range** |
|---------------|------------|----------|-----------|
| **Petrification** | Turn to stone | Fortitude negates | 30 ft |
| **Death** | Instant death | Fortitude negates | 30 ft |
| **Charm** | Charmed condition | Will negates | 30 ft |
| **Fear** | Frightened/panicked | Will negates | 30 ft |
| **Paralysis** | Paralyzed condition | Fortitude negates | 30 ft |
| **Sleep** | Magical sleep | Will negates | 30 ft |
| **Confusion** | Confused condition | Will negates | 30 ft |
| **Domination** | Dominated condition | Will negates | 30 ft |

### Special Gaze Rules

#### Multiple Gazes
- **Creature can have** multiple different gaze attacks
- **Each affects separately** (multiple saves required)
- **Usually** one gaze attack per type per round

#### Reflected Gazes
- **Mirrors and reflective surfaces** can reflect gaze attacks back
- **50% chance** the gaze affects the gazer instead
- **Creature uses its own save** against reflected gaze

#### Active vs Passive
- **Most gazes are passive** — automatic effect on viewing
- **Some gazes are active** — creature must choose to use them
- **Active gazes** may have limited uses per day

### Gaze Attack Limitations
- **Line of sight required** — must be able to see target's eyes
- **Does not work** on creatures without eyes or vision
- **Blocked by full cover** or total concealment
- **Subject to spell resistance** if save allowed
- **Usually once per target** per round
