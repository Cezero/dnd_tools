# Defensive Extraordinary Abilities

This document covers extraordinary abilities that provide defensive benefits and damage mitigation.

## Damage Reduction

A creature with this special quality **ignores damage** from most weapons and natural attacks. Wounds heal immediately, or the weapon bounces off harmlessly (in either case, the opponent knows the attack was ineffective). The creature takes **normal damage** from:
- **Energy attacks** (even nonmagical ones)
- **Spells**
- **Spell-like abilities** 
- **Supernatural abilities**

A certain kind of weapon can sometimes damage the creature normally, as noted below.

### Damage Reduction Types

Damage reduction is expressed as **"X/Y"** where:
- **X** = Amount of damage reduced
- **Y** = Type of attack that bypasses the reduction

#### Common Bypass Types
- **Magic** — Magic weapons, spells, supernatural abilities
- **Silver** — Silvered or alchemical silver weapons
- **Cold Iron** — Cold iron weapons
- **Good/Evil/Lawful/Chaotic** — Aligned weapons or creatures
- **+1, +2, etc.** — Weapons with minimum enhancement bonus
- **Epic** — Epic weapons (enhancement bonus +6 or higher)
- **—** (dash) — Cannot be bypassed by weapons

### Damage Reduction Mechanics
- **Subtract the DR** from the damage dealt by qualifying attacks
- **Minimum damage** is always 0 (never negative)
- **Does not stack** — if a creature has multiple sources of DR, only the highest applies
- **Energy damage** always bypasses damage reduction

### Examples
- **DR 5/magic** — Reduces damage by 5 unless from magic weapons/sources
- **DR 10/silver** — Reduces damage by 10 unless from silver weapons
- **DR 15/+1** — Reduces damage by 15 unless from +1 or better magic weapons

## Fast Healing

A creature with **fast healing** ability regains hit points at an exceptional rate, usually **1 or more hit points per round**, as given in the creature's entry.

### Fast Healing Properties
- **Automatic healing** at the start of each round
- **Works normally** until the creature is reduced to **negative hit points**
- **Stops functioning** when the creature **dies**
- **Does not restore** hit points lost from **starvation**, **thirst**, or **suffocation**
- **Does not allow** a creature to regrow or attach **severed body parts**

### Interaction with Death
- **Cannot save** a creature that has died
- If the creature is **brought back to life**, fast healing **continues to function normally**

## Regeneration

Creatures with this extraordinary ability **recover from wounds quickly** and can even regrow or reattach severed body parts.

### Regeneration vs Fast Healing
- **Functions like fast healing** with additional benefits
- **Can regrow lost portions** of its body
- **Can reattach severed parts** instantly by holding them to the wound
- **Cannot die** as long as its regeneration is functioning

### Stopping Regeneration
- Certain attack forms (typically **acid and fire**) cause the creature's regeneration to **stop functioning**
- During this time, the creature **can die normally**
- **Attack forms** that stop regeneration are specified in the creature's description
- **Regeneration resumes** once the stopping condition is removed

### Death and Regeneration
- A creature must make a **Constitution check** each round to survive after being reduced to negative hit points
- **If regeneration is stopped**, the creature dies normally when reduced to the appropriate negative hit point total
- **If regeneration is functioning**, the creature is difficult or impossible to kill through hit point loss

## Immunities

A creature with an **immunity** to a specific condition, energy type, or effect **never takes damage** or suffers the effects from that source.

### Common Immunities
- **Mind Effects** — Immune to all mind-affecting spells and abilities
- **Energy Types** — Immune to fire, cold, electricity, acid, or sonic damage
- **Conditions** — Immune to fear, charm, paralysis, etc.
- **Critical Hits** — Immune to critical hits and sneak attacks
- **Death Effects** — Immune to necromantic death effects

### Energy Immunity Rules
- **No damage** from the specified energy type
- **Often paired with vulnerability** to the opposite energy type
- **Affects all sources** of that energy type (natural, magical, supernatural)

## Resistances

A creature with **resistance** to an energy type takes **reduced damage** from attacks of that energy type.

### Resistance Mechanics
- **Subtract the resistance value** from damage of the specified type
- **Expressed as number** (e.g., "fire resistance 10")
- **Applied after** all other damage calculations
- **Multiple resistances** to the same energy type **do not stack**

### Common Resistances
- **Spell Resistance** — Chance to ignore spell effects entirely
- **Energy Resistance** — Reduce damage from specific energy types
- **Turn Resistance** — Bonus to resist turning attempts

## Evasion and Improved Evasion

**Evasion** allows a character to avoid even magical and unusual attacks with great agility.

### Evasion (Ex)
- **Reflex save for half damage** → **No damage** on successful save
- **No benefit** if wearing medium or heavy armor or carrying a medium or heavy load
- **Helpless characters** cannot use evasion

### Improved Evasion (Ex)
- **Successful Reflex save** → **No damage**
- **Failed Reflex save** → **Half damage** (instead of full)
- **Same restrictions** as regular evasion regarding armor and encumbrance

### Usage Notes
- **Only applies** to attacks that normally allow a Reflex save for half damage
- **Does not work** against attacks that don't allow saves or require other save types
- **Must be able to react** to the attack (not flat-footed or otherwise denied Dex to AC)
