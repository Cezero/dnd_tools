# Combat Extraordinary Abilities

This document covers extraordinary abilities that enhance combat effectiveness and provide special attack options.

## Improved Grab

If a creature with this special attack **hits with a melee attack** (usually a claw or bite attack), it deals normal damage and attempts to start a **grapple as a free action** without provoking an attack of opportunity.

### Improved Grab Mechanics
- **No initial touch attack** required (unlike normal grappling)
- **Grapple check** made immediately after hit
- **Size restrictions** usually apply (target must be specific size or smaller)
- **Does not provoke** attacks of opportunity

### After Successful Grab
- Creature has several options in subsequent rounds:
  - **Constrict** (if the creature has this ability)
  - **Continue grappling**
  - **Rake with claws** (if applicable)
  - **Swallow whole** (if applicable)

## Constrict

A creature with this special attack can **crush an opponent**, dealing bludgeoning damage, after making a **successful grapple check**. The amount of damage is given in the creature's entry.

### Constrict Usage
- **Requires successful grapple** to use
- **Automatic damage** each round grappling is maintained
- **Usually bludgeoning damage** type
- **Damage amount** specified in creature description

### Combination with Improved Grab
If the creature also has **improved grab**, it deals **constriction damage in addition to** damage dealt by the weapon used to grab.

## Rake

A creature with this special attack gains **extra natural weapon attacks** when it **grapples a foe**. Normally, a creature can attack with only one of its natural weapons while grappling, but creatures with rake can use **two or more natural weapons**.

### Rake Mechanics
- **Only usable while grappling**
- **Additional attacks** beyond normal grappling limitations
- **Usually claw attacks** at full attack bonus
- **Damage as specified** in creature description

### Usage Requirements
- Must have **successfully grappled** the opponent
- **Triggered automatically** each round of maintained grapple
- **Does not require** additional grapple checks

## Rend

If a creature **hits with two or more natural weapons** in the same round, it can cause tremendous damage by **latching onto the opponent's body** and tearing flesh.

### Rend Requirements
- Must hit with **two specific natural weapons** (usually claws) in the same round
- **Both attacks must hit** the same target
- **Automatic additional damage** when conditions are met

### Rend Damage
- **Extra damage** as specified in creature description
- **Usually significant** (often 2d6 + Str modifier or more)
- **Applied automatically** when rend conditions are met

## Swallow Whole

If a creature with this special attack **begins its turn** with an opponent held in its mouth (see Improved Grab), it can attempt a **new grapple check** (as though attempting to pin the opponent).

### Swallow Mechanics
- **Requires improved grab** or similar ability first
- **Grapple check** to swallow (as if pinning)
- **Target size restrictions** apply (usually one size smaller than swallower)
- **Success** means opponent is swallowed

### Being Swallowed
- **Considered grappled** while swallowed
- **Swallowing creature is not grappled**
- **Ongoing damage** as specified (usually acid damage)
- **Limited space** for movement and actions

### Escaping
- **Cut way free** with light slashing or piercing weapon
- **Required damage** specified in creature description
- **Escape grapple** returns character to creature's mouth (can be bitten or swallowed again)
- **Interior AC** typically 10 + ½ natural armor bonus

## Trample

As a **full-round action**, a creature with this special attack can move up to **twice its speed** and literally run over any opponents **at least one size category smaller** than itself.

### Trample Mechanics
- **Full-round action** to use
- **Move up to 2× speed** in straight line
- **Affects all creatures** whose space is completely covered
- **No attack roll** required

### Trample Damage
- **Bludgeoning damage** equal to creature's slam damage + 1½ Str modifier
- **Reflex save** for half damage (DC 10 + ½ HD + Str modifier)
- **One damage roll per target** regardless of movement path

### Trample Defenses
- **Attacks of opportunity** at -4 penalty
- **OR Reflex save** for half damage (defender's choice)
- **Cannot choose both** attack of opportunity and save

## Pounce

When a creature with this special attack makes a **charge**, it can follow with a **full attack**—including rake attacks if the creature also has that ability.

### Pounce Benefits
- **Charge + full attack** in same round
- **All attacks** at appropriate bonuses
- **Includes special attacks** like rake
- **Overcomes normal charge limitations**

### Usage
- **Must charge** to activate pounce
- **Full attack follows charge** automatically
- **No additional actions** required

## Spell Resistance

A creature with **spell resistance** can avoid the effects of spells and spell-like abilities that directly affect it.

### Spell Resistance Mechanics
- **Expressed as number** (e.g., "SR 15")
- **Opposed by caster level check** (1d20 + caster level)
- **Caster must equal or exceed** SR to affect the creature
- **Voluntary lowering** possible as a standard action

### What SR Affects
- **Spells** that target the creature or include it in area
- **Spell-like abilities** with same restrictions
- **Does not affect** extraordinary or supernatural abilities
- **Some spells** specifically bypass spell resistance

### Spell Resistance Notes
- **Always applies** unless voluntarily lowered
- **Lowering SR** affects all spells until raised again
- **Raising SR** is a standard action
- **Area spells** still affect if SR is overcome
