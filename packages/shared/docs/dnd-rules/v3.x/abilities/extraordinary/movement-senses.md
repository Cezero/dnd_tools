# Movement and Sensory Abilities

This document covers extraordinary abilities related to movement modes and enhanced senses.

## Movement Modes

### Burrow

A creature with a burrow speed can tunnel through dirt, but not through rock unless the descriptive text says otherwise. Creatures cannot charge or run while burrowing. Most burrowing creatures do not leave behind tunnels other creatures can use (either because the material they tunnel through fills in behind them or because they do not actually dislocate any material when burrowing); see the individual creature descriptions for details.

### Climb

A creature with a **climb speed** has a **+8 racial bonus** on all Climb checks. The creature must make a Climb check to climb any wall or slope with a DC higher than 0, but it always can choose to take 10, even if rushed or threatened while climbing. If a creature with a climb speed chooses an accelerated climb (see the Climb skill description), it moves at double its climb speed (or at its land speed, whichever is slower) and makes a single Climb check at a -5 penalty. Such a creature retains its Dexterity bonus to Armor Class (if any) while climbing, and opponents get no special bonus to their attacks against it. It cannot, however, use the run action while climbing.

### Fly

A creature with a **fly speed** can move through the air at the indicated speed if carrying no more than a medium load. (Note that medium armor does not necessarily constitute a medium load.) All fly speeds include a parenthetical note indicating maneuverability, as follows:

| **Maneuverability** | **Turn Capability** | **Turn in Place** | **Fly Backward** | **Hover** |
|-------------------|-------------------|------------------|------------------|-----------|
| **Perfect** | Any angle | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Good** | Up to 90° | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Average** | Up to 45° | No | No | Yes |
| **Poor** | Up to 45° | No | No | No |
| **Clumsy** | No more than 45° | No | No | No |

Creatures that fly can make dive attacks. A dive attack works like a charge, but the flying creature must move a minimum of 30 feet and descend at least 10 feet. A creature can make a dive attack only when flying downward at an angle of 45 degrees or steeper. If the attack hits, it deals double damage (or 1½ times damage if the creature is using a two-handed weapon). Creatures with poor maneuverability can't make dive attacks.

A creature with fly speed retains its Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) while flying. Opponents on the ground get no special bonus to their attacks against flying creatures.

### Swim

A creature with a **swim speed** can move through water at its indicated speed without making Swim checks. It has a **+8 racial bonus** on any Swim check to perform some special action or avoid a hazard. The creature always can choose to take 10 on a Swim check, even if distracted or endangered. The creature can use the run action while swimming, provided it swims in a straight line.

## Sensory Abilities

### Blindsight

Some creatures have **blindsight**, the extraordinary ability to use a nonvisual sense (or a combination of such senses) to operate effectively without vision. Such sense may include:
- **Sensitivity to vibrations**
- **Acute scent**
- **Keen hearing** 
- **Echolocation**

#### Blindsight Properties
- Makes **invisibility and concealment** (even magical darkness) **irrelevant** to the creature
- Still **can't see ethereal creatures**
- Must have **line of effect** to a creature or object to discern it
- Operates out to a **range specified** in the creature description

#### Blindsight Usage
- The creature usually does **not need to make Spot or Listen checks** to notice creatures within range
- Unless noted otherwise, blindsight is **continuous**, and the creature need do nothing to use it
- Some forms of blindsight must be **triggered as a free action**
- If triggered, the creature gains benefits only **during its turn**

#### Blindsight Limitations
- **Never allows** a creature to distinguish color or visual contrast
- A creature **cannot read** with blindsight
- Does **not subject** a creature to gaze attacks (even though darkvision does)
- **Blinding attacks do not penalize** creatures using blindsight
- **Deafening attacks thwart** blindsight if it relies on hearing
- Works **underwater** but **not in a vacuum**
- **Negates displacement and blur** effects

### Blindsense

Other creatures have **blindsense**, a lesser ability that lets the creature notice things it cannot see, but **without the precision of blindsight**.

#### Blindsense Properties
- Usually does **not need Spot or Listen checks** to notice and locate creatures within range
- Requires **line of effect** to that creature
- Any opponent the creature cannot see has **total concealment (50% miss chance)**
- The blindsensing creature still has the **normal miss chance** when attacking foes that have concealment
- **Visibility still affects movement** of a creature with blindsense
- Creature is still **denied Dexterity bonus to AC** against attacks from creatures it cannot see

### Darkvision

**Darkvision** is the extraordinary ability to see with no light source at all, out to a range specified for the creature. Darkvision is **black and white only** (colors cannot be discerned). It does not allow characters to see anything that they could not see otherwise—invisible objects are still invisible, and illusions are still visible as what they seem to be. Likewise, darkvision subjects a creature to **gaze attacks normally**. The presence of light does not spoil darkvision.

### Scent

This extraordinary ability lets a creature **detect approaching enemies**, **sniff out hidden foes**, and **track by sense of smell**. 

#### Detection Capabilities
- **Detect opponents** within **30 feet** by sense of smell
- If opponent is **upwind**, range increases to **60 feet**
- If **downwind**, range drops to **15 feet**
- **Strong scents** (smoke, rotting garbage) can be detected at **twice the ranges** noted above
- **Overpowering scents** (skunk musk, troglodyte stench) can be detected at **triple normal range**

#### Scent Usage
- When creature detects a scent, the **exact location** is not revealed—only its **presence somewhere within range**
- The creature can take a **move action** to note the **direction** of the scent
- Whenever the creature comes within **5 feet** of the source, the creature **pinpoints the source's location**

#### Tracking by Scent
- A creature with the scent ability can **follow tracks by smell**, making a Wisdom (or Survival) check to find or follow a track
- **Typical DC**: 10 (no modifiers if conditions or terrain are not exceptional)
- When tracking by scent, the creature **ignores the effects of surface conditions and poor visibility**

#### Scent in Water
- Creatures tracking by scent **ignore the effects of water**, but not the effects of very strong currents
