# Creature Spellcasting

Sometimes a creature can **cast arcane or divine spells** just as a member of a spellcasting class can (and can activate magic items accordingly). Such creatures are subject to the same spellcasting rules that characters are, except as follows.

## Components and Materials

### Somatic Components
A **spellcasting creature that lacks hands or arms** can provide any somatic component a spell might require by **moving its body**.

### Material Components
- Such a creature also **does need material components** for its spells
- The creature can cast the spell by either:
  - **Touching the required component** (but not if the component is in another creature's possession)
  - **Having the required component on its person**
- Sometimes spellcasting creatures utilize the **Eschew Materials feat** to avoid fussing with noncostly components

## Class Abilities and Levels

### Not Actually Class Members
- A **spellcasting creature is not actually a member of a class** unless its entry says so
- It does **not gain any class abilities**

### Cleric Spells
- A creature with access to **cleric spells** must prepare them in the **normal manner**
- Receives **domain spells** if noted
- Does **not receive domain granted powers** unless it has at least **one level in the cleric class**
