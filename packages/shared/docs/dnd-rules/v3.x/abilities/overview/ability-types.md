# Special Ability Types

This document explains the different categories of special abilities and their fundamental properties.

## Natural Abilities

This category includes abilities a creature has because of its **physical nature**. Natural abilities are those not otherwise designated as extraordinary, supernatural, or spell-like.

## Special Ability Comparison

| **Property** | **Extraordinary (Ex)** | **Spell-Like (Sp)** | **Supernatural (Su)** |
|--------------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Dispel** | No | Yes | No |
| **Spell Resistance** | No | Yes | No |
| **Antimagic Field** | No | Yes | Yes |
| **Attack of Opportunity** | No | Yes | No |

## Extraordinary Abilities (Ex)

**Extraordinary abilities** are **nonmagical**, though they may break the laws of physics. They are not something that just anyone can do or even learn to do without extensive training.

### Key Properties
- **Cannot be disrupted** in combat, as spells can
- **Generally do not provoke** attacks of opportunity
- **Unaffected by magic negation** - effects or areas that negate or disrupt magic have no effect on extraordinary abilities
- **Not subject to dispelling** and function normally in an antimagic field

### Usage
- Using an extraordinary ability is usually **not an action** because most extraordinary abilities automatically happen in a reactive fashion
- Those extraordinary abilities that are actions are **standard actions** unless otherwise noted

### Examples
- Fast healing, darkvision, damage reduction, scent, blindsight

## Spell-Like Abilities (Sp)

Usually, a **spell-like ability** works just like the spell of that name. A few spell-like abilities are unique; these are explained in the text where they are described.

### Components and Activation
- **No verbal, somatic, or material component**, nor does it require a focus or have an XP cost
- The user **activates it mentally**
- **Armor never affects** a spell-like ability's use, even if the ability resembles an arcane spell with a somatic component

### Timing and Usage
- Takes the same amount of time to complete as the spell that it mimics (usually **1 standard action**) unless otherwise stated
- **Cannot be used to counterspell**, nor can they be counterspelled
- In all other ways, functions just like a spell

### Spell-Like Properties
- Using a spell-like ability while threatened **provokes attacks of opportunity**
- Possible to make a **Concentration check** to use defensively and avoid provoking an attack of opportunity
- **Can be disrupted** just as a spell can be
- Subject to **spell resistance** and to being **dispelled by dispel magic**
- **Do not function** in areas where magic is suppressed or negated

### Usage Limits
- Usually has a **limit on how often** it can be used
- A spell-like ability that can be used **at will** has no use limit

### Caster Level and Saves
- **Designated caster level** defines how difficult it is to dispel their spell-like effects and level-dependent variables
- If no caster level is specified, the caster level equals the creature's **Hit Dice**

#### Save DC Formula
```
Save DC = 10 + Spell Level + Creature's Charisma Modifier
```

### Spell Versions
- Monster spell-like abilities are presumed to be the **sorcerer/wizard versions**
- If not a sorcerer/wizard spell, default to: cleric, druid, bard, paladin, and ranger (in that order)

### Special Cases
- Some creatures are actually **sorcerers of a sort** - they cast arcane spells as sorcerers do, using components when required
- An individual creature could have **both spell-like abilities and cast other spells** as a sorcerer

## Supernatural Abilities (Su)

**Supernatural abilities** are **magical** and go away in an antimagic field but are **not subject to spell resistance**, counterspells, or to being dispelled by dispel magic.

### Key Properties
- **Standard action** unless noted otherwise
- **Do not provoke** attacks of opportunity
- **Never require** Concentration checks
- May have a **use limit** or be usable **at will**, just like spell-like abilities

### Caster Level and Saves
- **Effective caster level** equal to the creature's Hit Dice unless otherwise noted

#### Save DC Formula
```
Save DC = 10 + ½ Creature's HD + Ability Modifier (usually Charisma)
```

### Examples
- Auras, gaze attacks, breath weapons, energy resistance
