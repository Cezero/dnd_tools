## Quick Reference

### Major Conditions

| **Condition** | **Effects** | **Duration** |
|---------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Blinded** | -2 AC, lose Dex bonus, ½ speed, -4 Str/Dex skills | Varies |
| **Confused** | Random actions (roll d%) | Varies |
| **Dazed** | No actions, no AC penalty | 1 round |
| **Disabled** | 0 HP, move OR standard action | Until healed |
| **Dying** | Unconscious, losing 1 HP/round | Until stable/dead |
| **Exhausted** | -6 Str/Dex, ½ speed, no charge/run | 1 hour rest |
| **Fatigued** | -2 Str/Dex, no charge/run | 8 hours rest |
| **Frightened** | -2 on attacks/saves/checks, must flee | Varies |
| **Helpless** | -4 AC, lose Dex bonus, coup de grace | Varies |
| **Paralyzed** | Cannot move, helpless | Varies |
| **Prone** | -4 attack, +4 AC vs melee, -4 AC vs ranged | Until stands |
| **Stunned** | Drop items, -2 AC, lose Dex bonus | Varies |

### Ability Score Conditions

| **Condition** | **Effect** | **Recovery** |
|---------------|------------|--------------|
| **Ability Damaged** | Temporary ability loss | 1 point/day |
| **Ability Drained** | Permanent ability loss | Restoration magic only |
| **Ability Score 0** | Special effects by ability | Varies |

### Mental Conditions

| **Condition** | **Primary Effect** | **Save** |
|---------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Charmed** | Regard caster as friend | Will negates |
| **Compelled** | Forced to take actions | Will negates |
| **Confused** | Random behavior | Will negates |
| **Dominated** | Under mental control | Will negates |
| **Fascinated** | Cannot take actions | Will negates |

### Physical Conditions

| **Condition** | **Movement** | **Actions** | **AC** |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Entangled** | ½ speed | -2 attack, -4 Dex | +0 |
| **Grappled** | No movement | Limited | Lose Dex vs others |
| **Pinned** | Helpless | Very limited | -4 |
| **Prone** | Must stand up | -4 attack | +4 vs melee, -4 vs ranged |

### Sensory Conditions

| **Condition** | **Vision** | **Hearing** | **Other Effects** |
|---------------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Blinded** | Cannot see | Normal | 50% miss chance vs you |
| **Dazzled** | Impaired | Normal | -1 attack/Search/Spot |
| **Deafened** | Normal | Cannot hear | -4 initiative, spell failure |

### Energy/Elemental Conditions

| **Condition** | **Source** | **Effect** |
|---------------|------------|------------|
| **Energy Drained** | Negative energy | Negative levels |
| **On Fire** | Fire damage | 1d6 damage/round |
| **Incorporeal** | Special ability | 50% miss chance, move through solid |

### Condition Interactions

| **Condition A** | **Condition B** | **Result** |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Prone** | **Helpless** | Both apply |
| **Blinded** | **Deafened** | Both apply |
| **Fatigued** | **Exhausted** | Exhausted only |
| **Shaken** | **Frightened** | Frightened only |

### Condition Removal

| **Condition Type** | **Common Removals** |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Fear effects** | Remove fear, calm emotions |
| **Mental effects** | Dispel magic, break enchantment |
| **Ability damage** | Lesser restoration, rest |
| **Ability drain** | Restoration, greater restoration |
| **Negative levels** | Restoration, 24 hours |
| **Physical conditions** | Usually time or specific actions |

### Save DCs by Condition Severity

| **Severity** | **Save DC** | **Examples** |
|--------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Minor** | 10-15 | Daze, fascinate |
| **Moderate** | 15-20 | Fear, charm |
| **Severe** | 20-25 | Dominate, paralysis |
| **Extreme** | 25+ | Permanent effects |
