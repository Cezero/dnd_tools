# Condition Summary

Conditions represent temporary or permanent changes to a character's capabilities. **If more than one condition affects a character, apply them all**. If certain effects can't combine, **apply the most severe effect**.

## Core Condition Rules

### Multiple Conditions
- **Apply all applicable conditions** unless they specifically conflict
- **Most severe effect takes precedence** when conditions cannot combine
- **Track each condition separately** for duration and removal purposes

### Condition Interaction Examples
- **Fatigued + Exhausted** = Only exhausted applies (more severe)
- **Blinded + Deafened** = Both apply (different senses)
- **Fear effects** = Higher fear level supersedes lower (shaken → frightened → panicked)

## Condition Severity Guide

### 🟢 **Minor Conditions**
*Manageable penalties that don't prevent most actions*
- **Dazzled** — -1 penalties to sight-based actions
- **Shaken** — -2 morale penalties  
- **Sickened** — -2 penalties to most rolls

### 🟡 **Moderate Conditions**  
*Significant limitations but character remains functional*
- **Fatigued** — Cannot run/charge, -2 Str/Dex
- **Frightened** — Must flee, -2 penalties
- **Entangled** — Movement restricted, attack penalties

### 🟠 **Severe Conditions**
*Major impairment requiring immediate attention*
- **Blinded** — Cannot see, major combat penalties
- **Confused** — Random actions each turn
- **Paralyzed** — Cannot move or act, helpless
- **Exhausted** — -6 Str/Dex, severe limitations

### 🔴 **Critical Conditions**
*Life-threatening or permanently damaging*
- **Dying** — Unconscious, losing hit points each round
- **Energy Drained** — Negative levels, risk of death
- **Dead** — Requires resurrection magic
- **Ability Drained** — Permanent ability score loss

## Common Condition Sources

### Magic
- **Spells** — Wide variety of condition-causing spells
- **Magic items** — Cursed items, activated effects
- **Spell-like abilities** — Monster and character abilities

### Combat
- **Weapon attacks** — Special weapon properties
- **Combat maneuvers** — Trip, grapple, bull rush
- **Critical hits** — Some cause additional conditions
- **Nonlethal damage** — Can cause staggered, unconscious

### Environmental
- **Weather** — Extreme temperatures, wind effects
- **Terrain** — Difficult movement, hazardous areas
- **Planar effects** — Unique conditions on different planes
- **Disease/Poison** — Ongoing condition effects

### Monster Abilities
- **Natural attacks** — Poison, disease, ability drain
- **Special abilities** — Gaze attacks, auras, breath weapons
- **Spell-like abilities** — Magical effects from creatures

## Condition Removal

### Immediate Removal
- **Targeted spells** — *Remove blindness/deafness*, *remove disease*
- **General removal** — *Break enchantment*, *greater restoration*
- **Class abilities** — Paladin healing, cleric channeling

### Time-Based Recovery
- **Natural healing** — Rest removes fatigue, time heals ability damage
- **Temporary conditions** — Many fade after spell duration
- **Stabilization** — Dying characters can become stable

### Prevention
- **Immunity** — Some classes/races immune to specific conditions
- **Resistance** — Bonuses to saves against conditions
- **Protective magic** — Spells that prevent conditions
