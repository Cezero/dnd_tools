# Sensory and Awareness Conditions

Conditions that affect a character's ability to perceive their environment or remain aware of their surroundings.

## Blinded
The character **cannot see** at all.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty to AC**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC** (if any)
- **Half speed movement**
- **-4 penalty** on Search checks and most Strength/Dexterity-based skill checks
- **Automatic failure** of vision-based activities (reading, Spot checks)
- **All opponents have total concealment** (50% miss chance)

**Adaptation**: Characters who remain blinded for a long time **grow accustomed** to these drawbacks and can **overcome some of them**.

### Combat Implications
- **Spellcasting**: Spells requiring line of sight cannot target specific creatures
- **Movement**: Must move carefully to avoid obstacles
- **Attacks**: All attacks subject to miss chance
- **Defense**: Significant AC penalties make character vulnerable

### Common Sources
- **Spells**: *Blindness/deafness*, *color spray*, *glitterdust*
- **Monster abilities**: Umber hulk gaze, bright light from light-sensitive creatures
- **Environmental**: Bright lights, magical darkness
- **Items**: Dust of sneezing and choking, certain cursed items

### Treatment
- **Remove blindness**: *Remove blindness/deafness* spell
- **Dispel magic**: If caused by magical effect
- **Time**: Temporary blindness may fade naturally
- **Cure condition**: Some general condition removal spells

## Dazzled
The creature **cannot see well** due to overstimulation of the eyes.

**Effects**:
- **-1 penalty** on attack rolls
- **-1 penalty** on Search checks
- **-1 penalty** on Spot checks

### Common Sources
- **Bright light**: Sudden exposure to intense illumination
- **Spells**: *Faerie fire*, *flare*, *daylight* (for light-sensitive creatures)
- **Monster abilities**: Some creatures with light-based attacks
- **Environmental**: Snow blindness, desert sun glare

### Duration
- **Temporary**: Usually lasts only while exposed to dazzling effect
- **Adaptation**: Eyes adjust after several rounds out of bright light
- **Protection**: Sunglasses or similar protection can prevent

## Deafened
A deafened character **cannot hear**.

**Effects**:
- **-4 penalty on initiative checks**
- **Automatically fails Listen checks**
- **20% spell failure chance** when casting spells with verbal components

**Adaptation**: Characters who remain deafened for a long time **grow accustomed** to these drawbacks and can **overcome some of them**.

### Combat and Social Implications
- **Communication**: Cannot hear spoken commands or warnings
- **Spellcasting**: Verbal component spells at risk of failure
- **Stealth**: Cannot hear approaching enemies
- **Initiative**: Slower reaction to combat start

### Common Sources
- **Spells**: *Blindness/deafness*, *sound burst*, *shout*
- **Monster abilities**: Banshee wail, certain sonic attacks
- **Environmental**: Extremely loud noises, explosions
- **Physical**: Ear damage from trauma

### Treatment
- **Remove deafness**: *Remove blindness/deafness* spell
- **Healing**: Physical damage may require magical healing
- **Time**: Temporary deafness from loud noise may fade

## Unconscious
The character is **knocked out and helpless**.

**Effects**:
- **Helpless condition**
- **Cannot take actions**
- **Unaware of surroundings**

### Unconscious States
- **Sleep**: Natural or magical sleep
- **Knockout**: Nonlethal damage exceeding current hit points
- **Magic**: Spells like *sleep* or *deep slumber*
- **Ability damage**: Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma reduced to 0

### Vulnerability
- **Coup de grace**: Can be executed against unconscious characters
- **Automatic hits**: Attackers automatically hit in melee
- **No defense**: Cannot dodge or defend against attacks
- **Robbing/binding**: Completely vulnerable to non-combat actions

### Awakening
- **Damage**: Any damage typically awakens sleeping characters
- **Loud noise**: May wake sleepers (Listen check while asleep)
- **Shaking**: Physical contact can wake normal sleepers
- **Time**: Natural sleep ends after normal duration
- **Magic**: *Dispel magic* can end magical sleep

### Common Sources
- **Sleep spells**: *Sleep*, *deep slumber*, *symbol of sleep*
- **Nonlethal damage**: Sap attacks, subdual damage overflow
- **Ability damage**: Mental ability scores reduced to 0
- **Exhaustion**: Extreme fatigue in some cases
- **Drugs/poison**: Some substances cause unconsciousness

## Invisible
**Visually undetectable**. An invisible creature gains a **+2 bonus on attack rolls** against sighted opponents, and **ignores opponents' Dexterity bonuses to AC** (if any).

### Combat Benefits
- **Attack bonus**: +2 against creatures that rely on sight
- **AC benefit**: Opponents lose Dex bonus to AC
- **Stealth advantage**: Automatic Hide bonus in most situations
- **Spell targeting**: Cannot be targeted by spells requiring sight

### Detection Methods
- **Listen checks**: Can still hear invisible creatures
- **Scent**: Creatures with scent can detect invisible foes
- **Tremorsense**: Detects movement through ground vibrations
- **True seeing**: Magical detection reveals invisible creatures

### Common Sources
- **Spells**: *Invisibility*, *greater invisibility*, *improved invisibility*
- **Magic items**: Rings of invisibility, cloaks of elvenkind
- **Monster abilities**: Natural invisibility, chameleon power
- **Potions**: Potions of invisibility

### Breaking Invisibility
- **Attacking**: Most offensive actions break invisibility
- **Spellcasting**: Most spells break invisibility (some exceptions)
- **Area damage**: Taking damage may reveal location
- **Duration**: Spell duration expires naturally
