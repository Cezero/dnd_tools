# Physical Conditions

Conditions that affect a character's physical state, health, or bodily functions.

## Death and Near-Death States

### Dead
The character's **life has ended** through various means.

**Death Conditions**:
- **Hit points reduced to -10**
- **Constitution drops to 0**
- **Killed outright** by spell or effect

**Death Effects**:
- **Soul leaves body**
- **Cannot benefit** from normal or magical healing
- **Can be restored** to life via magic
- **Body decays normally** unless magically preserved
- **Restoration magic** restores body to full health or death condition

### Disabled
A character with **0 hit points** or who has **negative hit points but is stable and conscious**.

**Action Limitations**:
- **Single action per round**: Move action OR standard action (not both)
- **No full-round actions**
- **Half speed movement**
- **Standard action risk**: Deals 1 damage after completion (may cause dying)

**Recovery Rules**:
- **With help**: Recovers hit points naturally
- **Without help**: 10% chance per day to start natural recovery, otherwise loses 1 hp
- **Once recovering**: No longer loses hit points (even if negative)

### Dying
A dying character is **unconscious and near death** (-1 to -9 hit points).

**Dying Mechanics**:
- **No actions** and unconscious
- **Stability check**: Roll d% each round for 10% chance to stabilize
- **Continued dying**: Loses 1 hit point if not stable
- **Death**: Dies when reaching -10 hit points

### Stable
A character with **negative hit points** who has stopped losing hit points.

**Effects**:
- **Unconscious but not dying**
- **No longer loses hit points** each round
- **Natural healing** does not restore hit points
- **Magical healing** works normally

## Incapacitation Conditions

### Helpless
The character is **completely at an opponent's mercy**.

**Causes**: Paralyzed, held, bound, sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise completely at an opponent's mercy.

**AC**: **5 + size modifier** (loses Dex bonus, treated as having Dex 0)

**Vulnerabilities**: 
- **Coup de grace** attacks possible
- **Sneak attacks** apply
- **Cannot take actions**

### Paralyzed
The character is **completely immobilized** but conscious.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot move, speak, or take any actions**
- **Effective Strength and Dexterity of 0**
- **Helpless condition** applies
- **Can take mental actions** if able

**Common Sources**:
- **Spells**: *Hold person*, *paralysis*, *flesh to stone*
- **Poison**: Carrion crawler tentacles, some spider venoms
- **Monster abilities**: Ghoul touch, umber hulk paralysis
- **Magic items**: Wands of paralysis, certain cursed items

### Stunned
The character is **frozen in place**.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot take actions**
- **-2 penalty to AC**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC**

**Duration**: Usually brief (1 round typical)

## Health and Fatigue Conditions

### Exhausted
The character is **extremely fatigued**.

**Effects**:
- **-6 penalty** to Strength and Dexterity
- **Cannot run or charge**

**Recovery**: One hour of rest reduces to fatigued.

### Fatigued
The character is **tired**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** to Strength and Dexterity
- **Cannot run or charge**

**Recovery**: Eight hours of rest removes condition.
**Progression**: Becomes exhausted if subject to further fatigue.

### Nauseated
The character is **experiencing stomach distress**.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot attack, cast spells, concentrate on spells, or do anything requiring attention**
- **Can take single move action** per turn

**Common Sources**:
- **Spells**: *Stinking cloud*, *nauseating breath*
- **Poison**: Certain ingested toxins
- **Disease**: Some diseases cause nausea
- **Environmental**: Extreme odors, motion sickness

### Sickened
The character is **impaired by nausea**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks

**Difference from Nauseated**: Can still take full actions, just at penalty

## Transformation Conditions

### Petrified
The character has been **turned to stone**.

**Effects**:
- **Considered unconscious**
- **Damage resistance 8**
- **Immune to most conditions**
- **Weight increases by factor of 10**

**Common Sources**:
- **Monster abilities**: Medusa gaze, basilisk gaze, cockatrice bite
- **Spells**: *Flesh to stone*, *transmute flesh to stone*
- **Cursed items**: Some artifacts or cursed equipment
- **Environmental**: Certain magical locations

**Recovery**:
- **Stone to flesh**: Primary reversal spell
- **Break enchantment**: May work for some causes
- **Wish/miracle**: Can reverse petrification
- **Physical damage**: If statue is destroyed, character dies

## Special Physical States

### Prone
The character is **lying on the ground**.

**Effects**:
- **-4 penalty** on melee attack rolls
- **Cannot use ranged weapons** except crossbow
- **+4 AC bonus** against ranged attacks
- **-4 AC penalty** against melee attacks

**Recovery**: Standing up provokes attacks of opportunity.

**Tactical Considerations**:
- **Trip attacks**: Common way to force prone
- **Spell effects**: Some spells knock targets prone
- **Environmental**: Slippery surfaces, earthquakes
- **Combat maneuvers**: Bull rush, overrun can cause prone

### Grappling
The character is **wrestling** with one or more attackers.

**Effects**:
- **Limited actions** available
- **Cannot move** normally
- **Can attempt** to escape, attack, or cast certain spells

**Grapple Actions**:
- **Attack**: Limited to natural weapons, light weapons, or unarmed
- **Cast spell**: Only spells without somatic components
- **Escape**: Opposed grapple check or Escape Artist
- **Damage**: Deal damage to grappling opponent

### Entangled
The character is **ensnared** but not completely immobilized.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** on attack rolls
- **-4 penalty** to effective Dexterity
- **Cannot move** from current space
- **Can take other actions** normally

**Common Sources**:
- **Spells**: *Entangle*, *web*, *black tentacles*
- **Monster abilities**: Spider webs, plant creature attacks
- **Environmental**: Thick vegetation, quicksand
- **Items**: Nets, bolas, tanglefoot bags

## Additional Physical States

### Incorporeal
**Having no physical body**. Incorporeal creatures are **immune to all nonmagical attack forms**. They can be harmed only by:
- **Other incorporeal creatures**
- **+1 or better magic weapons**
- **Spells, spell-like effects, or supernatural effects**

**Movement**: Can move through solid objects and other creatures without penalty.

### Pinned
**Held immobile** (but not helpless) in a grapple.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot move** from current position
- **Very limited actions** available
- **-4 penalty to AC** against attacks from non-grappling opponents
- **Cannot cast spells** with somatic components

**Escape**: Requires successful grapple check or Escape Artist check.

### Staggered
A character whose **nonlethal damage exactly equals current hit points** is staggered.

**Effects**:
- **Single action per round**: Move action OR standard action (not both)
- **No full-round actions** allowed
- **Recovery**: No longer staggered when current HP exceeds nonlethal damage
- **Unconsciousness**: Becomes unconscious if nonlethal damage exceeds HP

## Environmental Physical Conditions

### Blown Away
Depending on size, a creature can be **blown away by high-velocity winds**.

**Ground Creatures**:
- **Knocked down** and rolls 1d4 × 10 feet
- **Takes 1d4 nonlethal damage** per 10 feet

**Flying Creatures**:
- **Blown back** 2d6 × 10 feet  
- **Takes 2d6 nonlethal damage** from battering

### Checked
**Prevented from forward motion** by applied force (such as wind).

**Effects**:
- **Ground creatures**: Simply stop moving
- **Flying creatures**: Move back specified distance
- **No damage**: Unlike blown away condition

### Knocked Down
Creatures can be **knocked down by high-velocity winds** depending on their size.

**Effects**:
- **Ground creatures**: Knocked prone by wind force
- **Flying creatures**: Blown back 1d6 × 10 feet instead
- **Size matters**: Larger creatures more resistant to effects

## Special Combat States

### Flat-Footed
A character who **has not yet acted during combat** is flat-footed, not yet reacting normally to the situation.

**Effects**:
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC** (if any)
- **Cannot make attacks of opportunity**
- **Duration**: Until character acts in combat

**Common Causes**:
- **Initiative order**: Before first action in combat
- **Surprise rounds**: Surprised characters remain flat-footed
- **Special abilities**: Some abilities cause flat-footed condition

### Turned
**Affected by a turn undead attempt**. Turned undead **flee for 10 rounds** (1 minute) by the best and fastest means available to them.

**Effects**:
- **Must flee**: Use best available movement
- **Cannot attack**: While fleeing from turner
- **If cornered**: Cower instead of flee
- **Duration**: 10 rounds unless destroyed or dispelled

**Affects**: Only undead creatures subject to turning attempts.
