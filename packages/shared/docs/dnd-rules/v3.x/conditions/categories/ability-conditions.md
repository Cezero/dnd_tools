# Ability Score Conditions

Conditions that affect a character's ability scores, either temporarily or permanently.

## Ability Damaged
The character has **temporarily lost** 1 or more ability score points.

**Recovery**: Lost points return at a rate of **1 per day** unless noted otherwise by the condition dealing the damage.

**Effects at 0 Ability Score**:
- **Strength 0**: Falls to ground and is helpless
- **Dexterity 0**: Paralyzed  
- **Constitution 0**: Dead
- **Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma 0**: Unconscious

**Note**: Ability damage is different from penalties to ability scores, which go away when the conditions causing them go away.

### Common Sources
- **Poison**: Many poisons cause temporary ability damage
- **Disease**: Various diseases drain abilities over time
- **Spells**: *Ray of enfeeblement*, *touch of fatigue*
- **Monster attacks**: Shadows (Strength), stirges (Constitution)

### Treatment
- **Natural healing**: 1 point per day
- **Magical healing**: *Lesser restoration* removes all ability damage
- **Rest**: Complete bed rest doubles natural healing rate

---

## Ability Drained
The character has **permanently lost** 1 or more ability score points. 

**Recovery**: Character can regain drained points **only through magical means**.

**Effects**: Same as ability damage for 0 ability scores.

### Common Sources
- **Undead attacks**: Wraiths, specters, vampires
- **Necromantic spells**: *Enervation*, *energy drain*
- **Cursed items**: Some artifacts or cursed equipment
- **Planar effects**: Negative energy plane exposure

### Treatment
- **Restoration spell**: Removes 1d4 points of ability drain
- **Greater restoration**: Removes all ability drain
- **Wish/miracle**: Can restore drained abilities
- **Time**: Negative levels from energy drain may be removed naturally after 24 hours

---

## Energy Drained
The character gains **one or more negative levels**.

**Effects per Negative Level**:
- **-1 penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks
- **Loss of 5 hit points**
- **-1 to effective level** (for spells and abilities)
- **Spellcasters**: Lose one spell slot from highest level castable

**Death**: Character dies if negative levels ≥ Hit Dice.

### Negative Level Mechanics
- **Stacking**: Multiple negative levels stack
- **Effective level**: Reduces class level for all purposes except HD
- **Spell slots**: Lost from highest available level
- **Turn resistance**: Undead lose turn resistance if reduced below original HD

### Common Sources
- **Undead attacks**: Wraiths (1d4 negative levels), vampires (2 negative levels)
- **Necromantic spells**: *Enervation* (1d4 negative levels)
- **Magic items**: Some cursed weapons or armor
- **Planar travel**: Negative energy plane without protection

### Recovery
- **24-hour rule**: After 24 hours, make Fortitude save (DC = attack DC) to remove one negative level
- **Restoration**: Immediately removes one negative level
- **Greater restoration**: Removes all negative levels
- **Natural immunity**: Some creatures immune to negative levels

### Temporary vs Permanent
- **Temporary**: Most negative levels fade after 24 hours if save succeeds
- **Permanent**: Failed saves convert to permanent level loss
- **Level loss**: Permanent reduction in character level and all benefits
