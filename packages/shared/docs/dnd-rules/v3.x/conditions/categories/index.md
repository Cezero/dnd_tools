# Condition Categories

Conditions organized by type and effect for easier reference and understanding.

## Category Organization

### [Ability Score Conditions](ability-conditions.md)
Conditions affecting character ability scores:
- **Ability Damaged** — Temporary ability score loss
- **Ability Drained** — Permanent ability score loss  
- **Energy Drained** — Negative levels and level loss

### [Sensory Conditions](sensory-conditions.md)
Conditions affecting perception and awareness:
- **Blinded** — Cannot see, major combat penalties
- **Deafened** — Cannot hear, initiative and spell penalties
- **Dazzled** — Impaired vision, minor penalties
- **Unconscious** — Completely unaware and helpless

### [Mental Conditions](mental-conditions.md)
Conditions affecting mental state and decision-making:
- **Confused** — Random actions each turn
- **Fear effects** — Shaken, frightened, panicked, cowering
- **Dazed** — Cannot act for brief period
- **Fascinated** — Enthralled and unable to act

### [Physical Conditions](physical-conditions.md)
Conditions affecting physical state and capabilities:
- **Death states** — Dead, dying, disabled, stable
- **Incapacitation** — Helpless, paralyzed, stunned
- **Fatigue** — Exhausted, fatigued, nauseated, sickened
- **Position** — Prone, grappled, entangled

## Condition Severity Guide

### Minor Conditions
- **Dazzled** — Small penalties, easy to overcome
- **Shaken** — Minor fear effect
- **Sickened** — Manageable penalties

### Moderate Conditions  
- **Fatigued** — Reduced capabilities but still functional
- **Frightened** — Must flee but can still act
- **Entangled** — Movement restricted but actions available

### Severe Conditions
- **Blinded** — Major combat disadvantage
- **Confused** — Unpredictable and dangerous
- **Paralyzed** — Cannot act, helpless
- **Exhausted** — Severely impaired

### Critical Conditions
- **Dying** — Near death, requires immediate attention
- **Energy Drained** — Risk of death, permanent consequences
- **Dead** — Requires resurrection magic

## Common Condition Interactions

### Stacking Rules
- **Same type**: Usually don't stack (use worst effect)
- **Different types**: Generally stack together
- **Fear progression**: Can escalate through levels
- **Fatigue progression**: Fatigued → Exhausted

### Condition Removal Priority
1. **Life-threatening**: Dying, death, energy drain
2. **Incapacitating**: Paralysis, unconsciousness  
3. **Severely limiting**: Blindness, confusion
4. **Moderately limiting**: Fear, fatigue
5. **Minor**: Dazzled, sickened

### Treatment Strategies
- **Prevention**: Immunities, resistances, protective spells
- **Immediate relief**: Quick removal spells and abilities
- **Long-term care**: Rest, natural healing, comprehensive magic
- **Group support**: Teamwork and shared resources
