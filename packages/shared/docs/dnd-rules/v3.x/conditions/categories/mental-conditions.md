# Mental and Emotional Conditions

Conditions that affect a character's mental state, decision-making ability, or emotional control.

## Confused
A confused character's **actions are determined randomly**.

### Confusion Behavior Table
Roll d% at the beginning of the character's turn:

| **d%** | **Behavior** |
|--------|--------------|
| **01-10** | Attack caster with melee/ranged weapons (or close with caster) |
| **11-20** | Act normally |
| **21-50** | Do nothing but babble incoherently |
| **51-70** | Flee from caster at top speed |
| **71-100** | Attack nearest creature (familiar counts as part of self) |

### Special Rules
- **Unable to carry out action**: Does nothing but babble incoherently
- **No special vulnerability**: Attackers gain no advantage against confused character
- **Retaliation**: Automatically attacks attackers on next turn if still confused
- **No opportunity attacks**: Doesn't make attacks of opportunity except against devoted targets

### Common Sources
- **Spells**: *Confusion*, *chaos*, *insanity*
- **Monster abilities**: Umber hulk confusion gaze, chaos beast touch
- **Items**: Rod of wonder (random effect), some cursed items
- **Environmental**: Chaotic planar effects, mind-affecting terrain

### Treatment
- **Dispel magic**: If magical in origin
- **Remove curse**: For cursed item effects
- **Time**: Most confusion effects are temporary
- **Calm emotions**: May suppress emotional confusion

## Cowering
The character is **frozen in fear** and can take no actions.

**Effects**:
- **No actions allowed**
- **-2 penalty to AC**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC** (if any)

### Fear Progression
Cowering often represents the **extreme end** of fear effects:
1. **Shaken** (-2 morale penalty)
2. **Frightened** (must flee)
3. **Panicked** (flee randomly)
4. **Cowering** (completely frozen)

### Common Sources
- **Intimidation**: Extreme Intimidate check results
- **Fear spells**: *Cause fear*, *scare*, overwhelming fear effects
- **Monster abilities**: Dragon frightful presence, banshee wail
- **Overwhelming odds**: Facing vastly superior foes

### Recovery
- **Remove fear**: Magical fear removal
- **Calm emotions**: Suppresses fear effects
- **Rally**: Allies can attempt to snap character out of fear
- **Time**: Fear effects typically temporary

## Dazed
The creature is **unable to act normally**.

**Effects**:
- **No actions allowed**
- **No penalty to AC**

**Duration**: Typically lasts 1 round.

### Limited Nature
- **Brief effect**: Usually very short duration
- **Maintains defenses**: Unlike stunned, no AC penalty
- **Common result**: Many spells and abilities cause brief daze

### Common Sources
- **Stunning effects**: Lesser stunning that doesn't cause full stun
- **Mental overload**: Information overload, psychic shock
- **Spells**: *Daze*, *color spray*, some enchantment effects
- **Critical hits**: Some critical hit effects cause brief daze

## Fear Effects (Shaken, Frightened, Panicked)

### Shaken
The character is **slightly afraid**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks

**Duration**: Usually several rounds to minutes
**Severity**: Mildest fear effect

### Frightened  
The character is **shaken** and must flee.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks
- **Must flee** from source of fear
- **Can fight** if unable to flee

**Behavior**: Will use ranged attacks if possible while fleeing

### Panicked
The character is **overcome with fear**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on saving throws
- **Must flee** in random direction
- **Cannot take any other actions**
- **If cornered**: Cowers instead

**Severity**: Most severe standard fear effect before cowering

### Fear Immunity and Resistance
- **Paladins**: Immune to fear at higher levels
- **Barbarians**: Bonus on saves against fear while raging
- **Bards**: *Inspire courage* provides fear resistance
- **Magic items**: Various items provide fear immunity

### Common Fear Sources
- **Spells**: *Cause fear*, *scare*, *fear*, *symbol of fear*
- **Monster abilities**: Dragon frightful presence, ghosts
- **Intimidation**: Extreme Intimidate skill use
- **Environmental**: Certain haunted or cursed locations

---

## Fascinated
The character is **enthralled** by something and unable to act normally.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot take actions** except to pay attention to fascinating effect
- **-4 penalty** on skill checks made as reactions (Listen, Spot)
- **Broken by**: Obvious threat, loud noise, being shaken

### Fascination Mechanics
- **Voluntary failure**: Character doesn't want to resist
- **External threats**: Snap character out of fascination
- **Limited duration**: Most fascination effects are temporary
- **Multiple targets**: Can affect multiple creatures simultaneously

### Common Sources
- **Bard abilities**: *Fascinate* bardic music
- **Spells**: *Hypnotism*, *enthrall*, certain illusions
- **Monster abilities**: Harpy song, nymph beauty
- **Magic items**: Some enchanted instruments or artwork
