# Complete Condition Reference

A comprehensive alphabetical reference of all conditions that can affect characters and creatures.

## Quick Reference Table

| **Condition** | **Primary Effect** | **Duration** | **Common Source** |
|---------------|-------------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Ability Damaged** | Temporary ability loss | Until healed | Poison, disease, spells |
| **Ability Drained** | Permanent ability loss | Until restored | Undead attacks, spells |
| **Blinded** | Cannot see | Varies | Spells, bright lights |
| **Confused** | Random actions | Temporary | Mind effects |
| **Dead** | No longer alive | Permanent | HP loss, death effects |
| **Disabled** | Limited actions | Until healed | 0 hit points |
| **Dying** | Unconscious, losing HP | Until stable/dead | Negative hit points |
| **Exhausted** | -6 Str/Dex penalty | Until rested | Fatigue progression |
| **Fatigued** | -2 Str/Dex penalty | Until rested | Exertion, spells |
| **Helpless** | Defenseless | Varies | Paralysis, unconscious |
| **Paralyzed** | Cannot move/act | Varies | Spells, poison |
| **Unconscious** | Unaware, helpless | Varies | Damage, spells |

## Detailed Condition Descriptions

### Ability Damaged
The character has **temporarily lost** 1 or more ability score points.

**Recovery**: Lost points return at a rate of **1 per day** unless noted otherwise by the condition dealing the damage.

**Effects at 0 Ability Score**:
- **Strength 0**: Falls to ground and is helpless
- **Dexterity 0**: Paralyzed  
- **Constitution 0**: Dead
- **Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma 0**: Unconscious

**Note**: Ability damage is different from penalties to ability scores, which go away when the conditions causing them go away.

### Ability Drained
The character has **permanently lost** 1 or more ability score points. 

**Recovery**: Character can regain drained points **only through magical means**.

**Effects**: Same as ability damage for 0 ability scores.

### Blinded
The character **cannot see**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty to Armor Class**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC** (if any)
- **Moves at half speed**
- **-4 penalty** on Search checks and most Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks
- **Automatic failure** of vision-based activities (reading, Spot checks)
- **All opponents have total concealment** (50% miss chance)

**Adaptation**: Characters who remain blinded for a long time grow accustomed to these drawbacks and can overcome some of them.

### Blown Away
Depending on its size, a creature can be **blown away by winds** of high velocity.

**Ground Creatures**: Knocked down and rolls **1d4 × 10 feet**, taking **1d4 points of nonlethal damage per 10 feet**.

**Flying Creatures**: Blown back **2d6 × 10 feet** and takes **2d6 points of nonlethal damage** due to battering and buffering.

### Checked
**Prevented from achieving forward motion** by an applied force, such as wind.

**Ground Creatures**: Merely stop.
**Flying Creatures**: Move back a distance specified in the description of the effect.

### Confused
A confused character's **actions are determined by rolling d%** at the beginning of their turn:

| **d%** | **Action** |
|--------|------------|
| **01-10** | Attack caster with melee or ranged weapons |
| **11-20** | Act normally |
| **21-50** | Do nothing but babble incoherently |
| **51-70** | Flee away from caster at top possible speed |
| **71-100** | Attack nearest creature |

**Special Rules**:
- Character who can't carry out indicated action does nothing but babble
- Attackers gain no special advantage when attacking confused character
- Confused character who is attacked automatically attacks its attackers on next turn
- Does not make attacks of opportunity except against devoted targets

### Cowering
The character is **frozen in fear** and can take no actions.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty to Armor Class**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus** (if any)

### Dazed
The creature is **unable to act normally**.

**Effects**:
- **Can take no actions**
- **No penalty to AC**

**Duration**: Typically lasts 1 round.

### Dazzled
The creature is **unable to see well** because of overstimulation of the eyes.

**Effects**:
- **-1 penalty** on attack rolls, Search checks, and Spot checks

### Dead
The character's **hit points are reduced to -10**, Constitution drops to 0, or killed outright by a spell or effect.

**Effects**:
- **Soul leaves body**
- **Cannot benefit** from normal or magical healing
- **Can be restored to life** via magic
- **Body decays normally** unless magically preserved

### Deafened
A deafened character **cannot hear**.

**Effects**:
- **-4 penalty on initiative checks**
- **Automatically fails Listen checks**
- **20% chance of spell failure** when casting spells with verbal components

**Adaptation**: Characters who remain deafened for a long time grow accustomed to these drawbacks and can overcome some of them.

### Disabled
A character with **0 hit points**, or one who has negative hit points but has become stable and conscious.

**Actions**: May take a **single move action OR standard action** each round (but not both, nor full-round actions).

**Movement**: Moves at **half speed**.

**Risk**: Taking standard actions deals **1 point of damage** after completion. Unless the action increased hit points, character is now dying.

**Recovery**:
- **With help**: Recovers hit points naturally
- **Without help**: 10% chance per day to start recovering, otherwise loses 1 hp

### Dying
A dying character is **unconscious and near death** (−1 to −9 current hit points).

**Effects**:
- **Can take no actions** and is unconscious
- **Stability check**: 10% chance each round to become stable
- **Continued deterioration**: Loses 1 hit point if not stable
- **Death**: Dies at -10 hit points

### Energy Drained
The character gains **one or more negative levels**.

**Effects per Negative Level**:
- **-1 penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks
- **Loss of 5 hit points**
- **-1 to effective level** (for spells and abilities)
- **Spellcasters**: Lose one spell slot from highest level castable

**Death**: Character dies if negative levels ≥ Hit Dice.

### Entangled
The character is **ensnared** but not completely immobilized.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** on attack rolls
- **-4 penalty** to effective Dexterity
- **Cannot move** from current space
- **Can take other actions** normally

### Exhausted
The character is **extremely fatigued**.

**Effects**:
- **-6 penalty** to Strength and Dexterity
- **Cannot run or charge**

**Recovery**: One hour of rest reduces to fatigued.

### Fatigued
The character is **tired**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** to Strength and Dexterity
- **Cannot run or charge**

**Recovery**: Eight hours of rest removes condition.

**Progression**: Becomes exhausted if subject to further fatigue.

### Frightened
The character is **shaken** and must flee.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks
- **Must flee** from source of fear
- **Can fight** if unable to flee

### Grappling
The character is **wrestling** with one or more attackers.

**Effects**:
- **Limited actions** available
- **Cannot move** normally
- **Can attempt** to escape, attack, or cast certain spells

### Helpless
The character is **completely at an opponent's mercy**.

**Causes**: Paralyzed, held, bound, sleeping, unconscious, or otherwise completely at an opponent's mercy.

**AC**: **5 + size modifier** (loses Dex bonus, treated as having Dex 0)

**Vulnerabilities**: 
- **Coup de grace** attacks possible
- **Sneak attacks** apply
- **Cannot take actions**

### Nauseated
The character is **experiencing stomach distress**.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot attack, cast spells, concentrate on spells, or do anything requiring attention**
- **Can take single move action** per turn

### Panicked
The character is **overcome with fear**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on saving throws
- **Must flee** in random direction
- **Cannot take any other actions**
- **If cornered**: Cowers instead

### Paralyzed
The character is **completely immobilized** but conscious.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot move, speak, or take any actions**
- **Effective Strength and Dexterity of 0**
- **Helpless condition** applies
- **Can take mental actions** if able

### Petrified
The character has been **turned to stone**.

**Effects**:
- **Considered unconscious**
- **Damage resistance 8**
- **Immune to most conditions**
- **Weight increases by factor of 10**

### Prone
The character is **lying on the ground**.

**Effects**:
- **-4 penalty** on melee attack rolls
- **Cannot use ranged weapons** except crossbow
- **+4 AC bonus** against ranged attacks
- **-4 AC penalty** against melee attacks

**Recovery**: Standing up provokes attacks of opportunity.

### Shaken
The character is **slightly afraid**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 morale penalty** on attack rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks

### Sickened
The character is **impaired by nausea**.

**Effects**:
- **-2 penalty** on attack rolls, weapon damage rolls, saving throws, skill checks, ability checks

### Stable
A character with **negative hit points** who has stopped losing hit points.

**Effects**:
- **Unconscious but not dying**
- **No longer loses hit points** each round
- **Natural healing** does not restore hit points
- **Magical healing** works normally

### Stunned
The character is **frozen in place**.

**Effects**:
- **Cannot take actions**
- **-2 penalty to AC**
- **Loses Dexterity bonus to AC**

### Turned
An **undead creature** has been affected by a turn undead attempt.

**Effects**:
- **Must move away** from turner
- **Cannot attack** the turner
- **Can defend normally** against others

### Unconscious
The character is **knocked out and helpless**.

**Effects**:
- **Helpless condition**
- **Cannot take actions**
- **Unaware of surroundings**
